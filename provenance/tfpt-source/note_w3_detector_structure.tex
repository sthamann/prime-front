\begin{abstract}
The finite window quadratic form of the TFPT prime-front program ---
identified in the companion note \cite{HamW1} as a hat-function Galerkin
discretization of Suzuki's localized Weil operator --- is shown to satisfy
an exact structure theorem: the deployed odd-Toeplitz form is the
Cantoni--Butler odd-sector compression of the full Toeplitz matrix of the
Weil lag vector, its generalized spectrum is sandwiched between the
periodized-symbol minimum and the discrete-sine-mode reads (the naive
``DST-diagonal'' reading is \emph{false}; the measured parity defect is
$O(1)$), and every mode read is, unconditionally by the Weil explicit
formula, a $\sinc^2$-damped \emph{alias comb} over the nontrivial zeros
plus an explicit nonnegative pole layer, in which a hypothetical off-line
zero quadruple at $\tfrac12+\delta+i\gamma$ enters with the amplification
$\cosh(\delta u)\le\cosh(2\alpha\delta)$ per lag $u$ --- the form is an
off-line zero detector.  The detector is validated in two exact
laboratories: on the Ihara side (proven Ramanujan and non-Ramanujan
graphs) the machinery is positive semidefinite on every proven-true
control and breaks on the proven violators with the detection law
$K^*\!\cdot s\approx 2$--$3$, and at the Epstein zeta function of
$x^2+5y^2$ a measured argument-principle census of $26$ off-line zeros
($12$ in $[0.6,1.4]\times[2,100]$) predicts the measured form break
quantitatively ($\lambda_{\min}$ ratio $0.803$ inside the preregistered
factor-$2$ gate).  On the deployed $67$-window family the measured
positivity becomes a certificate with a quantified threshold map
$s_{\min}(w,\gamma)$ (median $2\alpha\,s_{\min}=1.4$--$2.6$ on the
resolved band $\gamma\le\pi/D\le 868$), and the $C=1$ contraction
constant of the model/real ladder is identified as a quadrature
statement: the zero-free density integral of the lock profile predicts
the measured constant with median ratio $1.005$.  The Fej\'er density
identity $s_{\mathrm{tot}}=2\pi\,(F_a\ast dN)$, verified to
$2.9\times10^{-6}$ and carrying an unconditional smoothed-density lower
bound with $c_0=0.306$ (Riemann--von Mangoldt counting plus Trudgian's
$|S(t)|$ bound, ten finite machine-checked certificates), is the lemma
connecting all three results.  Uniform positivity over the full window
family is the conjecture --- by Weil's criterion equivalent to the
Riemann Hypothesis \cite{Weil52,Yoshida92,BL99} --- and nothing in this
note claims it.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup}\label{sec:setup}

This note is the successor of \cite{HamW1}, which identified the TFPT
window quadratic form as a Galerkin discretization of Suzuki's localized
Weil operator $B_a$ \cite{Suz26} at the level of measures.  We keep its
conventions and recall only what is needed.  A \emph{frame-A window} is a
triple $(\alpha, M=2h, D=2\alpha/M)$; the deployed family has $70$
windows of which $67$ are \emph{complete} ($e^{2\alpha}$ inside the
frozen atom table; the three truncated windows $h=1219,1292,1445$ are
excluded throughout).  The lag vector is $c_d=c_{\mathrm{ar}}(d)+
c_{\mathrm{at}}(d)$, $d=0,\dots,M-1$: $c_{\mathrm{ar}}$ is the
archimedean layer read by a width-$D$ tent at lag $dD$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cat}
c_{\mathrm{at}}(d)=-\sum_{n\le e^{2\alpha}}\frac{\Lam(n)}{\sqrt n}\,
g_d(\log n),\qquad
g_d(u)=\tau_D(u-dD)+\tau_D(u+dD),
\end{equation}
with $\tau_D(u)=(1-|u|/D)_+$.  The window matrix is the odd (parity)
compression
\begin{equation}\label{eq:window}
(A_h)_{jk}=c_{|j-k|}-c_{(M-1)-j-k},\qquad j,k=0,\dots,h-1,
\end{equation}
and $G_h$ is the analogous compression of the hat mass vector
$(g_0,g_1)=(2D/3,\,D/6)$.  All spectral statements below are generalized
eigenvalue statements for the pencil $(A_h,G_h)$; mode reads are
Rayleigh reads $R_A(k)=u_k^{\mathsf T}A_hu_k$ of the discrete sine
(DST) vectors $u_k[j]=\sin\bigl(\theta_k(j-h+\tfrac12)\bigr)$,
$\theta_k=2\pi k/M$.  Every form and every mode read in this note is
assembled from \emph{primes and the digamma function only}; Riemann
zeros enter only the verification of identities (through a
Turing-certified cache of the first $2000$ zeros, live-checked to
$2.7\times10^{-12}$) and the Epstein census is a measured output of the
argument principle, never an input table.

Three results are established: \textbf{(A)} the structure theorem of
Sec.~\ref{sec:structure} --- window positivity is \emph{exactly} alias
positivity of the zero symbol, and off-line zeros enter with a
quantified $\cosh$ gain; \textbf{(B)} a two-laboratory validation and a
threshold map (Secs.~\ref{sec:labs}--\ref{sec:map}), typing the
measured $67$-window positivity as a certificate with quantified
reach; \textbf{(C)} a quadrature identification of the $C=1$
contraction constant (Sec.~\ref{sec:c1}), with the Fej\'er density
identity as the connecting lemma (Sec.~\ref{sec:fejer}).
Section~\ref{sec:scope} gives the honest scope, the circularity
ledger, and the implication map.

\subsection{Toeplitz $\Leftrightarrow$ symbol: the sandwich, not the
naive diagonalization}

With $f\in\mathbb R^M$ the odd extension of $x\in\mathbb R^h$
($f_j=x_j$, $f_{M-1-j}=-x_j$) one has exactly
$x^{\mathsf T}A_hx=\tfrac12 f^{\mathsf T}T_M(c)f$ with
$T_M(c)_{ij}=c_{|i-j|}$: every lag $0\le d\le M-1$ is present.  $T_M$
is persymmetric, and the reflection sectors are invariant:
$\spec T_M=\spec A_{\mathrm{even}}\cup\spec A_{\mathrm{odd}}$
(Cantoni--Butler \cite{CB76}), verified eigenvalue-by-eigenvalue on all
three windows to relative deviation $1.5\times10^{-15}$.

\emph{The honest correction.}  It is tempting to read the compressed
form as ``DST-diagonal with symbol eigenvalues''.  That statement is
\textbf{false} for the deployed lag vector: only the
reflection-symmetrized part $E_h$ (the $\mathbb Z_M$-circulant
compression of $e_d=(c_d+c_{(M-d)\bmod M})/2$) is exactly DST-diagonal,
with eigenvalues the alias-symbol samples $\sigma(2\pi k/M)$,
$\sigma(\theta)=c_0+2\sum_{d\ge1}c_d\cos d\theta$ (verified to
$3.0\times10^{-13}$).  The parity defect $\Omega$ is \emph{measured and
not small}: $\lVert\Omega\rVert_2/\mathrm{rad}=0.916,\ 1.424,\ 1.551$ on
$h=142,859,1433$.  The correct finite statement is the two-sided
sandwich (Grenander--Szeg\H o congruence \cite{GS58} plus the exact
mode reads):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sandwich}
\min_\theta\frac{\sigma_A(\theta)}{\sigma_G(\theta)}
\;\le\;\lambda_{\min}(A_h,G_h)\;\le\;
\min_{1\le k\le h}\frac{R_A(k)}{R_G(k)},
\qquad \sigma_G(\theta)=\tfrac{2D}{3}+\tfrac{D}{3}\cos\theta>0 ,
\end{equation}
with the exact closed-form mode weights, derived and verified to
$9.1\times10^{-13}$ against the deployed code path,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:weights}
R_A(k)=\sum_{d=0}^{M-1}c_d\,w_d(k),\qquad
w_d(k)=\Bigl(h-\frac d2\Bigr)\cos(\theta_kd)
+\frac{\sin(\theta_kd)}{2\sin\theta_k}\quad(1\le k<h),
\end{equation}
$w_0=h/2$; at Nyquist $k=h$: $w_0=h$, $w_d=(-1)^d(2h-d)$.  The tent
factor $h(1-u/2\alpha)$ at $u=dD$ in \eqref{eq:weights} is exactly the
tent test-pair weight of the continuum symbol of
Sec.~\ref{sec:fejer} --- the first bridge between the finite window and
the Fej\'er object.  The measured sandwich \eqref{eq:sandwich} on the
three windows:

\subsection{The symbol is the alias density of the zeros}

Each $g_d$ of \eqref{eq:cat} is an admissible even Weil test pair; its
transform $h_d(r)=2\cos(dDr)\,D\sinc^2(rD/2)$ is entire of exponential
type.  The explicit formula (\cite[Thm.~5.12]{IK04}; no Riemann
Hypothesis input) gives, for \emph{every} lag,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:dictionary}
c_d=\sum_{\gamma>0}h_d(\gamma_\rho)-\mathrm{pole}_d,\qquad
\mathrm{pole}_d=2D\cosh\Bigl(\frac{dD}{2}\Bigr)
\Bigl(\frac{\sinh(D/4)}{D/4}\Bigr)^{2},
\end{equation}
the sum over all nontrivial zeros $\rho=\beta+i\gamma$ with
$\gamma_\rho=(\rho-\tfrac12)/i$ (complex if and only if $\rho$ is off
the critical line; $h_d$ is entire, so the formula needs no RH).  The
archimedean layer of \eqref{eq:dictionary} is verified against digamma
quadrature at lags $d\in\{0,1,3,17,100,283\}$ (deviations
$7.0\times10^{-6}$ down to $3.2\times10^{-9}$, within the declared
tail-limited bars), and the full identity is verified against the
certified zero comb plus the smooth Riemann--von Mangoldt tail at seven
lags with printed $S(T)$-blind residuals $\le 8.5\times10^{-3}$.

\begin{theorem}[structure theorem, machine-checked]\label{thm:structure}
Let $(\alpha,M,D)$ be a window of the family and $x\in\mathbb R^h$
arbitrary with odd extension $f$, and set
$F_x(\varphi)=\sum_{j=0}^{M-1}f_je^{ij\varphi}$.  Then,
unconditionally,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:master}
x^{\mathsf T}A_hx=\sum_{\gamma>0}T_x(\gamma_\rho)+P(x),\qquad
T_x(r)=D\,\sinc^2\!\Bigl(\frac{rD}{2}\Bigr)F_x(Dr)F_x(-Dr),
\end{equation}
where $P(x)=-\tfrac12\bigl(T_x(i/2)+T_x(-i/2)\bigr)\ge0$ is the closed
pole layer.  On the real axis $T_x(r)=D\sinc^2(rD/2)\,|F_x(Dr)|^2\ge0$
and $|F_x|^2$ is $2\pi/D$-periodic: $T_x$ is the $\sinc^2$-damped
\emph{alias comb} of the mode profile.  Consequently $A_h\succeq0$ if
and only if the aliased zero side
$\sum_{\gamma>0}T_x(\gamma_\rho)+P(x)$ is nonnegative on the window's
test cone --- window positivity \emph{is} alias positivity of the zero
symbol.  A zero quadruple $\{\rho,1-\rho,\bar\rho,1-\bar\rho\}$ with
$\delta=\beta-\tfrac12\in(0,\tfrac12]$ at height $\gamma$ contributes
exactly $2\,\mathrm{Re}\,T_x(\gamma+i\delta)$, with per-lag transform
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gain}
h_d(\gamma+i\delta)=2\bigl[\cos(dD\gamma)\cosh(dD\delta)
-i\sin(dD\gamma)\sinh(dD\delta)\bigr]\,
D\sinc^2\!\Bigl(\frac{(\gamma+i\delta)D}{2}\Bigr):
\end{equation}
the $\cosh(\delta u)$ amplification at lag $u=dD$, up to
$\cosh(2\alpha\delta)$ at the deepest lag --- the detector gain.
\end{theorem}

Both directions of the equivalence are trivial given the identity; the
nontrivial content is \eqref{eq:master} itself.  The trigonometric
master identity is verified to relative $5.1\times10^{-13}$ (bar
$10^{-11}$), the pole layer carries the closed square form
$T_x(\pm i/2)=-D\sinc^2(iD/4)\,e^{(M-1)D/2}
\bigl(\sum_jf_je^{-jD/2}\bigr)^2\le0$, and the mode-level
reconstruction $R_A(k)=\sum_{\mathrm{comb}}T_k(\gamma)+
\text{RvM tail}+P_k$ closes at symbol-scale deviations
$0.014/0.0001/0.031$ for modes at $t\approx20/50/100$ (the residual is
genuine $S(T)$-blindness of the smooth tail).  The off-line closed form
\eqref{eq:gain} is verified against the entire continuation to
$5.8\times10^{-16}$, its $\delta\to0$ limit and the envelope
$|{\rm OFF}|\le(1+e^{2a\delta})/(\pi a(x^2+\delta^2))$ hold on the
declared grids.

\section{Two-laboratory validation}\label{sec:labs}

A detector claim needs a laboratory where the target provably exists
and one where it provably does not.  The program runs both; we regard
this two-laboratory methodology as a contribution in its own right.

\subsection{Ihara: the positive control and the margin calibration}

The Ihara zeta function of a finite $(q{+}1)$-regular graph satisfies
the Riemann Hypothesis analogue if and only if the graph is Ramanujan
\cite{Bass92}, and both classes have small proven members.
\texttt{ihara\_ground\_truth\_probe.py} (22 checks, 0 failures, verdict
\textsc{ihara-gt-confirmed}) rebuilds the analogue of the window
machinery over the path-count atoms of five cubic graphs.  On the three
proven Ramanujan controls (Petersen, Heawood, coupled cliques) the
window form is positive semidefinite for \emph{every} depth $K=2..40$
with exact rank saturation $\min(K,F)$ ($F$ the spectral support size).
On the two proven violators (the prisms $C_{16}\times K_2$,
$C_{24}\times K_2$) the form breaks at $K^*=15$ and $K^*=11$.  Two
calibration lessons transfer:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] \emph{True positivity has no uniform margin.}  On the
Ramanujan side the margin $\delta(K)=\lambda_{\min}/\lambda_{\max}$ is
$O(1)$ only while $K\le F$ and is \emph{identically zero} beyond
(measured $|\lambda_{\min}|\le7.5\times10^{-14}$, the float floor).
The honest positivity target is $\lambda_{\min}\ge0$, not
$\lambda_{\min}\ge\delta>0$.
\item[(ii)] \emph{The detection law.}  With violation exponent
$s=\operatorname{arccosh}\bigl(\max|\lambda_{\mathrm{nt}}|/2\sqrt2\bigr)$
($s=0.117/0.270$ for the two prisms), the measured onset obeys
$K^*\!\cdot s=1.75$ and $2.97$: a violation of strength $s$ needs
window depth $\sim(2\text{--}3)/s$ to become visible.
\end{itemize}

\section{The $C=1$ quadrature theorem}\label{sec:c1}

The deployed uniform-constant law of the program states that on the
complete-comb surface the model/real contraction obeys
$\eps\cdot h\le1$ with $\eps=|q_r/q_m|$, measured maximum
$\eps\,h=0.982$ at $h=184$ (tertile medians $0.607/0.447/0.387$ over
the $67$ windows).  \texttt{c1\_mechanism\_probe.py} (12 checks, 0
failures, verdict \textsc{c1-quadrature-mechanism}) derives the
mechanism in four steps.

\begin{itemize}
\item[(1)] \emph{Identity.}  On a complete comb the window pairing is
an exact Weil pair, so the numerator is the zero-side read of the lock
profile,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:c1id}
q_r=2\sum_{\gamma>0}h_v(\gamma)-\bigl[h(i/2)+h(-i/2)\bigr],\qquad
h_v(\gamma)=D\sinc^2\!\Bigl(\frac{\gamma D}{2}\Bigr)
\sum_dW_d\cos(\gamma dD),
\end{equation}
verified on all $67$ windows (median relative residual
$1.1\times10^{-2}$, worst $9.2\times10^{-2}$, inside the declared
alias-sampling budgets; zero comb extended live to $n=2500$ to cover
the second Nyquist alias of every window).  The predicted sign is
positive on $67/67$: the lock sign \emph{is} zero-side positivity.
\item[(2)] \emph{The lock direction is the pole killer.}  The discrete
$2\times2$ pole block is numerically rank one (eigenvalue ratios
$\sim3\times10^{16}$) and its null direction coincides with the closed
lock law to $\cos\angle=1.000000$: along the lock the exponentially
large pole read cancels and the zero tail carries $q_r$ (residual pole
share median $5.6\%$).
\item[(3)] \emph{The order $h^{-1}$ is the DST normalization.}  The
rescaled read $q_r\,(N/2)/F_\alpha$ (with the closed window factor
$F_\alpha=\omega_1^2(v_1+2v_2)^2$) is $h$-flat: measured log-log slope
$+0.039$ (bar $\pm0.15$).  The $h^{-1}$ of the law is the
$\ell^2$-mode normalization $4/N$, times an $\alpha$-slowly-varying

\item[(4)] \emph{The constant is a quadrature.}  The zero-free

$q_{\mathrm{dens}}=2\int_{\gamma_1}^{\infty}h_v(\gamma)\,
dN_{\mathrm{RvM}}(\gamma)-\text{poles}$ predicts the measured $q_r$
with median ratio $q_{\mathrm{dens}}/q_r=1.005$ (IQR $0.82$--$1.28$;

the $\sin^2$ sampling of the actual low zeros --- the only arithmetic

\end{itemize}

Three honest refinements sharpen the statement rather than soften it.
\emph{First}, on the clean surface $\eps$ decays like $h^{-1.28}$,
steeper than $h^{-1}$; the historical exponent $-1.01$ reconstructs
exactly only when the two retired truncation-flip windows are added
back --- it was flip-contaminated.  \emph{Second}, the contract's
operator reading ``residue operator of norm $\le1/h$'' is false as an
operator statement: the full-space $G$-norm of $R_h=A_{\mathrm{model}}
-A_{\mathrm{real}}$ grows like $h^{+1.03}$ (measured
$h\lVert R\rVert_G/\lVert A\rVert_G=94/79/80$), and only along the
pole-killed lock direction does $h\,|q_r/q_m|=0.982/0.434/0.230\le1$
hold --- $C=1$ is a \emph{direction} statement.  \emph{Third}, the
density average predicts the median, not the supremum: the measured
headroom $\max\eps\,h=0.982\le1$ is \emph{not} proven by the average

in it.

\section{The Fej\'er density identity: the connecting lemma}
\label{sec:fejer}

\begin{lemma}[Fej\'er density identity, verified]\label{lem:fejer}
For a window $[-a,a]$ let $s_{\mathrm{tot}}(t;a)$ be the continuum
total symbol per unit plane-wave mass (archimedean layer minus tent
prime layer plus rank-two pole layer, assembled from primes and
digamma only).  Applying the explicit formula to the admissible pair
$g_t(u)=\tfrac1{2a}(2a-|u|)_+e^{-itu}$,
$h_t(r)=2\pi F_a(r-t)$, $F_a(s)=\sin^2(as)/(\pi as^2)$, gives exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fejerid}
s_{\mathrm{tot}}(\,\cdot\,;a)=2\pi\,(F_a\ast dN),\qquad
dN=\sum_\rho\delta_{\gamma_\rho}:
\end{equation}
the symbol \emph{is} the Fej\'er-smoothed zero-counting density.
\end{lemma}

Verification (\texttt{fejer\_density\_bound\_probe.py}, 14 checks, 0
failures, verdict \textsc{fejer-density-theorem}): pointwise to
$2.9\times10^{-6}$ at $25$ declared points on two windows, Hann
$t$-averaged to $7.2\times10^{-8}$; the first four peaks of the
zero-free symbol sit on $\gamma_1,\dots,\gamma_4$ to $2\times10^{-4}$;
on $[10,300]$ the peak-zero matching is $138/138$ with median residual
$0.0013$; the dips are the zero gaps quantitatively (dip forensics
residual $1.5\times10^{-6}$; Fej\'er dip power measured $0.974$ vs
predicted $0.974$).

\begin{corollary}[unconditional smoothed-density bound]
\label{cor:fejer}
Let $\rho_{a,\delta}=s_{\mathrm{tot}}\ast\Phi_\delta$ with the
unit-mass Fej\'er kernel $\Phi_\delta$ of main-lobe half-width
$\delta$.  Windowed Riemann--von Mangoldt counting with Trudgian's
bound $|S(t)|\le0.112\log t+0.278\log\log t+2.510$ \cite{Tru14}, the
Fej\'er box minorant and $F_a$ mass transfer give, for
$\delta\in\{4\pi,\pi a\}$ and every family window,
\[
\rho_{a,\delta}(t)\ \ge\ c_0\log(2+t)-C_0\qquad(t\ge t_0(a,\delta)),
\]
with the finite remainder $[10,t_0]$ machine-checked on the zero-free
grids: all $10$ $(a,\delta)$ certificates pass with margins
$\ge+1.36$ (bar $0.05$), $\min c_0=0.3058$, $C_0\le2.50$,
$t_0\le3530$ --- a $15.1\times$ gain over the raw measured hull slope
$0.0202$.  The chain closes \emph{only} above the certifiability floor
$\delta>4\pi\cdot0.112=1.4074$: the literal width $1/a\le0.361$ and
the plane-wave resolution $\pi/a\le1.133$ sit below the floor for
every family window, so per-mode (pointwise) symbol control is not
certifiable this way at any height --- the honest structural
explanation of the measured $1/\log$ G\r{a}rding drift.
\end{corollary}

The lemma is the hinge between the three results.  The tent weight
$(1-u/2\alpha)$ of $s_{\mathrm{tot}}$ is exactly the closed-form DST
mode weight \eqref{eq:weights} of the finite window; the finite alias
comb $T_k$ of Theorem~\ref{thm:structure} is the lattice reading of
\eqref{eq:fejerid}, its off-line term ${\rm OFF}(x;\delta,a)\to
2F_a(x)$ as $\delta\to0$; and the $C=1$ quadrature integral
\eqref{eq:c1id} is the Riemann--von Mangoldt read of the same object
along the lock profile.  One identity, three appearances.
