\begin{abstract}
We describe a family of finite, explicitly computable operators
--- truncations of one glued geometric object built from the Gaussian
$E_8$ lattice --- whose spectral data measurably reproduce the Weil
measure of prime number theory without ever loading a prime table or
a zeta zero.  The finite places are the Hecke--commensurability tower
of the rank-$4$ Hermitian unimodular $\Z[i]$-lattice: its primitive
degrees \emph{are} the Gaussian prime norms, and a circle-free
logarithm generator plus conjugation descent produce the atom comb of
the Weil measure at deviation $0.0$ \MEASURED.  The archimedean place
is a $48$-site spin cover lift whose $\mu_4$-fixed heat trace is
exactly the density behind
$\Rey\psi(\tfrac14+i\tau/2)$ --- the $\tfrac14$ is derived, not
declared --- and the two sides glue with \emph{one} normalization: a
free three-scalar fit returns $(1,1,1)$ to below $10^{-12}$, and
among the dimension-$8$ unimodular lattices $\{\Z^8,E_8\}$ only the
Gaussian $E_8$ glues \MEASURED.  On every truncation the Weil
functional is a state (GNS vector state at KMS $\beta=1$); the
truncation eigenvalues, frozen by SHA-256 \emph{before} any zero is
loaded, hit $100\%$ of the first $377$ zeta zeros at tolerance
$0.25$ with ladder rate $-1.61$, and the matched-node
nearest-neighbour statistic is $0.6178$ against the zeros' own
$0.6189$ \MEASURED.  The finite trace formula of the family is exact
Gauss quadrature, and its term dictionary to the classical Weil
explicit formula closes block by block at $10^{-13}$--$10^{-16}$.  We
then decompose what remains: two of the three convergence steps are
classical or measured (half-plane convergence with an unconditional
Chebyshev majorant, boundary exactly at $s=\tfrac12$; tightness), the
node-capture half of the third is proof-near, and the one remaining
statement --- Weil positivity in the limit --- is localized in four
machine-verified equivalent languages (Hankel, Levinson, Fej\'er,
Krein--Suzuki), each finitely decidable per window.
\emph{No-proof fence:} this note claims no theorem about the Riemann
zeta function and no progress on the Riemann Hypothesis at theorem
level.  Its contributions are the computable zeta-free scaffold, the
prediction-freeze methodology, the documented negatives, and the
four-language localization of the remaining statement.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and claim typing}\label{sec:intro}

The Hilbert--P\'olya heuristic asks for a self-adjoint operator whose
spectrum is the sequence of nontrivial zeta zeros.  The obstruction
is well mapped: Weil's explicit formula \cite{Weil52} identifies the
zero side with a geometric side (pole, primes, archimedean term), and
the Riemann Hypothesis is equivalent to positivity of the geometric
functional on autocorrelations; Connes \cite{Connes99} produced a
trace-formula realization on the ad\`ele class space in which
positivity again carries the full content; Meyer \cite{Meyer05}
proved the spectral realization unconditionally --- \emph{without}
positivity, which marks the proved boundary line; Connes--Consani
\cite{CC21} established Weil positivity in the atom-free regime by
Sonin-space compression; and Suzuki \cite{Suzuki21} encoded GRH as
positivity of an explicit canonical-system Hamiltonian family,
constructed unconditionally exactly on the half-plane parameter range
$\omega>1$.

This note reports a different kind of object inside that map: a
\emph{computable, zeta-free truncation family} --- finite matrices
built from lattice counting and a cover-lift heat trace, with no
prime table and no zero data anywhere in the construction path ---
together with systematic \emph{measurements} of how much of the
Hilbert--P\'olya picture the family already exhibits at finite size,
and machine-checked bookkeeping of exactly what would remain to be
proved.  All results come from the permanent verification suite of
the TFPT repository \cite{TFPT}: the modules \texttt{v714},
\texttt{v716}--\texttt{v721} (the ``moonshot arc'') and
\texttt{v727}--\texttt{v734} (the ``keystone round''), each a
standalone script whose checks, bars and verdicts are printed by an
executed run.

Every load-bearing claim below is typed:
\begin{itemize}
\item \PROVED{} --- a finite computation certified exactly (integer
or rational arithmetic, or a residual at floating-point machine
scale), or a one-line classical argument reproduced and
machine-instantiated in the module;
\item \MEASURED{} --- a numerical finding with declared bars, error
ladders and controls; a measurement, not a theorem;
\item \KILLED{} --- a preregistered hypothesis whose kill criterion
fired; documented because negatives constrain the search space;
\item \DECLARED{} / \CITED{} --- a normalization chosen and frozen
before comparison, resp.\ classical input used with attribution.
\end{itemize}
The single most important methodological device is the
\emph{prediction freeze}: whenever zeta zeros are used as comparison
targets, every prediction of the construction (node positions, peak
lists, capture radii, centroids) is serialized and its SHA-256 hash
printed \emph{before} the module loads any zero; the zeros live only
in isolated \texttt{declared\_*} functions, and an AST firewall bans
prime/zeta identifiers from the construction path.

\emph{No-proof fence (stated first, repeated last).}  Nothing in this
note is a theorem about $\zeta$.  The spectral agreement of
Section~\ref{sec:measure} is a measurement; the positivity of the
truncations is a per-window finite computation, not a limit
statement; and the one statement that would close the circle is named
in Section~\ref{sec:wall} and left open.

\section{The glued object}\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Finite places: the \texorpdfstring{$\Z[i]$-$E_8$}{Z[i]-E8}
commensurability tower}\label{sec:tower}

Let $L$ be the $E_8$ root lattice with the root-compatible complex
structure $J$ ($J^2=-1$, $JL=L$), i.e.\ the rank-$4$ Hermitian
unimodular $\Z[i]$-lattice of \cite{TFPTnote3}.  Module \texttt{v714}
($19/19$ checks, verdict \textsc{moonshot-go}) builds the
commensurability tower of $L$: all $\Z[i]$-submodules of index $n$,
enumerated by Hermite-normal-form cells, graded by the abelian index
$n=N(\det)$.  Three findings \MEASURED:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \emph{Primitive degrees are the Gaussian prime norms.}  A
degree-$n$ layer is primitive iff an index-$n$ submodule admits no
proper intermediate module.  The machine census over $n\le35$ gives
the primitive degrees $\{2,5,9,13,17,29\}$ --- exactly the norms of
Gaussian primes, \emph{not} the rational primes: $3,7,11,\dots$ enter
only through their squares (inert).  Maximality is checked
exhaustively at $n=2,5,9,13$ ($15/15$, $312/312$, $820/820$,
$4760/4760$ maximal) and fails identically at the composite degrees
$4,8,10$ ($0$ maximal out of $155$, $1395$, $4680$).  No prime
projector exists anywhere in the code: the primes come \emph{out} of
the tower.
\item[(ii)] \emph{The logarithm is generated internally.}  The raw
layer count is divisorial ($a_5=312=2(1+5+25+125)$).  The
groupoid-internal log generator --- the derivative of the degree
grading, $a_n\log n=\sum_{d\mid n,\,d\ge2}\Lambda_A(d)\,a_{n/d}$ ---
combined with the rank-$4$ cell normalizer $1+n+n^2+n^3$
de-divisorizes the trace: the resulting $\Lambda_{\mathrm{geo}}$ is
supported exactly on prime-ideal powers, with on-support relative
deviation $1.1\times10^{-14}$ and off-support maximum
$4.0\times10^{-13}$ over $20000$ slots.
\item[(iii)] \emph{Conjugation descent yields the Riemann comb.}
Complex conjugation $\sigma$ acts on the tower; primitive classes
split into $\sigma$-swapped pairs (split, orbit length $\log N$),
ramified ($\log N$) and inert with $\mu_2$ isotropy (half orbit,
length $\tfrac12\log N=\log m$).  The descended orbit comb --- atoms
at $u=k\ell$ per orbit base, masses
$\mu_n=2\Lambda_{\mathrm{geo}}(n)/\sqrt n$ with the $\sqrt n$
half-density \DECLARED{} --- matches the first $100$ atom positions,
all $100$ masses and $368$ window-lag moments of the deployed Weil
measure at deviation $0.0$.
\end{enumerate}
The honest fence of the slice: the finite counting facts in
(i)--(iii) are carrier-generic ($E_8$-unspecific); the specificity
question belongs to the gluing (Section~\ref{sec:glue}).

\subsection{The archimedean place: cover lift and the derived
\texorpdfstring{$\tfrac14$}{1/4}}\label{sec:arch}

Module \texttt{v716} ($18/18$, verdict
\textsc{moonshot-stage2-glued}) identifies the archimedean
incarnation of the \emph{same} object.  The carrier is the $48$-site
Neveu--Schwarz cover lift: a clock of order $12$ with plane
$\Z/12=\mu_4\times\mu_3$, where $\mu_4$ matches the unit group
$\Z[i]^\times=\langle i\rangle$ of the finite tower (the $\Z[i]$
tower \emph{is} the $\mu_4$-fixed part of the $\Z$-commensurability
groupoid; character bookkeeping exact, deviation $0.0$) and $\mu_3$
carries the deck twists $\nu\in\{\tfrac16,\tfrac12,\tfrac56\}$
\MEASURED.  The $+i$ eigenspace of the half-turn --- the modes
$m\equiv1\bmod4$ --- has heat trace
\[
\rho(u)\;=\;\frac{e^{-u/2}}{1-e^{-2u}},
\]
and the machine-verified exact identity (deviation
$\le1.4\times10^{-10}$ over the $\tau$-battery)
\begin{equation}\label{eq:psi}
\Rey\,\psi\!\Bigl(\frac14+\frac{i\tau}{2}\Bigr)
=\int_0^\infty\Bigl[\frac{e^{-2u}}{u}-2\rho(u)\cos(\tau u)\Bigr]du
\end{equation}
shows that the deployed archimedean density is the cosine transform
of this lift heat trace.  Two normalization facts are typed with
care: the $\tfrac14$ in \eqref{eq:psi} is \emph{derived} --- it is
the $\mu_4$ fixed-sector offset, modes $m\equiv1\bmod4$ giving
spectrum $2(k+\tfrac14)$ \PROVED{} --- while $\log\pi$ is the
Frullani comparison of the UV reference scale $2\to2\pi$ (deviation
$0.0$), i.e.\ the self-dual (Tate) normalization of the archimedean
place \DECLARED, the exact mirror of the declared $\sqrt n$
half-density at the finite places; it is machine-pinned to enter the
deployed window only through the $d=0$ UV cell (at
$2.2\times10^{-16}$).  The pole term $2\cosh(u/2)$ is the two
boundary exponents $s=1,0$.  The finite-size operator ladder
converges to this continuum object at rate $\sim N^{-2}$: the joint
scalar $s^*(N)$ runs $0.99078720\to0.99996921$ over $N=48$--$768$
with geometric extrapolant $|s^*_\infty-1|=2.4\times10^{-7}$
\MEASURED.  (That the three digamma channels of the archimedean
density are exactly the three deck sectors of this lift was
established separately in \texttt{v705}, tower traces exact at
$3.4\times10^{-16}$, scalar forced to $1$.)

\subsection{One normalization; \texorpdfstring{$E_8$}{E8} forced at
the gluing}\label{sec:glue}

The unified trace functional --- the Weil measure candidate --- is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:weilmeasure}
W \;=\; 2\cosh(u/2)\,du\;-\;\rho(u)\,du\;-\;
\sum_{n}\frac{\mu_n}{2}\bigl(\delta_{\log n}+\delta_{-\log n}\bigr),
\qquad \mu_n=\frac{2\Lambda_{\mathrm{geo}}(n)}{\sqrt n},
\end{equation}
built with \emph{no} per-sector scalar: atoms from the tower comb
(Gaussian-class sieve to $X=262144$: $22996$ irreducibles, comb
slots $23150$, missing/extra none), archimedean sector from the lift
trace, pole from the boundary exponents.  The gluing test of
\texttt{v716} then measures: the atom-sector scalar
$\kappa_{\mathrm{at}}=1$ exactly on all five windows
($\le10^{-12}$); the unified object equals the deployed window
vectors at $2.2\times10^{-16}$ over all lags of all five windows; and
--- the nail --- a \emph{free} three-scalar least-squares fit
$(\kappa_{\mathrm{at}},\kappa_{\mathrm{ar}},\kappa_{\mathrm p})$
returns $(1.0,\,1.0,\,1.0)$ to below $10^{-12}$: one object, one
normalization \MEASURED.  Must-fail counterfactuals fire as demanded:
the $\mu_2$ tower ($\Z$ carrier, all odd modes) leaves residual
$0.326$, the wrong $\mu_4$ class ($m\equiv3\bmod4$) residual
$1.000$, the wrong deck-twist channel set misses $\rho$ by $0.474$
\KILLED.

Module \texttt{v719} (F3) adds the census form of the same
statement: the unimodular $\Z$-lattices in dimension $8$ are
classically exactly $\{\Z^8,E_8\}$ (Mordell \CITED); both carry a
complex structure, the carrier parity fixes the mode-tower period,
and in the gluing battery \emph{only} the Gaussian $E_8$ tower glues
($\kappa=1$, residual $8.5\times10^{-17}$), while $\Z^8/\mu_2$ rips
at $0.102$ and the wrong $\mu_4$ class at $0.314$ \MEASURED.  $E_8$
is not an input of the construction; it becomes forced \emph{at the
gluing}.

\subsection{Windows and truncations}\label{sec:windows}

The measurement frames \DECLARED{} are the frame-A window ladder:
odd piecewise-linear test functions on the half-integer lattice with
bandwidth parameter $D=D(h)$ and depth $M=2h$, for
$h=142,203,344,488,606,878,1027,1256,1433$ (nine windows; a
five-window subfamily $h=184$--$1433$ with sieve reach $X=256$ to
$262144$ is used where reach ladders matter).  The window sees $W$
only through its lag vector $p=c_{\mathrm{ar}}+c_{\mathrm{at}}
+c_{\mathrm p}$ (archimedean integral $+$ atom sums $+$ closed pole
lags), and the deployed quadratic form is the odd Toeplitz form
$B=\mathrm{odd\_toeplitz}(p)$.  The GNS truncation of the
multiplication/shift operator is obtained by mapping the trigonometric
moments to $[-2,2]$ via $x=2\cos\theta$, running the Wheeler
(modified-Chebyshev) algorithm to the $K=h$ Jacobi coefficients, and
diagonalizing the tridiagonal Jacobi matrix $J_K$; its eigenvalues
are the $K$ Gauss nodes of the window measure, reported in
$\tau$-coordinates $\tau_j=\arccos(x_j/2)/D$.

\section{Measurements}\label{sec:measure}

\subsection{The Weil functional is a state on every
truncation}\label{sec:state}

Module \texttt{v717} ($21/21$, verdict
\textsc{state-on-truncations}) poses convolution positivity exactly
on the finite test algebra: with $K$ the unified kernel of
\eqref{eq:weilmeasure} and $\tilde f=\overline{f(-\,\cdot)}$, the
binding identity
\[
T(f*\tilde f)\;=\;u^{\mathsf T}\,\mathrm{Toeplitz}(c)\,u
\;=\;2\,V^{\mathsf T}B\,V
\]
holds at relative deviation $10^{-12}$ over an $80$-vector battery
on all five windows \PROVED{} (float-exact identity).  Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \emph{Constructive state.}  The Levinson recursion on the
unified moment vector has all reflection coefficients $|k|<1$
(maximum $0.2376$, minimum headroom $0.76$): $\mathrm{Toeplitz}(p)$
is positive definite at full depth, and $T$ is exhibited as the GNS
vector state $\langle e_0,\cdot\,e_0\rangle$ of the truncated
translation flow \MEASURED.  The positive-definiteness margins are
$\lambda_{\min}(B,G_m)=2.679\times10^{-2}$ down to
$1.516\times10^{-3}$ over the five windows.
\item[(ii)] \emph{KMS structure.}  The degree grading as time
evolution (Bost--Connes \cite{BC95}) gives a Gibbs structure with
\emph{one} global half-density $e^{-u/2}$ across all three sectors:
the three closed identities (flow partition, boundary characters,
orbit counting $\mu_n\sqrt n=2\Lambda_{\mathrm{geo}}(n)$) hold at
$2.2\times10^{-16}$, $5.7\times10^{-14}$, $1.8\times10^{-15}$, and
evenness of $W$ --- the functional equation --- becomes detailed
balance $M(-u)=e^{-u}M(u)$ at $1.1\times10^{-16}$: KMS at
$\beta=1$, the Bost--Connes critical temperature, with the
$s=\tfrac12$ axis as the symmetric GNS splitting \PROVED{} (closed
identities).  The half-density exponent is measured, not assumed:
$-\tfrac12$ fits at residual $8.9\times10^{-16}$, while $-\tfrac14$
and $-1$ misfit by $2.577$ and $5.153$ \MEASURED.
\item[(iii)] \emph{Controls.}  On the Ihara/Petersen lab (Ramanujan
graph), where the trace formula is a theorem, the identical machinery
produces an exact state (integer path counts equal the spectral side
at $2.7\times10^{-9}$).  On the Epstein control ($x^2+5y^2$,
discriminant $-20$, class number $2$) the orbit measure itself has
negative sites (first at $n=36$) and the same window machinery
suffers Levinson breakdown: $T$ is \emph{not} a state there.  The
construction measures arithmetic, not an artifact of the pipeline
\MEASURED.
\end{enumerate}
The naive regular-representation reading (pole square $+$ diagonal
orbit state) does \emph{not} give $T$: the difference $\Delta$ is
mixed-sign and, at the generalized minimizers, carries
$|\Delta|/\text{margin}=104$--$1563$ --- the trace-formula boundary,
localized (and resolved classically in Section~\ref{sec:trace}).

\subsection{Spectrum onto zeros, behind a prediction
freeze}\label{sec:spectrum}

Module \texttt{v718} ($10/10$, verdict
\textsc{spectrum-converges-measured}) is the quantitative
Hilbert--P\'olya measurement.  Methodology first: the Gauss-node
predictions of all nine windows are serialized and frozen by the
printed hash \texttt{SHA256 bc78e79f4b39506e\ldots} \emph{before}
the declared target section loads $379$ zeros
($\gamma\le650.669$); the operator itself never sees them.  Findings
\MEASURED:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \emph{Hits.}  In the matching band ($\tau\in[10,\,
0.9\pi/D]$, which contains $377$ of the loaded zeros), the largest

\item[(ii)] \emph{Rates.}  Over the first $20$ zeros the $h$-ladder

log-log slope $-1.610$; the pure GNS ladder at fixed measure
($K=179,358,716,1433$) is monotone:
$0.4678\to0.0153\to0.0042\to0.0003$.  The truncation eigenvalues
move \emph{onto} the zeros.
\item[(iii)] \emph{The full spectrum, typed.}  At $h=1433$ the
non-partnered nodes are exactly the expected GNS quadrature picture:
$27$ nodes in the pole band $\tau<14.1$ (trivial-mode mass),

is the zero sequence \emph{plus} quadrature filling.
\item[(iv)] \emph{Statistics.}  As an observation (no gate), the
nearest-neighbour ratio statistic of the matched node positions is
$\langle r\rangle=0.6178$ versus $0.6189$ for the zeros themselves
over $374$ values in $[30,649]$ (GUE $0.5996$, Poisson $0.3863$
\CITED).
\item[(v)] \emph{Controls.}  On the Ihara lab the identical
construction rank-saturates and hits the graph spectrum at
$2.2\times10^{-16}$ --- exact spectral identification at finite $K$,
which is what a proved trace formula looks like.  The scramble
control (atom positions redrawn uniformly, masses kept, $h=606$)
suffers Levinson breakdown at depth $61$: the scrambled measure is
not even a state --- a structure-borne failure, stronger than any
density-chance bound \KILLED.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The decomposition: classical, measured, and what
remains}\label{sec:decomp}

The program's ledger splits the missing continuum statement into
three convergence steps K1--K3 plus one identification step L1.
Modules \texttt{v720}, \texttt{v721}, \texttt{v727}--\texttt{v731}
adjudicate them.

\subsection{K2 is classical on \texorpdfstring{$s>\tfrac12$}{s>1/2};
the boundary is exactly the critical line}\label{sec:k2}

Module \texttt{v720} ($14/14$, verdict
\textsc{k2k3-collapsed-classical}).  For the limit exchange
(window evaluations against $e^{-s|u|}\cos(tu)$), tail and
discretization errors separate cleanly, with a named unconditional
majorant \PROVED{} (classical ingredients):
\[
|F_h-F_\infty|\;\le\;2\,c_{\mathrm{RS}}\,
\frac{s+\tfrac12}{s-\tfrac12}\,X_h^{\frac12-s}\;+\;C_{\mathrm{disc}}
\,D_h^2,
\qquad c_{\mathrm{RS}}=1.03883,
\]
the Rosser--Schoenfeld Chebyshev bound $\psi(x)\le c_{\mathrm{RS}}x$
machine-verified in the sieve range ($x\le262144$, maximum
$1.03882$ at $x=113$) \CITED$+$\PROVED, discretization rates

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

There exists a finite, explicitly computable, zeta-free family of
operators --- one glued object: Gaussian-$E_8$ Hecke tower at the
finite places, a $48$-site cover lift at the archimedean place, one
normalization, $E_8$ forced at the gluing --- that is a state on
every truncation, satisfies an exact finite trace formula whose term
dictionary to the Weil explicit formula closes at machine precision,
and whose truncation spectra, frozen before any zero is loaded,
measurably converge onto the zeta zeros with quantified rates and
GUE-consistent statistics.  Around it, the program has now sorted
every step of the would-be limit argument into classical
(K2, with the boundary exactly at $s=\tfrac12$), measured (K3
tightness; the spectral identification), proof-near (node capture;
the pinning lemma with zero violations), killed (naive reflection
positivity, direct Sonin transfer, strong gap universality, the
locality mechanism), and \emph{open}: one statement --- PD
persistence of the window ladder, equivalently Hankel positivity of
the damped moments, nonnegativity of the Fej\'er symbol, or
$H\ge0$ for the Krein canonical system --- finitely decidable per
window, measured true on all nine windows, with the universal
quantifier over the depth $h$ as the entire remaining substance.

\paragraph{All-place/Tate audit (2026-09-05).}
A later claim audit narrows the role of every finite carrier in this
note.  Conditional on the strict compact-window floor reported in the
external Zhu v2 preprint, rank--nullity excludes representing the full
window Weil form as one exact linear pullback through a single fixed
finite-dimensional carrier.  It does not exclude this note's growing
finite ladder, nonlinear procedures, or a compatible direct limit.
The same audit proves the scalar Tate projection and its local zeta
factorization, but constructs neither the global all-place intersection

counterexample.  These boundaries are executable in
\texttt{v1021\_\allowbreak all\_\allowbreak place\_\allowbreak tate\_\allowbreak rank\_\allowbreak audit.py}
($23/23$).  No RH claim follows.

\emph{No-proof fence, repeated.}  We claim no theorem about the
Riemann zeta function and no progress on the Riemann Hypothesis at
theorem level.  The truncation family, the prediction-freeze
measurements, the exact finite trace formula, the documented
negatives and the four-language localization are offered as
computable scaffolding --- a place where the remaining statement can
be attacked one finite window at a time, and where any failure would
be visible as a single red check.
