Title

The window quadratic form as an off-line zero detector: a structure

Abstract (copy verbatim)
------------------------
The finite window quadratic form of the TFPT prime-front program --
identified in a companion note (submitted in parallel) as a
hat-function Galerkin discretization of Suzuki's localized Weil
operator -- is shown to satisfy an exact structure theorem: the
deployed odd-Toeplitz form is the Cantoni-Butler odd-sector
compression of the full Toeplitz matrix of the Weil lag vector, its
generalized spectrum is sandwiched between the periodized-symbol
minimum and the discrete-sine-mode reads (the naive "DST-diagonal"
reading is false; the measured parity defect is $O(1)$), and every
mode read is, unconditionally by the Weil explicit formula, a
$\mathrm{sinc}^2$-damped alias comb over the nontrivial zeros plus an
explicit nonnegative pole layer, in which a hypothetical off-line zero
quadruple at $\tfrac12+\delta+i\gamma$ enters with the amplification
$\cosh(\delta u)\le\cosh(2\alpha\delta)$ per lag $u$ -- the form is an
off-line zero detector. The detector is validated in two exact
laboratories: on the Ihara side (proven Ramanujan and non-Ramanujan
graphs) the machinery is positive semidefinite on every proven-true
control and breaks on the proven violators with the detection law
$K^*\cdot s\approx 2$--$3$, and at the Epstein zeta function of
$x^2+5y^2$ a measured argument-principle census of $26$ off-line
zeros ($12$ in $[0.6,1.4]\times[2,100]$) predicts the measured form
break quantitatively ($\lambda_{\min}$ ratio $0.803$ inside the
preregistered factor-$2$ gate). On the deployed $67$-window family
the measured positivity becomes a certificate with a quantified
threshold map $s_{\min}(w,\gamma)$ (median $2\alpha\,s_{\min}=
1.4$--$2.6$ on the resolved band $\gamma\le\pi/D\le 868$), and the
$C=1$ contraction constant of the model/real ladder is identified as
a quadrature statement: the zero-free density integral of the lock
profile predicts the measured constant with median ratio $1.005$. The
Fejer density identity $s_{\mathrm{tot}}=2\pi(F_a\ast dN)$, verified
to $2.9\times10^{-6}$ and carrying an unconditional smoothed-density
lower bound with $c_0=0.306$ (Riemann-von Mangoldt counting plus
Trudgian's $|S(t)|$ bound, ten finite machine-checked certificates),
is the lemma connecting all three results. Uniform positivity over
the full window family is the conjecture -- by Weil's criterion
equivalent to the Riemann Hypothesis -- and nothing in this note
claims it.
