\section{Dictionary and the four coordinates}\label{sec:four}

\begin{prop}[terminal dictionary]\label{prop:dict}
On a canonical window,
$q_N=\rho_{N-1}/(\tfrac57)=\tfrac75 Z^2$.
Hence $q_N<1$ if and only if $\lvert Z\rvert<M$
with $M=\sqrt{5/7}$.
Over $\mathbb{Q}$: $Z=\tfrac12$ gives
$q_N=\tfrac7{20}$.
The death channel
$\lvert\Zloc\rvert\ge M\iff\lvert Z\rvert\ge M\iff q_N>1$
is a $181/181$ census (r386).
The chart identity $\mathrm{sch}<0$ on the
living $42$ and $\mathrm{sch}>0$ on the six
terminal-dead $\chi$-sprouts is a census (r417).
\end{prop}

Four quantities were proposed as equivalent
death/life coordinates:
\[
  q_N>1
  \;\iff\;
  \lvert\Zloc\rvert\ge M
  \;\iff\;
  \mathrm{sch}>0
  \;\iff\;
  \text{razor-overlap }10^{-4}\text{ vs }10^{-8}.
\]
The first three match on every world of this
round (selected, core-$42$, dead $\chi$).
The fourth does not: see~\S\ref{sec:ov}.

\section{Razor-overlap is vacuous-only}\label{sec:ov}

The r422 four-order split
(living $\sim 10^{-8}$, dead $\sim 10^{-4}$)
was measured on \emph{vacuous} windows.
It does not extend.

\begin{prop}[overlap is not global]\label{prop:ov}
On the core $42$, P1 living razor-share occupies
$[8\cdot 10^{-9},\,3.6\cdot 10^{-2}]$
and overlaps the dead-$\chi$ range
$[1.5\cdot 10^{-4},\,2.4\cdot 10^{-2}]$.
Vacuous living core share is at most $2.9\cdot 10^{-7}$;
dead vacuous $\chi$ is at least $1.5\cdot 10^{-4}$.
The four-order separator is a vacuous-chart
classifier, not a $q_N$-margin meter.
Witness: $\mathrm{kz}=16$ (living, $q_N=0.9805$,
$\lvert\Zloc\rvert=0.756$, $\mathrm{sch}=-0.00151$)
has razor-share $5.4\cdot 10^{-9}$.
\end{prop}

The candidate mechanism was: when $q_N>1$,
border mass $s$ walks into the bulk near-defect
$\psi_{\Cmin}$ (the $C_{\min}$-mode, a
near-Nyquist face of the hole Gram).
On P1, $\Cmin<1$ and
$\langle\psi_{\Cmin},v_{\mathrm{Nyq}}\rangle\approx-0.386$
at $k_z=9$ (the r410 number): $s$ couples at
share $\sim 10^{-2}$ \emph{because the bulk already
has a defect}, independently of $q_N<1$.
On the vacuous chart the candidate fails in
the other direction:

\begin{itemize}
\item $\langle\psi_{\Cmin},v_{\mathrm{Nyq}}\rangle$
is $O(10^{-2})$ (k$=6$: $-0.007$; k$=7$: $+0.006$).
The $C_{\min}$-mode is \emph{not} the hole-Nyquist
vector (r410: Nyquist is not a spectral atom).
\item $s$ still has Nyquist overlap $O(10^{-2})$,
living or dead.
\item Living vacuous orthogonality
$\lvert\langle s,\psi_{\Cmin}\rangle\rvert^2/\lVert s\rVert^2\sim 10^{-8}$
is orthogonality to an \emph{implicit} near-threshold
eigenmode of $C$, not an explicit oscillating
source sum against $(-1)^j$.
\end{itemize}

\noindent
\textbf{Gate protocol.}
The $10^{-8}$ statement is not unconditional
(it needs the $C$-spectrum), not
PNT-elementary (no explicit phase), and would
be RH-near if one tried to identify
$\psi_{\Cmin}$ with a prime-comb test function.
The Nyquist rewrite is refuted.
Overlap remains a sharp \emph{vacuous} census,
not the most provable coordinate.
