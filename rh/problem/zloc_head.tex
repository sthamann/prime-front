\section{What $\Zloc$ is}\label{sec:id}

On a canonical window the terminal drive is
$Z=t_{N-2}+\mathrm{chain}$ with
$\mathrm{chain}=\alpha_{N-2}r_{N-2}+\beta_{N-2}r_{N-3}$.
The smooth-border reconstruction
$t_{N-2}=\sum_j w_j\,x_j\,v^{(2)}(x_j)\,\mathrm{fac}$
splits by the frozen hull-edge mask
$E=\{x\le \ell+F(\mathfrak{h}-\ell)\}\cup
\{x\ge \mathfrak{h}-F(\mathfrak{h}-\ell)\}$
at $F=\tfrac15$:
\begin{align*}
  t_{\mathrm{loc}}
  &=
  \sum_{x_j\in E} w_j\,x_j\,v^{(2)}(x_j)\,\mathrm{fac},\\
  \Zloc
  &=
  t_{\mathrm{loc}}+\mathrm{chain},\\
  Z
  &=
  \Zloc+t_{\mathrm{bulk}}.
\end{align*}
The hull of every selected window in this
round is $[-1,1]$ (up to a $2\cdot10^{-4}$
right endpoint).
$E$ is a \emph{geometric} tail, not the
set of small prime powers.

\begin{prop}[head identity]\label{prop:id}
$\Zloc=t_{\mathrm{loc}}+\mathrm{chain}$
and $Z=\Zloc+t_{\mathrm{bulk}}$ hold
identically (reconstruction $10^{-14}$;
Lean \texttt{canonical\_split}).
On the three live pins
$k=3,4,5$ the formula reproduces
$0.486846$, $0.157211$, $0.227187$.
At $k=4$,
$t_{\mathrm{loc}}=-0.03720$ and
$\mathrm{chain}=-0.12001$.
\end{prop}

$n_{\mathrm{edge}}$ on selected is
$85,217,113,429,989,1691$:
this is not a finite von~Mangoldt head.

\section{The unsigned estimate}\label{sec:tri}

\begin{prop}[triangle grows]\label{prop:tri}
The unsigned edge triangle
$\mathrm{tri}=\sum_{x_j\in E}\lvert ct_j\rvert$
on selected occupies $[2.240,\,19.245]$,
every value $>\tfrac12$.
The two-point log-log slope against $N_w$
($k=3$ vs $k=9$) is $+0.719$.
A Chebyshev majorant $\psi(x)\le 1.04x$
cannot close: the support of $E$ is a
positive fraction of a hull of width $2$,
not an interval $[1,X_{\mathrm{head}}]$.
Left--right pairing fails as a SATZ
($\mathrm{pair}_c=1$ on $k=5,7,9$).
Abel partial sums of the edge weights
have $\lvert A\rvert_{\max}/\lVert w\rVert_1=1$
on $k=3,4,6$; the phase $\varphi=xv^{(2)}\mathrm{fac}$
has variation $10^3$--$10^6$.
There is no explicit $k_0$.
\end{prop}

The signed $\lvert\Zloc\rvert$ itself falls
versus $N_w$ on this list (census, not SATZ)
because of cancellation of factor
$0.013$--$0.145$.
Selected is \emph{not} nicer for the
unsigned bound than the old $42$-rung
ladder (r386 slope $+0.25$, triangle up
to $14.5$); here the triangle grows faster.
