\section{Why this is not $\lambda_*$}

Three distinct gaps remain.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Discrete $D$ is a \emph{compression} of
$\Minf=(1-P)A(1-P)$, not the operator.
A lower bound on $D$ (so that $D^{-1}$ is controlled
from above) on the true $L^2$ complement is open.
\item $\varepsilon$ of $K_8$ only kills hat-mass on
$[0,8]$.  A higher uniform floor $\sigma(t_1)$ for
$t_1>8$ on the $K_8$-complement needs a new argument
(Slepians of $K_{t_1}$, or a Markov bound).
\item The $L^2$ leftover of the Nystr\"om space $V_{96}$
is the same completeness hole as r483/r484.
Certified jets stop at $\psi_8$ (r484 garbage
criterion).  There is no certified ONB of the complement
on which to run Filon$/1\sigma$.
\end{enumerate}

The flat majorant $D\succeq c_{\mathrm{tail}}I$ is
already false on the $T=150$ discrete rest
($d_{\min}=0.15$).
The flat Schur that uses $c_{\mathrm{tail}}$ can be
optimistic on a truncated $[0,T]$ Gram; that is why
the $T=400$ column is recorded.

\section{Open problem (Erd\H{o}s form)}

The remainder is a self-contained analytic question.
It does not mention the Riemann hypothesis, primes,
or the von~Mangoldt comb ($P\equiv0$ at this $L$).

\begin{problem}[Weighted archimedean coupling]
\label{pr:coupling}
Bound the coupling of the certified $5$-mode even
Slepian block into its orthocomplement against the
weighted archimedean reserve.
\end{problem}

\noindent\textbf{Data.}
Let $L=3/10$.
Let $A$ be the (even) Fourier multiplier on
$L^2[-L,L]$ with symbol
$\sigma_A(t)=\mathrm{Re}\,\psi(\tfrac14+it/2)-\log\pi$,
normalised so
$\langle u,Au\rangle=(1/2\pi)\int_{\mathbb R}\sigma_A(t)\,|\hat u(t)|^2\,dt$.
Let $K_{t_*}=\mathbf{1}_{[-t_*,t_*]}(D)$ in the Fourier
picture (the time-band-limited projection at $t_*=8$),
and let $\psi_0,\psi_2,\psi_4,\psi_6,\psi_8$ be the first
five even eigenfunctions of $K_8$ on $[-L,L]$
(prolate spheroidal / Slepian; $\lambda_0\ge\cdots\ge\lambda_8$,
with $\lambda_6\le1.46\cdot10^{-8}$ already certified).
Write $V=\mathrm{span}\{\psi_0,\psi_2,\psi_4,\psi_6,\psi_8\}$
and $V^\perp$ its orthogonal complement in $L^2[-L,L]$.
Let $P_V$ be the orthogonal projection onto $V$.
Set
\[
  B = P_V(A+\Pi)P_V,\qquad
  C = P_V(A+\Pi)(1-P_V),\qquad
  \Minf = (1-P_V)(A+\Pi)(1-P_V),
\]
where $\Pi$ is the even rank-one
$\Pi=2|\langle\,\cdot\,,\cosh(x/2)\rangle|^2$.

\noindent\textbf{Ask.}
Prove either of the following (they are equivalent
for the Schur test, up to the already-certified
$\lambda_{\min}(B)$ and the $\varepsilon$-bookkeeping
on $[0,8]$):

\begin{enumerate}
\item[\rm(i)]
$\| \Minf^{-1/2}\,C\, B^{-1/2} \| \le 1-\delta$
for an explicit $\delta>0$
(reviewer path B);
\item[\rm(ii)]
the operator Schur complement
$S = B - C\Minf^{-1}C^*$
satisfies $\lambda_{\min}(S)\ge c$ for an explicit
$c>0$
(then $\lambda_*(0.3)\ge c$ on the even sector,
and the odd sector is easier).
\end{enumerate}

A useful sufficient form is a quadratic-form lower
bound
$\langle v,\Minf v\rangle \ge
\int_{|t|\ge8}\sigma_A(t)\,|\hat v(t)|^2\,\frac{dt}{2\pi}
- E(v)$
for $v\in V^\perp$, with $E$ controlled by
$\varepsilon$ (band mass on $[0,8]$ is
$\le\varepsilon\|v\|_2^2$) plus an explicit time-band
edge.  The $1/\sigma$-weighted integrals that would
enter a Filon/IBP attack on $C\Minf^{-1}C^*$ are the
same class as r483, only with a $1/\sigma$ factor.

\noindent\textbf{What is already known, and may be used.}
$\sigma_A$ is even and increasing on $[0,\infty)$;
$\sigma_A(8)\ge0.2408$;
$V^\perp$ has almost no $K_8$-mass
($\varepsilon\le1.46\cdot10^{-8}$);
the leftover operator norm of $C$ on the $n=96$
Nystr\"om surface is $0.294$;
$99.1\%$ of that leftover HS-mass on $[8,150]$ sits
in $[30,150]$;
the top singular vector of the discrete $C$ has
$Q_A[0,150]/c_{\mathrm{tail}}=4.49$;
the finite compression $S_w$ on $V_{96}$ is
interval-PD.
Certified jets for IBP stop at $\psi_8$;
modes $\psi_{10+}$ are garbage at every $n\le128$.

\noindent\textbf{What is forbidden as a pad.}
The flat floor $\Minf\succeq c_{\mathrm{tail}}I$
used as if it were the operator
(already false on the $T=150$ discrete rest);
Fourier--HS majorants; crude $\kappa_{\mathrm{high}}$
as a SATZ pad; integer Fourier ONB;
$\sigma^2$ Plancherel as $\|Au\|_{[-L,L]}$;
IBP envelope as pad; exponential-sum Ci to $\infty$;
the $n=48$ leftover $2.76\cdot10^{-2}$ as a SATZ pad;
a $20\times$ coupling drop per even double-step;
QR sinc-IBP of garbage jets;
any representation of $V^\perp$ by unresolved
Nystr\"om nullspace modes.

\noindent\textbf{Honest type.}
This is an \emph{ideas} problem, not a remaining
arithmetic grind.
The frequency distribution is measured;
the Filon/IBP machine stands;
the finite $S_w$ is PD.
What is missing is a handle on $\Minf$ that does not
represent $V^\perp$ by garbage eigenmodes:
either a genuine lower bound for the weighted
quadratic form on the true complement, or a
completeness statement that the leftover of $V_{96}$
is smaller than the Higham margin.
A higher uniform floor $\sigma(t_1)$ is a new
analytic lemma, not a tighter quadrature.
