\begin{abstract}\noindent
Round 460 reduced the finite selected-window race to
\[
 \qdag=\sum_j\frac{|\langle u,e_j\rangle|^2}{1-\lambda_j}.
\]
This note tests the proposed smooth-versus-rough mechanism on the
production-faithful windows $k=5,\ldots,12$.  The qualitative alignment
effect is real: the top MAIN eigenvectors are low-frequency, their small
overlaps correlate with lower variation, the ARCH control is almost
perfectly aligned, and a deterministic scramble destroys the tiny MAIN
alignment.  The proposed Abel proof nevertheless fails its own numerical
gate.  Even the \emph{exact measured} partial sums of $u$ give a
three-mode bound $91.703$ to $119482.632$ times the measured band
contribution; the prescribed factor-$10^3$ stop gate fires for
$k=7,\ldots,12$.

There is also a decisive type obstruction.  The vector $u$ in the
spectral formula is the smooth-border functional after the
MAIN-dependent orthogonal-polynomial basis change.  It is not the raw
tent-folded von-Mangoldt lag.  Rosser--Schoenfeld controls the source
before that nonlinear construction, whereas Abel needs coordinate
partial sums after it.  No source-to-basis estimate is known.
The verdict is therefore
\begin{center}
\fbox{\parbox{0.92\linewidth}{\centering
\stuck:\quad \texttt{QUALITATIVE\_ALIGNMENT\_REAL;}\\
\texttt{ABEL\_CURRENCY\_TOO\_COARSE;}\\
\texttt{SOURCE\_TO\_BORDER\_BASIS\_BRIDGE\_MISSING}.}}
\end{center}
This is finite research documentation.  It does not prove a cofinal
overlap bound, the frequently-selected statement, or the Riemann
hypothesis.  No RH claim and no anti-RH claim is made.
\end{abstract}

\section{Object and sealed diagnostic band}

For the production-faithful selected window let $B$ be the negative-channel
sampling matrix in the positive-measure orthonormal-polynomial basis and
let $b$ be the smooth-border readout.  Round 460 uses
\[
 C=B^{T}B,\qquad u=\frac{b}{\sqrt{B_w}},\qquad
 C e_j=\lambda_j e_j,\qquad \|e_j\|_2=1.
\]
When $C<I$, spectral calculus gives the displayed formula for $\qdag$.
The arithmetic source enters both $C$ and $u$ through the construction
of the orthonormal basis.  In particular, there is no coordinate identity
$u=c^P$.

The diagnostic edge band is sealed as the three largest eigenvalues.
Equivalently, for each $k$ it is the adaptive interval
$\lambda>1-\delta_k$, where $\delta_k$ is the gap of the third-largest
mode.  This avoids choosing a threshold after seeing the overlaps.
At the two smallest dimensions the third mode is not close to one; it is
retained as a conservative control and not advertised as an asymptotic
edge mode.

For a unit vector $e=(e_0,\ldots,e_{N-1})$ define
\[
 \TV(e)=\sum_{r=0}^{N-2}|e_{r+1}-e_r|.
\]
The reported low-frequency share is the squared real-FFT mass in the
lowest quarter of available frequencies.  The contribution and budget
share are
\[
 q_j=\frac{|\langle u,e_j\rangle|^2}{1-\lambda_j},
 \qquad s_j=q_j/\qdag.
\]

The top-mode low-frequency share is $0.926$--$0.989$.  Thus the first
anatomical premise is true on this finite census.  However, the fitted
variation law is
\[
 \TV(e_{\rm top})\asymp N^{-0.196225},
\]
not $O(N^{-1})$.  Nor would an $O(N^{-1})$ theorem follow merely from
``finite Jacobi edge mode'': $C=B^TB$ is generally dense in this basis,
not the Jacobi multiplication matrix.  Even for tridiagonal Jacobi
matrices, coefficient regularity and delocalization hypotheses would be
needed to exclude localized edge eigenvectors.

The correlation between $\TV(e)$ and
$\log_{10}|\langle u,e\rangle|^2$ is $+0.745344$ on the eight top modes
and $+0.702626$ on all 24 tabulated modes.  This supports an association,
not a causal inequality or an asymptotic law.

\paragraph{ARCH.}
The smooth ARCH control aligns to its top mode at
$0.995813$--$0.998970$, exactly in the hypothesized direction.
Its normalized $u$ profile is also smoother than MAIN.  At full depth
the ARCH contraction has eigenvalues above one and its signed chain is
not a positive comparison object, as already recorded in round 460.
Only alignment, not an ARCH $\qdag$ budget, is compared here.

\paragraph{SCRAMBLE.}
The deterministic source-position scramble uses seed $46701+k$, preserves
the channel weights and border atoms, and changes the arithmetic
positions before rebuilding the orthogonal basis.  Its normalized top
overlap is 28.5 to $5.3\cdot10^5$ times MAIN.  This proves that the tiny
MAIN alignment is not a generic consequence of dimension or normalization.
It does \emph{not} isolate roughness: the scrambled top-eigenvector
variation is not held fixed, and the scrambled $u$ is often rough while
its overlap is much larger.  The control therefore supports arithmetic
specificity but refutes a monotone slogan ``rougher $u$ means smaller
overlap'' as a sufficient mechanism.

\section{The exact Abel inequality}

\begin{lemma}[Finite summation by parts]\label{lem:abel}
For $u,e\in\mathbb R^N$, let
\[
 U_r=\sum_{i=0}^{r}u_i,\qquad 0\le r<N.
\]
Then
\[
 \langle u,e\rangle
 =U_{N-1}e_{N-1}
  +\sum_{r=0}^{N-2}U_r(e_r-e_{r+1}),
\]
and consequently
\[
 |\langle u,e\rangle|
 \le \max_{0\le r<N}|U_r|\,
 \left(|e_{N-1}|+\TV(e)\right).
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Write $u_r=U_r-U_{r-1}$ with $U_{-1}=0$, expand the scalar
product, and shift the second finite sum.  The inequality is the triangle
inequality applied to the resulting identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Finite edge-band bound]\label{cor:band}
For any set $J$ of modes with $\lambda_j<1$, put
$A=\max_r|U_r|$ and
$V_j=|e_{j,N-1}|+\TV(e_j)$.  Then
\[
 \sum_{j\in J}
 \frac{|\langle u,e_j\rangle|^2}{1-\lambda_j}
 \le A^2\sum_{j\in J}\frac{V_j^2}{1-\lambda_j}.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Square Lemma~\ref{lem:abel}, divide by the positive spectral gaps,
and sum.
\end{proof}

This corollary is exact but is not the requested source lemma: $A$ is
computed after the MAIN basis change.  It is nevertheless the strongest
possible test of whether the proposed Abel currency is numerically close,
because replacing the exact $A$ by a classical source majorant cannot
repair a bound that already fails with the actual coordinate partial sums.

The classical input itself is available.  Rosser and Schoenfeld give
\[
 \psi(x)\le c_{\rm RS}x,\qquad c_{\rm RS}=1.03883.
\]
The finite sieve verifies
$\max_{5\le k\le12}\psi(2^k)/2^k=1.018864<c_{\rm RS}$.
Partial summation also gives
\[
 \sum_{n\le x}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}
 \le c_{\rm RS}(2\sqrt{x}-1).
\]
What is missing is an inequality transporting this raw-source estimate
through lag folding, signed moment formation, positive-channel
orthogonalization, and the border readout to
$\max_r|\sum_{i\le r}u_i|$.  Calling that transport constant one already
produces the bad third column of Table~\ref{tab:abel}; no such
constant-one theorem is asserted.

The user's decision rule was: continue if the bound is within factor
10, and stop honestly if it is worse than factor $10^3$.  No top-mode
factor is below 10.  The full three-mode Abel factor exceeds $10^3$ on
each $k=7,\ldots,12$ and reaches $119482.632$.  The route therefore
stops.  No lemma of the requested form
\[
 \sum_{\lambda_j>1-\delta}
 \frac{|\langle u,e_j\rangle|^2}{1-\lambda_j}\le B_{\rm safe}
\]
with source-explicit constants has been reached.

\section{Conditioning, determinism, and alias audit}

All eight decompositions were repeated by symmetric eigensolution at
80 decimal digits after exact conversion of the binary64 matrix entries.
Across the top-three modes, the maximum eigenvalue discrepancy is
$7.288\cdot10^{-16}$, the maximum overlap-square discrepancy is
$2.119\cdot10^{-14}$, and the maximum binary64 symmetric residual is
$1.310\cdot10^{-15}$.  These are diagnostics, not interval enclosures
of the source construction; no proof conclusion depends on them.
The interval-certified finite $\qdag<1$ statements remain those of
round 465.

The probe has fixed seeds, fixed top-mode count, fixed FFT cutoff, sealed
pins, and no zero data.  Two runs are required to produce byte-identical
numerical lines.

\paragraph{Audit against the thirty-one closed routes.}
The new \emph{anatomy} is not the old unsigned $|\mathcal Z_{\rm loc}|$
triangle: it measures the spectral projector coordinates that round 460
identified.  However, the proposed proof step is not wholly new.
The r429 catalog already contains ``Chebyshev $\psi$ / left--right Abel''
and r460 contains raw-resolvent majorization.  Once
$\max|U_r|$ is replaced by a source majorant and the mode sum is bounded
termwise, the present route enters exactly that triangle/majorant class.
The claimed novelty survives only as the empirically observed
test-direction alignment; it does not survive as a source-derived proof.

The remaining twenty-eight routes in the catalog use different
coordinates or quantifiers: fold pairing, finite-head Chebyshev,
Gershgorin/Schur assist, OSC--Geronimus, checkerboard, gap masks,
source Weyl energy, FORM\_T/FORM\_P, moment/Euler Gram, rank-one
Loewner, Nyquist density, Krein transport, Fourier/Bernstein test
polynomials, saturation, hole modes, de Branges phase, denominator gaps,
vacuous-chart coordinates, and finite fixed-$r$ blocks.  None is used as
a hidden premise here.  In particular there is no PNT replacement
density, Potapov product, octave additivity, or inferred cofinal
monotonicity.

\paragraph{Remaining gap, verbatim.}
\emph{Prove, from the tent-folded von-Mangoldt source and with constants
uniform on an infinite subsequence of selected windows, a bound on the
post-orthogonalization partial sums
$\max_r|\sum_{i\le r}u_{k,i}|$ that simultaneously beats the shrinking
spectral gaps; or replace coordinate Abel summation by a cancellation
estimate that retains the joint MAIN dependence of $u_k$ and the edge
projector.  Neither statement is proved here.}
