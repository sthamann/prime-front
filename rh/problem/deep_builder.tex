\section{Fit, both abscissae}\label{sec:aic}

Three diagnostic models (r421, not a proof):
$M_1$ floor $+ck^{-a}$, $M_2$ pure power, $M_3$
$c/\log$.
Primary sequence $=$ ABD-ok selected windows
(the r443 object), now including live $k=8$.

\begin{prop}[Slice with live $k=8$]\label{prop:aic}
On $k=5,6,7,8,9$, $M_1$ wins for $\delta$ with
$\delta_{\infty}=+0.02741$
($\Delta\mathrm{AIC}$ vs $M_2$ is $5.11$).
Without $k=8$ this was $+0.02670$ / $6.01$.
Live $k=8$ sits on the curve and does not flip
the slice verdict.
On the full ABD-ok $k=3..9$, $M_3$ still wins
(the $k=5$ bump $0.109$).
Against $N$, $M_2$ wins: $N$ is not monotone
(r427, illegal abscissa).
Drop-$k=5$ flips to $M_2$; drop-$k=9$ keeps $M_1$
but jumps $\delta_{\infty}$ to $0.039$.
\end{prop}

$k=10/11/12$ do not extend the ABD-ok sequence,
so they do not decide floor vs decay.
A formula-literal $k=10$ point at $\delta=4\cdot 10^{-4}$
would drive $M_1$ onto the grid floor $-0.02$;
that is a sign-flip artefact, not a Selected datum.

The liminf itself stays open.
If a floor $\delta_{\infty}>0$ exists then
eventually $\qd\le 1-\delta_{\infty}$ and
$\kapd=0$ by r442 --- tautological, not a proof.
