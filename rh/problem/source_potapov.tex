\begin{abstract}\noindent
The reviewer architecture (DCCXCVII) asks for a
source-pure rational transfer
$\Theta_k=\Theta_\infty\redh\prod_{p\le 2^{2k}}\Theta_{p,k}$
whose finite Pick matrix on the canonical nodes is
congruent to $I-\Tfrak_0^*\Tfrak_0$ (the r409 graph
resolvent).
Each prime-power block is a finite $J$-factor built
only from $(p,r,\log p,\Lambda=\log p$, Fold, Tent,
Arch, Border); the constructor may not read eigenvalues
of $C$, the SVD of $\Tfrak_0$, target Schur complements,
or $Z_{\mathrm{loc}}$.
The unipotent Redheffer product of point-mass $2\times 2$
factors \emph{is} the Cauchy $m$-function of the
$X$-measure (exact over $\mathbb{Q}$), and Lagrange $S_0$
at interpolating depth $d_0=|Y|$ is the Cauchy--Pick
kernel of the $\pi_Y(X)^2$-dressed $X$-measure (exact).
That Pick kernel is positive definite, while
$I-\Tfrak_0^*\Tfrak_0$ has one negative square on every
one-defect window: Gate~3 fails.
The one negative square is the interpolant residual
$D_0=W_Y-S_0$, not an Euler factor.
No RH claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{The factor}\label{sec:def}

Let $X$ be the occupied dual nodes of a window, $Y$ the
holes, $u^\vee$ the r356 dual weights, and
$d_0=N_w-3$ the interpolating depth of $\Tfrak_0$ (r409).
A source atom $n=p^r$ contributes a unipotent $2\times 2$
\begin{equation}\label{eq:E}
E(z;x,w)
=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\ w/(x-z)&1\end{pmatrix},
\end{equation}
with $x$ the folded position of $r\log p$ and $w$ the
corresponding dual (or $\Lambda$) weight.
The prime block is the Redheffer product (ordinary
$2\times 2$ multiplication)
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Thetap}
\Theta_{p,k}(z)
=\prod_{r:\,p^r\le 2^{2k}} E\bigl(z;x_{p^r},w_{p^r}\bigr),
\end{equation}
and $\Theta_\infty$ is the same factor for the Arch /
completion atom.
The matrices $E$ commute:
$E(z;x,w)\,E(z;x',w')=E(z;x',w')\,E(z;x,w)$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sum}
\Theta_\infty\redh\prod_p\Theta_{p,k}
=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\ m_X(z)&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
m_X(z)=\sum_{a\in X}\frac{w_a}{x_a-z}.
\end{equation}
The finite Pick matrix of this $\Theta$ on the $Y$-nodes
is the Cauchy kernel
\begin{equation}\label{eq:PX}
(P_X)_{ij}
=\sum_{a\in X}\frac{w_a}{(x_a-y_i)(x_a-y_j)}.
\end{equation}

\section{Exact identities over $\mathbb{Q}$}\label{sec:q}

The r409 five-atom toy, labelled by primes
$2,2^2,3,5$ and an Arch atom, is the $\mathbb{Q}$-test.

\begin{lemma}[Redheffer $=$ Cauchy]\label{lem:redh}
On the toy, the product~\eqref{eq:sum} equals $m_X$
exactly.
At $z=7/3$ both sides equal $-7471/22950$.
The factors of $p=2$ and $\infty$ (the $X$-atoms)
already exhaust the product; $p=3,5$ sit on $Y$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Cauchy--pi formula for Lagrange $S_0$]\label{lem:pi}
At interpolating depth $d_0=|Y|$ (unique Lagrange
interpolant, kernel dimension $0$),
\[
S_0
=\Delta^{-1}\,C\,\Pi\,W_X\,\Pi\,C^T\,\Delta^{-1},
\]
where $C_{ia}=1/(x_a-y_i)$, $\Pi=\mathrm{diag}(\pi_Y(x_a))$,
$\pi_Y(x)=\prod_k(x-y_k)$, and
$\Delta=\mathrm{diag}(\pi_Y'(y_j))$.
Equivalently $S_0$ is the Cauchy--Pick kernel of the
$\pi_Y(X)^2$-dressed $X$-measure, conjugated by $\Delta$.
The identity is exact over $\mathbb{Q}$.
The graph defect is the residual
$D_0=W_Y-S_0$, congruent to $I-\Tfrak_0^*\Tfrak_0$.
\end{lemma}

\section{Gate 3}\label{sec:gate3}

\begin{prop}[Pick of the product is not the graph kernel]\label{prop:gate3}
On the $d_0=|Y|$ slice of the toy, $D_0$ has inertia
$(1,1,0)$ (the $(2,2)$ entry equals $-1744/2025$), while
$P_X$ has inertia $(2,0,0)$.
The two kernels are not congruent.
On flagship MAIN ($k_z=9$): $\|\Tfrak_0\|=1.08014$,
$\indm(I-\Tfrak_0^*\Tfrak_0)=1$, while $P_X$ is positive
definite ($\lambda_{\min}(P_X)=8.677$).
The same mismatch holds at $k_z=15$
($\|\Tfrak_0\|=1.10672$) and $k_z=18$
($\|\Tfrak_0\|=1.24966$).
\end{prop}

\begin{remark}
A positive $X$-measure produces a Herglotz $m_X$ and a
positive Pick kernel, independently of how the masses are
grouped by primes.
The one negative square of GraphResolvent is the
\emph{interpolant} residual $D_0=W_Y-S_0$ in $P_{<d_0}$,
which mixes all primes through the polynomial space.
No local Euler factor sees it.
\end{remark}

\section{Index anatomy}\label{sec:anatomy}

\begin{prop}[the one square is not a prime prefix]\label{prop:anatomy}
Growing-$Y$ Uvarov innovations of $\Tfrak_0$, taken in
source order (holes attributed to the nearest prime power,
then $p$ ascending, $r$ within), have \emph{zero} negative
pivots on $w_9$ (minimum $3.25\cdot 10^{-7}$).
The r409 position-order chain has one negative pivot at
index $36$: the sign of a sequential Uvarov pivot is
order-dependent and does not localise the defect to a
prime block.
Lag-local interpolants $\mathrm{fold}(c^{P}_p)$ (the
tent deposits of a single prime, same $X/Y$ mask) have
$\indm=18,19,15$ for $p=2,3,5$, versus $\indm=1$ for the
full operator --- the source product is \emph{not} a
simpler index structure (stop rule~3 of DCCXCVII).
\end{prop}

Permute of $\Lambda$-weights on the fixed mask sends
$\indm(I-\Tfrak^*\Tfrak)$ from $1$ to $20$; Gaussian
jitter to $19$; scramble to $21$.
The Euler product remains of index $0$ (positive masses).
The r289 rational twin keeps $\indm=1$ and
$\|\Tfrak_0\|$ bit-near $1.08014$.

\section{What is a theorem, what is closed}\label{sec:honest}

The source Potapov \emph{product} as a factorisation of
$m_X$ is a theorem (Lemmas~\ref{lem:redh}--\ref{lem:pi}).
As a factorisation of GraphResolvent it is closed:
Gate~3 fails on every one-defect window tested, the
constructor is source-pure (AST: no eig$(C)$, no SVD of
$\Tfrak_0$, no $Z_{\mathrm{loc}}$), and the candidate
index is simpler than the operator only because it is
the wrong object.
R432 (Krein--Langer index one of $\Theta$) is blocked by
the contract (requires \texttt{FACTOR\_EXACT}).
P1 stays $\lambda_2(C)\ge 1$ on the dual quotient.
No RH claim.
