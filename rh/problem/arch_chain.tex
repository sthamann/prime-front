\section{The weight}\label{sec:w}

The discrete ARCH fold of a window
$(\alpha,M,L=2M-2,\Delta=2\alpha/M)$ is
\begin{align}
m_j
&=\sum_{k=1}^{L/2} w_L(\theta_k)\,T_j(\cos\theta_k),
\qquad
\theta_k=\frac{2\pi k}{L},\label{eq:m}\\
w_L(\theta)
&=\frac{2}{L}\,(1-\cos\theta)\,f_A(\theta),\label{eq:w}\\
f_A(\theta)
&=A(0)+2\sum_{p=1}^{M-2}A(p\Delta)\cos(p\theta)
 +A((M-1)\Delta)\cos((M-1)\theta).\label{eq:f}
\end{align}
$A(s)$ is the tent-smoothed archimedean
kernel of the explicit formula
(\texttt{arch\_A\_far} / \texttt{arch\_A\_near}):
an explicit Gauss--Legendre integral of
$e^{-w/2}/(1-e^{-2w})$ against $t_\Delta$.
The Nyquist point $k=L/2$ carries the
usual half-weight.

The factor $(1-\cos\theta)=1-x$ is a
Jacobi $(+\tfrac12,-\tfrac12)$ shift of
Chebyshev $d\theta$.
That candidate is \emph{refuted}:
$\int_0^\pi(1-\cos\theta)\cos(j\theta)\,d\theta=0$
for every $j\ge 2$, but
$m_2=0.1698990367924736\neq 0$.
$f_A$ is a genuine multiplier.
For each finite window $f_A$ is a
trigonometric polynomial, so $w_L$ is a
polynomial times the Chebyshev weight ---
Christoffel-modified Chebyshev, not a
named $(\alpha,\beta)$ family, and
$(a_n,b_n)$ are not closed in $A$.

$f_A$ changes sign (13 negative DFT modes
on kz$17$, 32 on kz$500$).
The negative fold mass is
$2\cdot10^{-4}$ (kz$17$) and
$<10^{-10}$ (kz$500$).
The $\mu$-chain of the program uses only
the positive atoms: $h_j>0$ is the
classical fact that a positive discrete
measure with $N$ atoms has $h_j>0$ for
$j<N$.

\section{The three-lag identity}\label{sec:lag}

\begin{lemma}[three-lag form]\label{lem:lag}
For the fold~\eqref{eq:m}--\eqref{eq:f},
\[
m_0=c_0-c_1,
\qquad
m_j
=-\tfrac12\,c_{j-1}+c_j-\tfrac12\,c_{j+1}
\quad(j\ge 1),
\]
to residual $<10^{-14}$ (kz$17$ ARCH,
$j\le 8$).
\end{lemma}

This is discrete orthogonality of
$\{1,\cos p\theta\}$ on the circulant
grid against $(1-\cos\theta)\cos(j\theta)$.
Every folded moment sees three lags only.

\section{$[L1]$ is elementary}\label{sec:L1}

Write $c=c^A+c^P$ for the MAIN lags.
Linearity of the fold gives
$m^{\mathrm{MAIN}}-m^{\mathrm{ARCH}}$
as the three-lag form in $c^P$.
The lag $c^P_i$ is the tent of width
$\Delta$ centred at $i\Delta$, applied
to $-\Lambda(n)n^{-1/2}$.
Its support starts at the smallest
$u=\log n$ with $\Lambda(n)\neq 0$,
i.e.\ $n=2$.

\begin{lemma}[{$[L1]$, elementary}]\label{lem:L1}
$c^P_i=0$ whenever $(i+1)\Delta<\log 2$.
Hence
\[
m_j^{\mathrm{MAIN}}
=m_j^{\mathrm{ARCH}}
\qquad\text{for all }
j\le J_P-2,
\quad
J_P=\bigl\lceil\log 2/\Delta-1\bigr\rceil.
\]
On the windows
$\{17,116,136,197,230,500\}$ one has
$n_{\mathrm{stab}}=J_P-1$ exactly.
Sister / oversampled windows have a
\emph{neighbour} $n_{\mathrm{stab}}<J_P$
(conservative).
In particular: for each fixed $n$, every
window with $\Delta<\log 2/(n+2)$
satisfies $m_j^{\mathrm{MAIN}}=m_j^{\mathrm{ARCH}}$
for all $j\le n$.
\end{lemma}

\textbf{Circularity audit.}
The only arithmetic input is
$\Lambda(n)=0$ for $n<2$ (definition of
$\Lambda$) plus the fold identity.
PNT is not used.
A zero-free region is not used.
RH is not used.
The identity is stronger than PNT:
$|m^{\mathrm{PNT}}-m^{\mathrm{ARCH}}|[2]$
is $6\cdot10^{-6}$ (kz$17$) down to
$10^{-10}$, while
$|m^{\mathrm{MAIN}}-m^{\mathrm{ARCH}}|[2]$
is $10^{-16}$.
The r452 neighbour rate $a^{-1.9}$ is
PNT-carried (MAIN and PNT-world neighbour
exponents $2.94$ and $2.95$) and is
\emph{not} load-bearing for eventually.
Verdict \texttt{L1\_PNT\_SUFFICIENT}
(surplus strength: elementary).
\textbf{Not \texttt{RH\_CIRCULAR}.}

The error formula, for the record:
\begin{align*}
m_j^{\mathrm{MAIN}}-m_j^{\mathrm{ARCH}}
&=\bigl(-\tfrac12 c^P_{j-1}+c^P_j-\tfrac12 c^P_{j+1}\bigr),\\
c^P_i
&=-\sum_n\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\,t_\Delta(i\Delta-\log n)
  +(\text{reflection if }\log n<\Delta).
\end{align*}
PNT replaces the sum by
$\int e^{u/2}t_\Delta(i\Delta-u)\,du$;
that is a different classical object,
not the intermediary of $[L1]$.

\section{$[L3]$ as a prefix SATZ}\label{sec:L3}

$\rho_n=f_{b,n}^2/\eta_n$ with
$f_{b,n}=\int p_n\,d(\mathrm{border})$
and $\eta_n=\int p_n^2\,d\sigma$
(r243 / r453).

\begin{lemma}[identical measures]\label{lem:sympy}
If the border signed measure equals
$d\sigma$, then $f_{b,0}=\int d\sigma$
and $f_{b,n}=0$ for every $n\ge 1$
(orthogonality of the OP system).
Verified in exact rationals on a
four-atom toy ($\rho_0=1$, $f_{b,1}=0$,
$\rho_1=0$).
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[shared moments]\label{lem:share}
If border and ARCH agree as functionals
on $\mathbb{P}_J$, then
$f_{b,n}=0$ for $1\le n<J$, hence
$\rho_n=0$.
\end{lemma}

The sealed border is the same fold
applied to ARCH plus
$\mathtt{PB.smooth\_comb}$, a Riemann
sum of $2e^{u/2}$ starting at
$\mathtt{BG\_DU}=0.01$
(not at $u=0$).
The extra lags begin at
$p_B\sim\mathtt{BG\_DU}/\Delta$.

\begin{lemma}[{$[L3]$, eventual per $n$}]\label{lem:L3}
On kz$69,137,500$ the first
$10^{-12}$-break between border and ARCH
moments is at $J_B\in\{9,11,21\}$,
matching $\lfloor\mathtt{BG\_DU}/\Delta-1\rfloor$.
Thus $\rho_n=0$ for $1\le n<J_B$.
The case $n=0$ is the mass exception
$\rho_0=M_\partial$.
The $\mu$-chain satisfies $h_j>0$ for
$j<N_\mu$.
For each fixed $n\ge 1$, every window
with $\Delta<\mathtt{BG\_DU}/(n+2)$
has $h$-prefix positivity through $n$
and $\rho_n=0<5/7$.
\end{lemma}

There is no single infinite Jacobi
chain of a limit measure:
$m_0$ and $m_1$ grow with the window
(r454).
The program never needed that object ---
it needs the property at each fixed $n$.
