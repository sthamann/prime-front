\begin{abstract}\noindent
Round 381 reduced $G_\varepsilon$ to the triple
$G_\varepsilon^\mu\wedge C_\varepsilon\wedge R_2$.
Rounds 385--389 closed the signed half as a cancellation object.
This note attacks the remaining positive sibling:
the last twelve monic Jacobi coefficients of the $\mu$-chain
satisfy $\lvert\gamma_k-\tfrac14\rvert\le\tfrac1{16}$ and
$\lvert\log(\gamma_{k+1}/\gamma_k)\rvert\le\tfrac25$.
What is proved: the Jacobi-$(0,1)$ identity (r379);
Chebyshev-$U$ quarters $\gamma_k=\tfrac14$; homogeneity of monic
$\gamma$; and that Fej\'er weights on the \emph{full} cosine grid
are a discrete Bernstein--Szeg\H{o} measure
($\max_{k\ge 2}\lvert\gamma_k-\tfrac14\rvert=1.8\cdot10^{-6}$ on
FRAME-A $w=9$).
What is \emph{not} proved: the box.
Occupied-only Fej\'er (the $\nu$-complement deleted) already lies
in $\lvert\gamma-\tfrac14\rvert$ (core-$42$ max $0.04892$), with jump
margin $0.0058$ against $\tfrac25$.
The $d_{\mathrm{arm}}$ multiplier pulls \emph{in} on $40/42$ and
is load-bearing for jump headroom.
A ratio bound $\kappa=\sup(1+\eps)/\inf(1+\eps)=4200$ is true and
useless.
A permutation of the same positive weights on the same nodes
leaves the box on $17/20$ seeds: $G_\varepsilon^\mu$ is not
construction-pure.
The hydra-legal remainder is the pair $(F_\eps,W_\eps)$:
Fej\'er-on-occupied (node deletion from Bernstein--Szeg\H{o}),
and the r342 digamma/tent multiplier versus scramble-of-weights.
No RH claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Recollection}\label{sec:rec}

$G_\varepsilon^\mu$ is the positive sibling of $G_\varepsilon$
in~\cite{geps}: writing $n=N-2$ for the $v_2$-depth, the last
twelve monic Jacobi coefficients of the $\mu$-chain (occupied
cosine nodes, weights Fej\'er $\times d_{\mathrm{arm}}$) satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gepsmu}
\bigl\lvert\gamma_k-\tfrac14\bigr\rvert\le\tfrac1{16},
\qquad
\bigl\lvert\log(\gamma_{k+1}/\gamma_k)\bigr\rvert\le\tfrac25,
\qquad
k\in[n-12,n).
\end{equation}
The mesh $x=\cos(2\pi k/L)$, $L=4h-2$, is a construction identity
of~\cite{v3p}.
The source weights follow the r342 dictionary
$F_A(\xi)=-\log\pi+\Re\psi(\tfrac14+i\xi/2)$ plus the two-cell tent
of the prime layer, $v_{\mathrm{pred}}<10^{-4}$.
There is no signed cancellation on this chain: the four dead
majorant routes of the evening do not apply.
The r381 measurement on FRAME-A $w=9$ is last-twelve $0.02605$,
jump $0.1224$.

\section{The Fej\'er basis}\label{sec:fejer}

\begin{lemma}[Jacobi-$(0,1)$]\label{lem:jac}
For the continuous weight $(1-x)$ on $[-1,1]$,
\[
\gamma_n=\frac{n(n+1)}{(2n+1)^2},
\qquad
\bigl\lvert\gamma_n-\tfrac14\bigr\rvert
=\frac{1}{4(2n+1)^2}.
\]
Exact over $\Q$ for $n=1,\ldots,79$.
\end{lemma}

This is the r379 identity.  It is \emph{not} the discrete Fej\'er
chain on the cosine grid: that grid with weights $1-x$ samples a
Bernstein--Szeg\H{o} measure (Chebyshev-$U$ class), not Gauss--Jacobi
nodes of $(0,1)$.

\begin{lemma}[Chebyshev-$U$ quarters]\label{lem:U}
The monic Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind satisfy
$p_2=x^2-\tfrac14$ and $\gamma_k=\tfrac14$ for every $k\ge 1$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$U_0=1$, $U_1=2x$, $U_2=4x^2-1$.  The monic forms are
$p_0=1$, $p_1=x$, $p_2=x^2-\tfrac14=x\,p_1-\tfrac14\,p_0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[homogeneity]\label{lem:hom}
Monic Jacobi coefficients are invariant under $w\mapsto c\,w$
for $c>0$.  Exact over $\Q$ on a five-atom toy.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[full-grid Fej\'er]\label{lem:bs}
On the unique cosine folds $x=\cos(2\pi k/L)$, $k=1,\ldots,L/2$,
with Fej\'er weights $1-x$, the monic Stieltjes coefficients of
FRAME-A $w=9$ ($S=367=S_++S_-$, depth $182$) satisfy
$\max_{k\ge 2}\lvert\gamma_k-\tfrac14\rvert=1.84\cdot10^{-6}$.
The union of $\mu$ and $\nu$ atoms \emph{is} this grid.
\end{lemma}

Occupied-only Fej\'er is therefore a \emph{node deletion}: the
$\nu$-complement is removed from a Bernstein--Szeg\H{o} discrete
measure.  The r381 figure $0.071$ is signed Fej\'er
($\mu-\nu$ with Fej\'er weights on both sides) and lives in
$C_\varepsilon$, not here.

\begin{lemma}[jump not implied]\label{lem:jump}
$\log\tfrac53>\tfrac25$.  The jump half of~\eqref{eq:gepsmu}
is not a corollary of the $\lvert\gamma-\tfrac14\rvert$ box.
\end{lemma}

\section{Positive perturbation}\label{sec:pert}

Write $w=w_{\mathrm{Fejer}}\cdot(1+\eps)$ on the occupied nodes,
with $\eps$ the Fej\'er-stripped $d_{\mathrm{arm}}$ (r342 profile).

\begin{lemma}[ratio bound, useless]\label{lem:ratio}
If $m\le 1+\eps\le M$ pointwise then
$(m/M)\gamma_k(w_{\mathrm{Fejer}})\le\gamma_k(w)\le(M/m)\gamma_k(w_{\mathrm{Fejer}})$.
On FRAME-A $w=9$, $\kappa=M/m=4200.6$
($\lVert\eps\rVert_\infty=5.166$).
The image of the occupied-Fej\'er last twelve under this bound
is $[10^{-4},1163]$, which misses $[\tfrac3{16},\tfrac5{16}]$.
\end{lemma}

The bound is legitimate --- everything is positive --- and too
crude.  Last-twelve-relevant mass (nodes carrying $90\%$ of
$p_{n-6}^2 w$) still sees $\kappa=4200$.
Nevai--Blumenthal / Rakhmanov stability under a relative weight
bound on a fixed infinite support is an $o(1)$ statement as
$k\to\infty$; here $k\sim n=N-2$ is half-filling depth on a
\emph{finite} atomic subset, and no rate yields $\tfrac1{16}$.

Directed measurement, not a bound: at $w=9$,
$\lvert\gamma(\mu)-\gamma(\mathrm{Fejer})\rvert_{12}=0.030$
and $\lvert\gamma(\mu)-\tfrac14\rvert_{12}=0.026<0.036=\lvert\gamma(\mathrm{Fejer})-\tfrac14\rvert_{12}$.
The $d_{\mathrm{arm}}$ multiplier pulls \emph{in}.

\section{Census and cofinality}\label{sec:census}

\begin{prop}[core-$42$ census]\label{prop:census}
On the $42$ admissible MAIN windows, last-twelve of the
$\mu$-chain and of occupied-Fej\'er both satisfy~\eqref{eq:gepsmu}:
$\mu$ max $0.03304$ (kz~$14$), max jump $0.2469$;
occupied-Fej\'er max $0.04892$ (kz~$55$), max jump $0.3942$.
The $d_{\mathrm{arm}}$ multiplier pulls toward $\tfrac14$ on
$40/42$.
Builder fallback: core-$42$ only (MAIN-$85$ aborted above two
minutes on EXT windows).
\end{prop}

There is no $m_4$.  The smallest core window ($n=140$) already
lies in the box; depth improves the numbers (kz~$82$, $n=532$:
$0.0085$) but is not a threshold.  The box on this family is a
sealed census, not a cofinal theorem.

The jump half of occupied-Fej\'er is razor-thin (margin $0.0058$
against $\tfrac25$).  That is where $d_{\mathrm{arm}}$ is
load-bearing: it supplies jump headroom the Fej\'er-on-occupied
chain barely has.

\section{Adversaries}\label{sec:adv}

\paragraph{Permutation on the same nodes.}
Twenty independent shuffles of the $w=9$ $\mu$-weights on the
fixed occupied cosine nodes: $17/20$ leave~\eqref{eq:gepsmu}
(worst last-twelve $0.101$, jump $0.621$).
Seed $3$ is the named kill ($0.07445$, jump $0.459$).
Seed $1$ happened to stay in --- a single permutation is not a
theorem of positivity plus the grid.

\paragraph{Scramble-seed $\mu$.}
Window scramble seed $1$ (occupation \emph{and} weights move):
$\mu$-only last-twelve $0.07407$, jump $0.3518$, out of the box.
The node set selected by $\mathrm{sign}(d_{\mathrm{arm}})$ is
part of the construction; scrambling it is a different support.

\paragraph{Two-period weight.}
On occupied Fej\'er, the multiplier $1+a(-1)^i$ with
$0\le a\le 0.95$ stays in the box ($a=0.95$: last-twelve $0.0497$,
jump $0.385$).
Smooth oscillation of the weight is not the kill; spatial
scramble of the $d_{\mathrm{arm}}$ multiset is.

\paragraph{Mutants.}
The $\lvert\gamma-\tfrac14\rvert$ half at $w=9$ still holds
against $\tfrac1{32}$ ($0.02605<0.03125$).
The jump half fails against $\tfrac1{10}$ ($0.1224>0.10$).
Tightening the jump bar to $0.39$ would fail occupied-Fej\'er
on kz~$55$.

\paragraph{Equal weights, power weights.}
Equal weights on the occupied nodes, and weights $(1-x)^p$ for
$p\in\{-0.4,0,0.5,1,2,4\}$, all stay in the box at $w=9$.
Regular profiles compatible with Fej\'er do not kill;
the r342 assignment of which mass sits on which fold does.

\section{What remains}\label{sec:rest}

\begin{prop}[reduction]\label{prop:red}
$G_\varepsilon^\mu$ reduces to the pair $(F_\eps,W_\eps)$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $F_\eps$: last-twelve box~\eqref{eq:gepsmu} for
Fej\'er weights on the occupied cosine subset (node deletion
of the $\nu$-complement from Lemma~\ref{lem:bs}; equivalently
the F1-bounded $\nu$-runs of~\cite{pivot} acting on
Bernstein--Szeg\H{o} discrete).
\item $W_\eps$: the r342 digamma/tent multiplier does not kick
that chain out of~\eqref{eq:gepsmu} (permutation of the same
masses on the same nodes is a named counterexample to any
claim that positivity plus the grid suffice).
\end{itemize}
Both are strictly smaller than $G_\varepsilon^\mu$:
$F_\eps$ freezes the weights, $W_\eps$ is a multiplicative
perturbation of a named reference.
$G_\varepsilon^\mu$ is not construction-pure.
The arithmetic content of the $G_\varepsilon$ triple that lives
in the \emph{weights} (not the nodes) is $W_\eps$ here and
$C_\varepsilon$ on the signed side.
The chain
\[
T_2'
 \;\Longleftarrow\;
 \cdots
 \;\Longleftarrow\;
 G_\varepsilon
 \;\Longleftarrow\;
 G_\varepsilon^\mu\,\wedge\,C_\varepsilon\,\wedge\,R_2
 \;\Longleftarrow\;
 F_\eps\,\wedge\,W_\eps\,\wedge\,C_\varepsilon\,\wedge\,R_2
\]
stands.  No $m_4$.  No RH claim.
\end{prop}

A Geronimus/Szeg\H{o} bound that would absorb $W_\eps$ into
$F_\eps$ is exactly the ratio majorant of Lemma~\ref{lem:ratio},
and it does not close.
T1 / COMPOSE unchanged.
