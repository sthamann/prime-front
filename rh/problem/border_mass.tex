\section{The exact pack identities}\label{sec:id}

Write $S_k=\sum_{i\le k}\rho_i$ and
$B_w=S_{n-2}+5/7$, as in the r362/r369 pack.

\begin{lemma}[Schur mass identity]\label{lem:id}
On every measured depth of kz$5,17,116,136,137,230$,
\[
q_n^{\dagger}
=\frac{S_{n-1}}{B_w},
\qquad
\delta_n
=1-q_n^{\dagger}
=\frac{5/7-\rho_n}{B_w},
\]
to residual at most $5\cdot10^{-14}$
(Gram residual $\le10^{-15}$).
\end{lemma}

These are the r362 telescope
$D=B-\sum\rho$ evaluated at prefix depth:
$b^\top(I-C)^{-1}b=S_{n-1}$.

\begin{thm}[$g$-bound]\label{thm:g}
$q_n^{\dagger}<1$ if and only if
$\rho_n<5/7$.
Whenever $B_w$ is still the plateau value
$B_w^*=M_\partial+5/7$
(the range $n\le n_{\mathrm{stab}}$ and the
first leak),
\[
q_n^{\dagger}-q_*
=\frac{\rho_n}{B_w}
=:g(\rho_n).
\]
On the plateau $\rho_n\approx 0$, so
$g=0$ and $q_n^{\dagger}\equiv q_*$.
\end{thm}

kz$136$ at $n=176$:
$\rho=0.02540$, $B_w=5.5337$,
$g=0.00459=q_{176}-q_*$ (CLIFF).
kz$137$ at $n=176$:
$\rho=3.08\cdot10^{-10}$, $g\sim0$
(CONTINUES).
kz$17$ at $n=19$:
$\rho=0.1721$, $g=0.05164=q_{19}-q_*$.

The needed mincut comparison
$\mathrm{err}(\rho)\le(5/7)/B_w^*$
is exactly $\rho_n\le 5/7$ on a frozen
$B_w$.

\section{What $M_\partial$ is}\label{sec:md}

\begin{prop}[Folded $m_0$]\label{prop:md}
$M_\partial$ is the signed mass of the
smooth-border fold.
On every $k_z\ge 69$,
$M_\partial=\mu_0-\nu_0=m_0(\tilde\mu-\tilde\nu)$
of the MAIN fold (equality to $10^{-14}$).
Source-pure,
\[
m_0
=\sum_{j=1}^{L/2}
\frac{2}{L}\,(1-\cos\theta_j)\,d_j
\]
with the $j=L/2$ half-weight
($\theta_j=2\pi j/L$, $d$ the window
spectral density).
This is \emph{not} a raw
$\psi$-difference of the window edges
(those are size $\sim a^2$).
\end{prop}

The sister pair has almost the same $a$
($631$ vs $641$) and
$M_\partial=4.817$ vs $6.436$.
The source split is the window volume:
$N=1641$ vs $8300$, $L=6562$ vs $33198$.
$\mathrm{corr}(M_\partial,\log N)=0.992$.
A linear fit $M_\partial=-0.81+0.79\log N$
has rms $0.17$; drop-$500$ is stable.
The sample is growing (no ceiling at
kz$500$, $M_\partial=6.98$).
Honest growth law: \texttt{GROWING},
source-identified (\texttt{CLASSICAL\_REDUCED}),
not a PNT theorem.
