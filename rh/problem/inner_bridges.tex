\section{Finite PSD bridge}

The exact proved Lean theorem is:
\begin{quote}
\code{selectedACapPsdImpliesPlainReads_of_representation}\\
\code{(hrep : SelectedReadQuadraticRepresentation) :}\\
\code{SelectedACapPsdImpliesPlainReads}.
\end{quote}
Its proof is the finite implication
\[
 A_{\mathrm{cap}}\succeq0,\qquad
 \operatorname{fullRead}(f)=z^{\mathsf T}A_{\mathrm{cap}}z
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \operatorname{fullRead}(f)\geq0 .
\]
The sole remaining channel proposition is
\code{SelectedReadQuadraticRepresentation}.  It asks for the actual
augmented direction \(z\), including Fourier/Chebyshev coordinates,
whitening, border coordinate, and pole correction.

The old target has not been weakened.  In particular, it quantifies over
all \code{GridElement}s satisfying only
\code{f.meshExp <= selectedMesh k}.  Their \code{steps} field is unbounded,
whereas \(A_{\mathrm{cap}}\) is finite-dimensional.  At \(k=5\), the probe
records the admissible arithmetic witness
\[
 \operatorname{meshExp}=19,\qquad \operatorname{steps}=11,\qquad
 \operatorname{cap}=10.
\]
This is a quantifier obstruction to a degree-\(<\!\operatorname{cap}\)
argument, not a numerical counterexample to positivity.

\section{Archimedean bridge}

\code{productionArchLag} is now a concrete Lean definition.  For \(i=0\)
it is the Euler--\(\log\pi\) regularized near-cell integral
\code{productionArchLagNear}; for \(i\geq1\) it is the ordinary tent
integral \code{productionArchLagFar}.  The definitions transcribe Python
\code{arch_A_near}, \code{arch_A_far}, and \code{arch_lags}.

The former generic stabilization hole is replaced one-for-one by the
single named theorem
\begin{quote}
\code{arch_gauss_mellin_digamma_identity :}\\
\code{ArchGaussMellinDigammaIdentity}.
\end{quote}
The existing theorem \code{arch_elementwise_stabilization} is now a direct
application of this identity.  Mathlib 4.29.1 contains the digamma
functional equation and special values, but its source explicitly lists
Gauss's integral representation as a TODO; the needed Mellin
identification is therefore not available from the imported API.
