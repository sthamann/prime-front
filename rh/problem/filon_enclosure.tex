\begin{abstract}\noindent
The r481 remainder was the pair of integrals
$\int_0^T\sigma_A\hat\psi_i\hat\psi_j$ and
$\int_T^\infty\sigma_A\hat\psi_i\hat\psi_j$
in the refined even Schur
$S=B-CC^\top/c_{\mathrm{tail}}$.
This round encloses both.
The Nystr\"om sample Fourier transform is an explicit
exponential sum; panel-linear Filon with a split
$M_2$ remainder seals every $[0,T]$ entry
(midpoints hit the r480/r481 trapezoid to $10^{-7}$).
The $[T,\infty)$ tail of the exponential sum is
almost-periodic and is \emph{not} used
(Ci-sum to infinity yields $\lambda_{\min}(S)\sim-1.6$,
killed).
The decaying interpolant Fourier transform is the
four-term endpoint integration-by-parts of the sinc
extension; diagonal tails are positive and booked to $B$
($A_+$ side, sign bookkeeping explicit); off-diagonal
tails are kept as signed values.
Higham on the finite even $3\times3$ Schur with entry
radius $10^{-4}$ gives $\mu\ge1.19\cdot10^{-3}$
(odd $\mu\ge0.103$).
A would-be
$c_*=\mu/(1+\|C\|^2/c_{\mathrm{tail}}^2)\approx1.68\cdot10^{-4}$
exists \emph{if} $C$ were the full operator coupling.
It is not.
Leftover $\|P_{\mathrm{block}}A P_{\ge12}\|_F=2.76\cdot10^{-2}$
on $[0,T]$ (modes $12,14$) against an IBP-inflated budget
$\|C_{\mathrm{rest}}\|<2.4\cdot10^{-4}$.
Deficit factor $\sim115$.
Verdict
\texttt{REDUCED(finite-S-interval-PD; leftover-C-HS=2.74e-2-vs-budget-2.4e-4)}.
Fourier--HS is not reused.
Yoshida--Bombieri zone.  No RH claim.

\section{Convention}

$\mathrm{supp}\,h\subset[-L,L]$, $g=h*\widetilde h$,
$\QW=A-P+\Pi$, prime nodes $n\le e^{2L}$.
At $L=0.3$ one has $P\equiv0$ (scramble-sensitive,
vacuous).
Plancherel $\hat h(t)=\int h(x)e^{itx}\,dx$,
$\|h\|_2^2=(2\pi)^{-1}\int|\hat h|^2$,
$A=(2\pi)^{-1}\int\sigma_A|\hat h|^2$ with
$\sigma_A(t)=\mathrm{Re}\,\psi(\tfrac14+it/2)-\log\pi$.
The r469/r479/r480 contract is in force:
$t_*=8$, $\varepsilon=\lambda_6(K_8)\le1.46\cdot10^{-8}$,

\section{Class 1: Filon on $[0,T]$}

The Nystr\"om interpolant sampled at Gauss--Legendre
$n=48$ has Fourier transform
\[
 \hat\psi_i(t)=\sum_{q=1}^{48} w_q\psi_i(x_q)\,e^{itx_q},
\]
an exponential sum.  No $x$-quadrature remainder attaches
to this discrete object.
Write $\alpha_{iq}=w_q\psi_i(x_q)$.  Evenness gives
\[
 A_{ij}[0,T]=\frac1\pi\sum_{q,r}\alpha_{iq}\alpha_{jr}
 J_T(x_q-x_r),\qquad
 J_T(\omega)=\int_0^T\sigma_A(t)\cos(\omega t)\,dt.
\]
Linear interpolation of $\sigma_A$ on panels, with
$|\sigma_A''|\le36.8$ on $(0,8]$ and $|\sigma_A''|\le0.018$
on $[8,150]$ (finite-difference majorants, $15\%$ pad),
has per-band $L^1$ remainder $M_2 h^3/8\cdot N_{\mathrm{pan}}$.
At $N_{\mathrm{pan}}=(8000,8000)$ one has
$\mathrm{rem}_{L^1}\le1.38\cdot10^{-4}$, hence
$|\Delta A|\le(\sum|\alpha_i|)(\sum|\alpha_j|)\,\mathrm{rem}_{L^1}/\pi
\le2.5\cdot10^{-5}$.
Midpoints differ from the r480 trapezoid by
at most $1.72\cdot10^{-7}$ on the even diagonal.

\begin{lemma}[{Filon on the compact interval}]
Every even/odd $A_{ij}[0,150]$ of the Nystr\"om sample
transform is enclosed to radius $2.5\cdot10^{-5}$.
The no-tail even Schur has midpoint
$\lambda_{\min}(S)=5.692\cdot10^{-3}$, matching r480
($5.686\cdot10^{-3}$).
\end{lemma}

\section{Class 2: decaying IBP tail}

\paragraph{Exponential-sum Ci (killed).}
The product $\hat\psi_i\hat\psi_j$ of two exponential
sums is a double trigonometric polynomial with frequencies
$|x_q-x_{q'}|\le2L$.
The $\omega=0$ (diagonal $q=q'$) coefficient is
$\mathrm{DC}_{ij}=\pi^{-1}\sum_q\alpha_{iq}\alpha_{jq}$.
On the diagonal $\mathrm{DC}_{ii}>0$ and
$\int_T^\infty\sigma_A=+\infty$; dropping it would lower
$A_{ii}$ (safe for $B$).
Off-diagonal $\mathrm{DC}_{ij}$ can have either sign
($-1.40\cdot10^{-3}$ for $(2,4)$) and is not safe to drop.
The oscillatory Ci-sum of the exponential sum, DC dropped,
produces $\lambda_{\min}(S)\approx-1.62$.
The exponential sum does not decay; it is not the
$[T,\infty)$ Fourier transform of the interpolant.

\paragraph{Four-term IBP of the interpolant (kept).}
The sinc extension
$\widetilde\psi(x)=\lambda^{-1}\sum_j w_j K(x,x_j)\psi(x_j)$
is entire.  For even $\psi$,
\[
 \hat\psi(t)
 =\sum_{k=0}^{3}
  2\,\psi^{(k)}(L)\,
  \frac{\phi_k(tL)}{t^{k+1}}
 +R_4(t),
\]
with phases $(\sin,\,\cos,\,-\sin,\,-\cos)$ and
$|R_4(t)|\le 2L\|\psi^{(4)}\|_\infty/t^4$.
At $T_{\mathrm{rem}}=800$ the product remainder on
$C_{46}$ is $O(10^{-5})$.
Binet: $\sigma_A(t)=\log(t/(2\pi))-1/(24t^2)+O(t^{-4})$,
sealed by $|t^2\rho|=1/24$ on $[20,800]$.

\paragraph{Sign bookkeeping.}
(i)~Diagonal IBP tails are positive
($\mathrm{diag}\,A_{\mathrm{tail}}
\approx(0.0078,\,0.130,\,0.266,\,0.385,\,0.500)$
even) and are booked to $B$ ($A_+$ enlargement).
(ii)~Off-diagonal IBP tails are included as signed
values, not dropped and not moved into the error.
(iii)~Exponential-sum $\omega=0$ pairs are not
integrated to infinity.
(iv)~Leftover $C$ after $\psi_{10}$ is not moved into
$B$ or into $c_{\mathrm{tail}}$.

\begin{lemma}[Finite IBP Schur]
Adding the four-term IBP tail on modes $0..11$ keeps
\[
 \lambda_{\min}(S_{\mathrm{even}})=5.949\cdot10^{-3},\quad
 \|C\|_{\mathrm{even}}=0.595,
\]
\[
 \lambda_{\min}(S_{\mathrm{odd}})=0.211,\quad
 \|C\|_{\mathrm{odd}}=0.631.
\]
Higham with entry radius $10^{-4}$ yields
$\mu_{\mathrm{even}}\ge1.196\cdot10^{-3}$ and
$\mu_{\mathrm{odd}}\ge0.103$.
\end{lemma}

\section{Scaling at $L=0.8$}

At $L=0.8$ the prime range $n\le e^{2L}$ is nonempty:
$P$ is a cosine sum with literal $\log n$
(scramble-sensitive, live).
The exponential-sum structure of $\hat\psi$ survives;
$\sigma_A-\sigma_P$ is still an explicit combination of
a Digamma symbol and a finite cosine sum, so Filon and
four-term IBP apply verbatim.
What becomes more expensive: more frequencies
$|x_q-x_{q'}|\le2L$, a larger $M_2$ of $\sigma_P$ on
$[0,t_*]$, and a rank-$2$ (not rank-$3$) $P$ after the
r469 pairing.
The leftover-$C$ obstruction is not cured by $L$;
it is a completeness statement about $C$, not about $T$.

\section{What is not claimed}

The finite interval-PD Schur is a lemma, not
$\lambda_*(0.3)\ge c$.
Assembling the SATZ would require
$\|C_{\mathrm{rest}}\|<2.4\cdot10^{-4}$
(or a Loewner substitute that does not inflate $\|C\|$
to $0.60$).
Neither is available.
No $L^*$ claim, no $R^\dagger$ claim, no RH claim,
no anti-RH claim.
