\section{Open problem (Erd\H{o}s form)}

The remainder is a self-contained analytic
question.  It does not mention the Riemann
hypothesis, primes, or the von~Mangoldt comb
($P\equiv0$ at this $L$).

\begin{problem}[Operator residual of the enemy resolvent]
\label{pr:opres}
Bound $\|(1-P)A^+R_s^{-1}\varphi_i\|_{L^2[-L,L]}$
for the certified enemy modes $\varphi_i$ of
$K_{t_c}$ against the Higham/BS margin
$5.6\cdot10^{-3}$.
\end{problem}

\noindent\textbf{Data.}
Let $L=3/10$ and $s=1/20$.
Let $A^+$ be the Fourier multiplier on
$L^2[-L,L]$ with symbol
$\sigma_+(t)=\max\{\sigma_A(t),0\}$,
and $\Pi$ the even/odd rank-one as above.
Let $R_s=A^++\Pi+sI$.
Let $\varphi_0,\varphi_1,\varphi_2$ be the
first three eigenfunctions of $K_{t_c}$
(Slepian numbers
$0.859391$, $0.313778$, $0.027320$;
certified, no garbage).
Let $P$ be the orthogonal projection onto
a finite certified subspace containing
those modes (the $G$-certificate uses $V_5$).
The discrete $G$-certificate on $V_n$ is
already $<1$ (BS $0.99439$ at $N=5$).
The discrete residual in $V_n$ is
$2\cdot10^{-16}$.
The measured operator leftover on the
certificate solves is
$\|(1-P_5)A^+_{[t_c,150]}u_1\|/s=5.86$.

\noindent\textbf{Ask.}
Produce an explicit bound
$\|(1-P)A^+R_s^{-1}\varphi_i\|_2\le\delta s$
with $\delta<5.6\cdot10^{-3}/3$
for each certified $i$,
\emph{or} prove that no such bound exists
for any finite certified $P$ whose
$G$-certificate remains $<1$.
Either statement freezes $\lambda_*(0.3)$.

\noindent\textbf{What is already known, and may be used.}
$R$ is not coercive
($\lambda_{\min}(R)$ collapses with $n$).
The shift $s=0.05$ is mandatory;
$R_s\succeq sI$.
$N=3+|\sigma_A(0)|\cdot\mathrm{rest}/s=1.078$
overshoots and is not a SATZ pad.
Full-space GL leftover of truncated $A^+$
is spectrally small at $n\ge96$, but
those solves are not the certificate.
Certified jets for IBP stop at the first
three Slepians of $K_{t_c}$;
higher Nystr\"om modes are garbage.

\noindent\textbf{What is forbidden as a pad.}
Fourier--HS majorants;
crude $\kappa_{\mathrm{high}}$;
integer Fourier ONB;
$\sigma^2$ Plancherel as $\|Au\|_{[-L,L]}$;
IBP envelope as pad;
exponential-sum Ci to $\infty$;
$n=48$ leftover as SATZ pad;
$20\times$ coupling drop;
QR sinc-IBP of garbage jets;
flat $c_{\mathrm{tail}}I$ as $M_\infty$;
constant minorant $\sigma(0)K+\Pi$;
$K_8$ garbage jets;
$T=400$ quadrature as a tail pad;
$N=3+|\sigma_A(0)|\mathrm{rest}/s$ as SATZ;
$\lambda_{\max}(BG)$ as Birman--Schwinger;
the GL leftover of truncated $A^+$ as if it
were the operator residual of the
$V_5$ certificate;
unshifted $R^{-1}$ as coercive.

\noindent\textbf{Honest type.}
This is an \emph{ideas} problem, not a
remaining arithmetic grind.
The off-space is float-accessible and
$O(1)$.
Filon/IBP and GL spectral convergence
are measured.
What is missing is a handle on
$(1-P)A^+u$ for a $u$ that is
\emph{both} low-mode (so IBP lives)
\emph{and} complete enough that the
leftover sits under $5.6\cdot10^{-3}$.
Those two demands have been in tension
since r483.
No eleventh discrete form is proposed.
