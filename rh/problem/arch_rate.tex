\section{A --- F1(ii) isolated, not closed}

Mathlib v4.29.1 still lists ``Prove Gauss' integral representation''
as a TODO on \texttt{Digamma.lean}.  It supplies
\code{Complex.digamma = logDeriv Gamma}, the recurrence, and the
values at $0,1,1/2$.  There is no \code{Real.digamma} and no
Gauss/Mellin identification.

The r464 exact identity
\begin{quote}
\code{ArchGaussMellinDigammaIdentity}:\\
$\forall f\,\exists a_0\,\forall a\ge a_0\,\forall m\ge\mathrm{meshExp}\,f$,\\
$\mathrm{archRead}(a,m,f)=\mathrm{weilArchSide}\,f$
\end{quote}
cannot close as stated.  Lean now proves
\begin{quote}
\code{productionArchDelta\_tendsto\_atTop}:\\
at each fixed $m$, $\Delta(a,m)=\log a/(m+1)\to+\infty$ as $a\to\infty$.
\end{quote}
The inner quantifier therefore asks for exact tent/integral equality
on meshes that become arbitrarily coarse.  r473 already exhibits a
mesh-and-onset-compatible witness with a strictly positive arch
tent error.  The historical \code{sorry} is retained (the FREQ
extraction still consumes the exact form).  The missing Mathlib
brick is the named Prop
\code{GaussDigammaIntegralRepresentation} --- not a second
\code{sorry}.

\code{weilArchSide} is no longer opaque.  It is the concrete
$u$-space pairing
\[
 A(g)=C_b g(0)+\int_0^b w(u)\bigl(e^{-3u/2}g(0)-g(u)\bigr)\,du,
\]
$w(u)=2e^{-u/2}/(1-e^{-2u})$,
$C_b=-\gamma-\log\pi-\log(1-e^{-2b})$.

\textbf{Status A: IDENTITY\_ISOLATED.}  Census $8\to8$ (honest:
exact equality is the wrong quantifier; Gauss is a Mathlib TODO).

\section{B --- $O(\Delta^2)$ rate at fixed $f$}

Along the selected sequence $\Delta_k=2^{-\lfloor\sqrt k\rfloor}\log2$,
the tent error of the r470 witness is
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{rccc}
\hline
$k$ & $\Delta_k$ & $\mathrm{err}_{\mathrm{arch}}$ & $\mathrm{err}/\Delta^2$\\ \hline
5--8 & $0.1732867951$ & $0.119793278323$ & $3.9893$\\
9--12 & $0.0866433976$ & $0.028876619985$ & $3.8466$\\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Halving $\Delta$ divides the error by $\approx4.15$.  The rate is
$O(\Delta_k^2)$ with witness constant $C\le4$.

Lean names
\begin{quote}
\code{SelectedArchErrorQuadraticRate}:\\
$\forall f\,\exists k_0\,\forall k\ge k_0$,
$\mathrm{err}(k,f)\le \code{archRateConst}\,f\cdot\Delta_k^2$,
\end{quote}
and proves
\begin{quote}
\code{selectedArchError\_tendsto\_zero\_of\_rate}:\\
the named rate plus \code{selectedDelta\_tendsto\_zero} imply
$\mathrm{err}(k,f)\to0$ as $k\to\infty$ at each fixed $f$.
\end{quote}
This is the allowed fixed-$f$ convergence.  It is not positivity
(that would need the cone for infinitely many $k$).

The r473 composition is now quantitative:
$\mathrm{weilForm}(f)\ge-2\,\mathrm{err}(k,f)$ with
$\mathrm{err}(k,f)\le C(f)\Delta_k^2\to0$ at fixed $f$.
\code{SelectedPolynomialApproximatesGrid} is thereby a rate
statement, not only an existential residual.
