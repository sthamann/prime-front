\section{Classical object (r461, taken literally)}

Write $\QW(h)$ for the Guinand--Weil functional of the even
autocorrelation $g=h*\widetilde h$.  After r461,
\[
 \lambda_*(L)=
 \inf_{\substack{0\ne h\in L^2(\mathbb R)\\
                  \operatorname{supp}h\subset[-L,L]}}
 \frac{\QW(h)}{\|h\|_2^2},\qquad
 L_k=\frac{k\log 2}{4}.
\]
Then $\operatorname{supp} g\subset[-2L,2L]$.  The classical
Yoshida/Bombieri zone is the prime-blind range $U< \log 2$, i.e.\
$L<(\log 2)/2\approx 0.34657$.  The external preprint
arXiv:2608.24827 certifies a strictly positive floor for \emph{all}
even $L^2$ test functions with $\operatorname{supp}h\subset[-0.8,0.8]$.
The translation ``$R^\dagger(W_k)\succeq\tfrac12 I\implies\lambda_*(L_k)\ge 0$''
remains conditional on the named r462/r463 bridges; this round does
not use that dictionary.

\section{Self-defined form}

For even compactly supported $g$,
\[
 \QW(g)=A(g)-P(g)+\Pi(g),
\]
\begin{align*}
 P(g)&=\sum_{n\ge 2}\frac{2\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\,g(\log n),\\
 \Pi(g)&=4\int_0^\infty g(x)\cosh(x/2)\,dx,\\
 A(g)&=-(\gamma+\log\pi)\,g(0)
  +2\int_0^\infty\frac{g(0)e^{-2w}-g(w)e^{-w/2}}{1-e^{-2w}}\,dw.
\end{align*}
Compact support of $g$ cuts $P$ to the finite range $n\le e^{2L}$.
(The briefing's $n\le e^L$ mixes the autocorrelation length $U=2L$
with $L$; the probe follows r461.)  On piecewise-linear $g$ the pole
term is closed-form.  The archimedean integral is enclosed by a
Bernoulli expansion of $x/(1-e^{-x})$ on a prefix $[0,\eta]$ and a
geometric Bose series with an integral tail bound on $[\eta,2L]$.

\section{Grid family $F_{L,\delta}$}

Let $h$ be piecewise constant on $n=2L/\delta$ equal bins of
$[-L,L]$.  Then $g$ is a linear combination of tents of half-width
$\delta$, and $\QW$ becomes an $n\times n$ Toeplitz form $Q_L$ in the
bin coefficients.  Interval entries are sealed: $P$ from an integer
Eratosthenes list of prime powers, $\Pi$ exact, $A$ as above.
A Higham-validated Cholesky factor of the midpoint, with the
interval-radius row-sum subtracted, produces a rigorous lower bound
$\mu$ on $\lambda_{\min}(Q_L)$.  If $\mu>0$ the type is
\[
 \cert(L,\delta)\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad
 Q_L\succeq 0\text{ on }F_{L,\delta}.
\]
This is scramble-sensitive: $P$ evaluates $g$ at the literal nodes
$\log n$.  A source-position scramble moves those nodes and changes
$Q_L$.  The bound is not a $q^\dagger$ pairing and is not
scramble-invariant (r469 anti-list item 3).

\section{Discretization gap (F2b)}

The infimum $\lambda_*(L)$ ranges over a Sobolev/Weil test class on
which $g$ is continuous, so that $P(g)$ is well-defined pointwise and
$A(g)$ is the classical Mellin--digamma pairing.  Piecewise-constant
$h$ are dense in $L^2([-L,L])$, but $L^2$ is the wrong topology for
$\QW$: a jump in $h$ makes $g$ only Lipschitz, and the Weil class is
usually $C^2_c$ (or the Fourier transform of a nonnegative even
function of compact support).  The missing lemma is therefore exactly
F1(iii) of the r469 judgment, restricted to one fixed $L$:

\begin{itemize}
\item dyadic (or grid) autocorrelations are dense in the Weil test
class at support length $L$;
\item $\QW$ is continuous there, with an explicit modulus
$\omega_L(\delta)$.
\end{itemize}
If a mesh with bin width $\delta$ satisfies
$\mu(Q_L)>\omega_L(\delta)$, the grid certificate upgrades to
$\lambda_*(L)\ge\mu-\omega_L(\delta)$.  At $L=0.8$, $n=24$, one has
$\delta=1/15$ and $\mu\approx 1.32\cdot 10^{-4}$.  A $C^2$ modulus of
order $O(\delta^2)$ would close at a plausible constant; an $O(\delta)$
modulus would need a constant $\lesssim 2\cdot 10^{-3}$.  Neither
bound is proved here.  F2b is standard analysis at fixed $L$ and does
not restate a cofinal quantifier.
