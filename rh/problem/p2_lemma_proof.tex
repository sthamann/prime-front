\begin{abstract}\noindent
On a real Hermitian invertible matrix $A_{0}$ with exactly one
negative eigenvalue $-\lam<0$ and a two-column factor $U=(u,v)$,
the Schur matrix $K_{2}=I_{2}+U^{T}A_{0}^{-1}U$ admits the exact
factorization
\[
\det K_{2}
=\det(I+\Rp)\,
\bigl(1-\gamma/\lam\bigr),
\]
where $\Rp$ is the Gram of $(u,v)$ in the positive rest form of
$A_{0}$ and $\gamma=w^{T}(I+\Rp)^{-1}w$ with $w=U^{T}\psim$.
The prefactor $\det(I+\Rp)\ge 1$ is a sum of positive Gram
terms.  The sign of $\det K_{2}$ is carried entirely by the
dressed overlap $\gamma-\lam$.  This is finite $2\times2$
algebra, independent of any family.  On the nneg-$1$ branch of
the canonical dual CD construction the identity holds live, the
r359 fold-pair binding ratio is a \emph{different} $2\times2$
(already positive at rank $N-3$), and a raw overlap floor is
false (the CD pair may be almost orthogonal to $\psim$).  What
remains of (P2) is a source lower bound on the relative excess
$\gamma/\lam-1$.  Nothing in this document is evidence for or
against the Riemann hypothesis.
\end{abstract}

\section{The object}\label{sec:obj}

Let $A_{0}$ be real symmetric and invertible, of size $m\ge 2$,
with inertia $(m-1,1,0)$: one simple negative eigenvalue
$-\lam<0$ and a positive definite complement.  Write $\psim$ for
a unit eigenvector of $-\lam$, $\Ap^{-1}$ for the inverse of
$A_{0}$ on $\psim^{\perp}$ (zero on $\operatorname{span}\{\psim\}$),
and $U=(u,v)\in\R^{m\times 2}$.  Set
\[
K_{2}=I_{2}+U^{T}A_{0}^{-1}U.
\]
In the dual CD construction of rounds 367--371 one has
$A_{0}=R_{N-3}-\half I$ on the node set $Y$ and $U$ the last two
dual Christoffel--Darboux columns.  The Haynsworth identity
(sorry-free in \texttt{RH/Haynsworth.lean}) converts
$\operatorname{ind}_{-}(A_{0})=1$ together with $\det K_{2}<0$
into $A_{0}+UU^{T}\succ 0$.  The present note attacks only the
determinant sign and floor, by expanding $K_{2}$ in source sizes.

\section{Six scalars}\label{sec:six}

Write $\alpha=\langle u,\psim\rangle$, $\beta=\langle v,\psim\rangle$,
$w=(\alpha,\beta)^{T}$, and
\[
\Rp
=
\begin{pmatrix} r_{uu} & r_{uv}\\ r_{uv} & r_{vv}\end{pmatrix}
=U^{T}\Ap^{-1}U.
\]
The six real scalars are $(\alpha,\beta,\lam,r_{uu},r_{vv},r_{uv})$.

\begin{lemma}[Spectral expansion]\label{lem:six}
\[
U^{T}A_{0}^{-1}U
=-\frac{ww^{T}}{\lam}+\Rp.
\]
In particular the three quadratic forms of identity~(12) of
round 371 are
\begin{align*}
\langle u,A_{0}^{-1}u\rangle
&=-\alpha^{2}/\lam+r_{uu},\\
\langle v,A_{0}^{-1}v\rangle
&=-\beta^{2}/\lam+r_{vv},\\
\langle u,A_{0}^{-1}v\rangle
&=-\alpha\beta/\lam+r_{uv}.
\end{align*}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The spectral calculus of $A_{0}$ on the nneg-$1$ splitting
$\R^{m}=\operatorname{span}\{\psim\}\oplus\psim^{\perp}$ is
$A_{0}^{-1}= -\lam^{-1}\,\psim\psim^{T}+\Ap^{-1}$.  Sandwiching
with $U$ is the claim.  On the rational $4$-node model
$A_{0}=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,2,3)$, $U=[(2,0,0,0)^{T},(1,1,0,0)^{T}]$
one has $(\alpha,\beta,\lam,r_{uu},r_{vv},r_{uv})=(2,1,1,0,1,0)$
and the three forms equal $(-4,0,-2)$ exactly.
\end{proof}

Let $\delta=\alpha v-\beta u$ (the $\psim$-component of the
two-form $u\wedge v$) and
\[
N=\alpha^{2}+\beta^{2}+\langle\delta,\Ap^{-1}\delta\rangle,
\qquad
\gamma=w^{T}(I+\Rp)^{-1}w.
\]
A $2\times2$ cofactor expansion gives the identity
\[
\gamma
=\frac{N}{\det(I+\Rp)},
\qquad
N=\alpha^{2}+\beta^{2}+\bigl(\alpha^{2}r_{vv}+\beta^{2}r_{uu}-2\alpha\beta r_{uv}\bigr).
\]

\section{Factorization}\label{sec:fact}

\begin{lemma}[Matrix-determinant factorization]\label{lem:fact}
$K_{2}=(I+\Rp)-ww^{T}/\lam$ and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fact}
\det K_{2}
=\det(I+\Rp)\,
\bigl(1-\gamma/\lam\bigr).
\end{equation}
Equivalently, the positive-structure form
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pos}
-\det K_{2}
=\frac{N-\lam\,\det(I+\Rp)}{\lam}
=\det(I+\Rp)\,
\bigl(\gamma/\lam-1\bigr).
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The rank-$1$ display is Lemma~\ref{lem:six} plus $K_{2}=I+U^{T}A_{0}^{-1}U$.
The matrix-determinant lemma on that update is~\eqref{eq:fact}.
The second display is the same identity rewritten with $N=\gamma\det(I+\Rp)$.
On the $4$-node model, $\det(I+\Rp)=2$, $\gamma=9/2$, $N=9$ and
$\det K_{2}=2\cdot(1-9/2)=-7$, matching identity~(12).
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Sign carrier]\label{lem:sign}
Assume $\lam>0$ and $I+\Rp\succ 0$.  Then
\[
\det K_{2}<0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\gamma>\lam
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
N>\lam\,\det(I+\Rp).
\]
The sign of $\det K_{2}$ is carried by the single scalar
$\gamma-\lam$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$\det(I+\Rp)>0$ by $I+\Rp\succ 0$, and $\lam>0$ by nneg-$1$.
The first factor of~\eqref{eq:fact} cannot change sign.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Positive structures]\label{lem:pos}
$\Rp\succeq 0$, hence $\det(I+\Rp)=1+\operatorname{tr}\Rp+\det\Rp\ge 1$.
The numerator $N$ is a sum of two nonnegative terms: the raw
pair-overlap $\|w\|^{2}$ and the rest energy
$\langle\delta,\Ap^{-1}\delta\rangle$ of the wedge component.
Neither term is a priori disposable.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$\Ap^{-1}$ is positive semidefinite by construction, so $\Rp$ is a
Gram matrix and $I+\Rp\succeq I$.  The splitting of $N$ is the
$2\times2$ cofactor identity of \S\ref{sec:six}.  Load-bearing of
the second summand is Lemma~\ref{lem:delta} below.
\end{proof}

Identity~(12) of round 371 is the expansion of $\det K_{2}$ in the
three quadratic forms; it is compatible with~\eqref{eq:fact} but
does not isolate the sign.  The wedge term of that identity is
the Gram determinant of $A_{0}^{-1}$, which mixes the negative
mode with the rest; after the spectral split it becomes
$\det\Rp-\langle\delta,\Ap^{-1}\delta\rangle/\lam$, a difference of
positive structures whose sign is stable on the branch (round 371)
but is \emph{not} equivalent to $\gamma>\lam$ without the linear
terms $1+\langle u,A_{0}^{-1}u\rangle+\langle v,A_{0}^{-1}v\rangle$.

\section{The adversarial half}\label{sec:adv}

\begin{lemma}[P1 does not imply P2]\label{lem:adv}
There exist real symmetric $A_{0}$ of inertia $(m-1,1,0)$ and
columns $U$ with $\det K_{2}>0$.  Explicitly, on the $4$-node
model, replacing $U$ by $0.05\cdot[(1,0,0,0)^{T},(0,1,0,0)^{T}]$
yields $\gamma=0.0025<\lam=1$ and $\det K_{2}=+1.0025>0$; the
updated matrix $A_{0}+UU^{T}$ keeps a negative eigenvalue.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Direct $4\times4$ arithmetic.  Haynsworth is one-sided: both
premises are load-bearing (round 367, swapped-premise mutants).
\end{proof}

Algebraically, small overlaps fail to kill $\psim$.  A source
property of the dual CD columns must forbid $\gamma\le\lam$ on the
nneg-$1$ branch.  A \emph{uniform} raw-overlap floor
$\|w\|^{2}\ge\kappa_{0}>0$ is the wrong shape: on deep nneg-$1$
rows the pair-span of $\psim$ onto $\operatorname{span}\{u,v\}$
drops to $6\cdot10^{-4}$ while $\det K_{2}$ remains large and
negative, because $\lam$ is smaller still.  The comparison that
can be uniform is the \emph{relative} excess $\gamma/\lam-1$.

The sufficient Cauchy bound $\|w\|^{2}>\lam\bigl(1+\lambda_{\max}(\Rp)\bigr)$
(which ignores the $\delta$-energy) already fails on a well-conditioned
nneg-$1$ row (kz46: the $\delta$-share of $N$ is $0.723$).  The wedge
dressing of Lemma~\ref{lem:pos} is load-bearing.

\begin{lemma}[The $\delta$-term is necessary]\label{lem:delta}
There is a canonical nneg-$1$ window at which
$\|w\|^{2}\le\lam\bigl(1+\lambda_{\max}(\Rp)\bigr)$ while
$\gamma>\lam$.  Dropping $\langle\delta,\Ap^{-1}\delta\rangle$ from
$N$ does not imply (P2).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The window kz46 of the sealed r367 ladder: $\lam=9.869\cdot10^{-2}$,
$\|w\|^{2}=0.281$, pair-span $0.743$, $\delta$-share $0.723$,
excess $\gamma/\lam-1=0.298>0$, and the sufficient Cauchy bound
fails.  Machine-checked by the companion script.
\end{proof}

\section{Not the binding thesis}\label{sec:bind}

Round 359 defined
$\mathrm{bind}=\lambda_{\min}(S_{N})/(\lambda_{\min}(R)-\half)$
for the $2\times2$ Schur block of $R-\half I$ at the critical
fold pair $(2,4)$.  That ratio consumes the terminal margin by
construction.

\begin{lemma}[P2 is not bind]\label{lem:notbind}
On every resolvable nneg-$1$ window of the r367 ladder the
fold-pair block of $A_{0}$ is already positive definite
($\lambda_{\min}$ of the $2\times2$ pair restriction of $A_{0}$
is positive, $45/45$).  The remaining negative mode $\psim$ is
rest-hosted on $42/45$ (coordinate mass of $\psim$ on the fold
pair $<1/2$).  Therefore $\psim$ is not the r359 pair, and
$\gamma>\lam$ does not consume $\lambda_{\min}(S_{N})$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The pair/rest split is the r367 negative-direction ledger,
reproduced by the companion at w9 ($\mathrm{pmass}=0.1514$,
$\lambda_{\mathrm{pair}}>0$) and on the branch ($45/45$ pair
blocks positive).  The two $2\times2$ matrices are different
objects: last two CD columns against $A_{0}$, versus the fold
pair Schur of the full $R-\half I$.
\end{proof}

A reduction of (P2) to $\mathrm{bind}\ge 1$ would be a restatement
of the terminal margin and is forbidden.  The shadow test of the
excess against $\log|\lambda_{\min}(R_{N-1})-\half|$ on the
calibration nneg-$1$ windows is $+0.046$ (not a rewrite).

\section{The reduction}\label{sec:reduction}

Lemmas~\ref{lem:fact}--\ref{lem:pos} convert (P2) on the nneg-$1$
branch into a comparison of two positive scalars.  Because
$\det(I+\Rp)\ge 1$ is SATZ, the inequality
\begin{equation}\label{eq:red}
\gamma\ge(1+\varepsilon)\,\lam
\tag{P2$^{-}$}
\end{equation}
implies $-\det K_{2}\ge\varepsilon$.  This is strictly stronger
than the sign $\det K_{2}<0$, strictly closer to the construction
(a Rayleigh quotient of the two-vector $w=U^{T}\psim$ against one
rest eigenvalue, no $2\times2$ Schur determinant of $K_{2}$), and
does not consume $\lambda_{\min}(S_{N})$.  It is \emph{not}
equivalent to a uniform floor $\|w\|^{2}\ge\kappa_{0}$ (false,
\S\ref{sec:adv}) and not equivalent to identity~(12)'s capture
clause (that clause is $\det K_{2}<0$ in other letters, round 371).

The original (P2) with some fixed $c_{0}>0$ is therefore reduced
to~\eqref{eq:red}.  A numerical $\varepsilon$ is not frozen here:
the shallowest well-conditioned nneg-$1$ window (kz18) has excess
$0.2746$, so any $\varepsilon\le 1/4$ is currently compatible with
the branch, but a fitted constant is not a source statement.

\begin{prop}[Branch check, not a law]\label{prop:branch}
On the $45$ resolvable nneg-$1$ windows of the r367 ladder,
$\gamma>\lam$ holds $45/45$, identity~\eqref{eq:fact} holds at
f64 grade except two near-singular EXT rows whose $\lam$ is
$10^{-7}$ (the identity is SATZ; the residual is a spectral
reconstruction artefact), $-\det K_{2}\in[1.157,1126.389]$,
$\det(I+\Rp)\ge 3.307$, excess $\gamma/\lam-1\in[0.275,3.295]$,
and the Cauchy sufficient bound holds only $44/45$
(Lemma~\ref{lem:delta}).  Character-twisted analogs: $\chi_{3}$
has $21$ nneg-$1$ rows, all sign-stable, w9 already
positive-definite (world-separating); $\chi_{4}$ has $19$ nneg-$1$
rows, all sign-stable.  The matched scramble breaks named at
(P1) ($\operatorname{ind}_{-}(A_{0})=21$) while $\det K_{2}<0$
survives.  No resolvable canonical window realises
Lemma~\ref{lem:adv}.
\end{prop}

The proposition is a machine check, not a cofinal law, and is not
the outcome of the round.

\section{Named remainder}\label{sec:rem}

What (P2$^{-}$) still asks of the source:
\begin{quote}
The last two dual CD columns, with closed dual weights
$u^{\vee}=c_{j}(1-x)/|f|$ (round 356) and Christoffel--Darboux
kernel~\emph{(13)} of round 371, have dressed overlap
$\gamma=w^{T}(I+\Rp)^{-1}w$ against the unique negative mode
$\psim$ of $R_{N-3}-\half I$ strictly larger than $\lam$.
\end{quote}
The mode $\psim$ is an object of the dual ensemble (Borodin
complementation, round 356; occupation duality, round 360).  It
is not node-pinned at the saturated block (round 363) and not
hosted by the critical fold pair (Lemma~\ref{lem:notbind}).
Expanding $(\alpha,\beta)$ against that mode, via the dual OP
restrictions and the kernel $K_{N-3}$ on $Y$, is the remaining
finite identity.  No $\lambda_{\min}$ of a Schur block of the
full $R$, and no terminal margin, enters the target.

\section{Claim boundary}

Finite real algebra on a Hermitian pair $(A_{0},U)$, plus a
named remainder on the dual CD construction.  Haynsworth remains
sorry-free and does not assert (P1) or (P2) on windows.  L$^{*}$
and the Riemann hypothesis are not addressed in either direction.
