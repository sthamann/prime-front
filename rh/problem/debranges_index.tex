\begin{abstract}\noindent
After rounds 413/415 closed the hole-top-mode route, the
reviewer (DCCLXXIX, path~B) asks for the last unused
structural language: de~Branges / Potapov on the two finite
positive OP systems of the continuation
($X$ = $\mu$-chain on the $X$-nodes with dual weights $u^\vee$;
$Y$ = hole measure).
The candidate lemma has three equivalent forms: a contractive
embedding $H(E_Y)\hookrightarrow H(E_X)$ up to one mode; a
rational transfer matrix $\Theta$ with Potapov index
$\kappa(\Theta)\le 1$; Pr\"ufer phase dominance
$\varphi_X'\ge\varphi_Y'$ except one deficit of total drop
$<2\pi$.
The index is basis-free (no exceptional vector).
The Hermite--Biehler pair of each positive discrete measure
is a finite SATZ (zeros of $p_n$ and $q_n$ real and
interlacing; Wronskian constant over $\mathbb{Q}$).
The phase-dominance lemma is \emph{refuted} on flagship MAIN:
the combinatorial R\"ucklauf counter (YY-adjacencies of
$\mathrm{Gauss}_X(d_0)$ against the hole atoms) equals $3$,
not $\le 1$, and is uncorrelated with
$\indm(I-\Tfrak_0^*\Tfrak_0)$.
Permute does not inflate the counter.
No RH claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Definitions}\label{sec:def}

Let $Y$ be the hole nodes of a window, $X$ the complementary
dual nodes, and $u^\vee$ the r356/r407 dual weights.
Write $q=|Y|$ and $d_0=N_w-3$ for the dual interpolating
depth of $\Tfrak_0$ (r409).
The $X$-system is the Jacobi chain of $(X,u^\vee|_X)$ at
degree $n_X=\min(d_0,|X|-1)$; the $Y$-system is the chain of
$(Y,u^\vee|_Y)$ at degree $n_Y=q-1$.
First-kind orthonormal polynomials $p_n$ and second-kind
$q_n$ (Cauchy transform against $p$) give the Hermite--Biehler
function $E=A-iB$ with $A=p_n$, $B=q_n$.
Gauss zeros of $p_n$ are the eigenvalues of the $n\times n$
Jacobi matrix; zeros of $q_n$ are those of the trailing
principal submatrix.
The combinatorial R\"ucklauf counter $\mathrm{yy}_A$ is the
number of consecutive $Y$-atoms with no $X$-Gauss node
between them in the merged ordered list
(one mode $\Leftrightarrow \mathrm{yy}_A\le 1$).
The equal-dimension control $\mathrm{yy}_S$ merges
$\mathrm{Gauss}_X(q-1)$ with $\mathrm{Gauss}_Y(q-1)$.

\section{Finite identities}\label{sec:satz}

\begin{lemma}[Wronskian]\label{lem:wron}
On the r409 rational $3$-atom $X$-measure, the monic pair
$p_2(z)=(z-a_1)(z-a_0)-\gamma_1$, $q_2(z)=z-a_1$ has
Wronskian $p_2 q_1 - p_1 q_2 = -\gamma_1$ independent of $z$,
with $\gamma_1=252000/3463321>0$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[interlacing over $\mathbb{Q}$]\label{lem:inter}
In the same toy, $\mathrm{disc}(p_2)=(a_0-a_1)^2+4\gamma_1
=17761/19600>0$, and $p_2(a_1)=-\gamma_1<0$, so the unique
zero of $q_2$ strictly interlaces the two zeros of $p_2$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Hermite--Biehler of a positive Jacobi matrix]\label{lem:hb}
For every measured positive discrete measure in the census
(MAIN $42$, living $\chi$ $78$, dead $\chi$ $6$, permute,
scramble, flagship $w_9$, the $5$-atom toy) the zeros of
$p_n$ and $q_n$ are real and interlace.
This is the classical Gauss / Gauss--Radau relation for a
positive Jacobi matrix, gated rather than assumed.
\end{lemma}

\begin{remark}
Lemma~\ref{lem:hb} is automatic for any positive measure.
It does \emph{not} constrain the relative zero sets of the
$X$-system and the $Y$-system, which is where the index
lemma lives.
\end{remark}

\section{Machine checks}\label{sec:prop}

\begin{prop}[degree balance]\label{prop:balance}
At $k_z=9$, $|X|=263$, $|Y|=104$, $d_0=181$, so
$n_X=d_0=181<|X|-1$ (the $X$-chain is truncated to the
interpolating depth) and $n_Y=103=q-1$.
Both Stieltjes transforms are Herglotz at $z=0.3+0.7i$
($\operatorname{Im}m_X,\operatorname{Im}m_Y>0$), while
$\indm(I-\Tfrak_0^*\Tfrak_0)=1$ and $\|\Tfrak_0\|=1.08014$.
The full-measure Uvarov path (r392 elementary Stieltjes
steps) has Potapov index $0$ and cannot see the truncated
interpolant.
\end{prop}

\begin{prop}[primary test]\label{prop:primary}
On flagship MAIN, $\mathrm{yy}_A=3\not\le 1$ and
$\mathrm{yy}_S=24\gg 1$.
The phase counter is not the operator index
($\mathrm{yy}_A\neq \indm(I-\Tfrak^*\Tfrak)$).
The dominant YY-interval versus the top right singular
vector of $\Tfrak_0$ has relative location $0.092$ at
$k_z=9$, but across MAIN the location spans $[0.059,1]$:
it is not a coordinate that walks with the window-individual
defect.
\end{prop}

\begin{prop}[census and kills]\label{prop:census}
MAIN $42$: Hermite--Biehler $42/42$; $\mathrm{yy}_A$ in
$\{0,\ldots,15\}$ with median $4$; of $28$ P1 windows only
$3$ have $\mathrm{yy}_A\le 1$; of $14$ PD windows only $3$
have $\mathrm{yy}_A=0$;
$\mathrm{corr}(\mathrm{yy}_A,\indm(I-\Tfrak^*\Tfrak))=-0.17$.
Living $\chi$ ($78$): $\mathrm{yy}_A$ median $7$, max $26$.
Dead $\chi$ ($6$): median $2$, max $4$ --- death is
\emph{fewer} R\"uckl\"aufe, not a bulk-phase overflow.
Permute: $\mathrm{yy}_A=4$ (the MAIN median) with
$\indm=20$ --- the world-separator does not fire on the
phase counter.
Scramble: $\mathrm{yy}_A=16\neq 21$.
The mutant $B:=p_n$ (no second kind) breaks interlacing
by degree.
\end{prop}

\section{What is a theorem, what is a census}\label{sec:honest}

The counting-argument candidate --- interlacing of $X$-Gauss
zeros against $Y$-atoms as a corollary of the fold partition
of the ordered node set --- is closed: the fold does not
force $\mathrm{yy}_A\le 1$.
Hermite--Biehler of each system separately is a theorem
(Lemmas~\ref{lem:wron}--\ref{lem:hb}).
The YY table is a census.
The Potapov index of $\Tfrak_0$ itself remains
$\indm(I-\Tfrak_0^*\Tfrak_0)$ (r409 dictionary); re-reading
it as a de~Branges phase of the two OP systems does not
produce a new P1 proof.
P1 stays $\lambda_2(C)\ge 1$ on the dual quotient.
No RH claim.
