window is $k_z136$.

\section{Pivot anatomy}\label{sec:anat}

ABD.ok requires the $\tilde\mu$-chain to reach
depth $N$ with every $h_n>0$.
The recorded pivot is the signed mass
$\eta=P-N$ at step \emph{start},
$P=\sum w\,q^2$, $N=\sum v\,p^2$,
$\mathrm{rel}=|\eta|/(P+N)$.

\begin{prop}[Float first flips]\label{prop:float}
On the selected sequence the float64 chain
records:
$k=10$ first flip $n=3788$,
$\eta=-7.87\cdot 10^{-14}$,
$P\approx N\approx 3.44\cdot 10^{-8}$,
$\mathrm{rel}=1.14\cdot 10^{-6}$;
$k=11$ first flip $n=10581$,
$\eta=-7.64\cdot 10^{-16}$,
$\mathrm{rel}=4.81\cdot 10^{-8}$;
$k=12$ first flip $n=12654$,
$\eta=-1.01\cdot 10^{-22}$,
$\mathrm{rel}=4.66\cdot 10^{-14}$,
then $\eta=0$ abort at $n=12737$
(class \texttt{ETA\_UNDERFLOW}).
$k=8,9$ have $0$ flips
($\min|\eta|=2.6\cdot 10^{-14}$ / $5.5\cdot 10^{-12}$).
\end{prop}

Six-digit cancellation at $k=10$ still leaves
about ten float64 digits.
That is \emph{not} an underflow of $\eta$ itself;
it is small enough that a recurrence-rounding
story was live (r427 ward: pivots $\sim 10^{-8}$
at $N\ge 4071$).

\section{mpmath adjudication}\label{sec:mp}

Atoms stay float64 (the construction).
The three-term recurrence is re-run at high dps.
In-window start-$\eta$ at $k=5$ ($N=96$) agrees
with float to $10^{-14}$ relative
(the post-window extra step is not an ABD row).

\begin{prop}[REAL]\label{prop:real}
The $k=10$ chain at dps $40$ (full, $503\,\mathrm{s}$)
flips first at $n=3788$,
$\eta=-7.938908159491412\cdot 10^{-14}$,
$\mathrm{rel}=1.150853124516389\cdot 10^{-6}$,
$n_{\mathrm{flip}}=115$, $\mathtt{pos\_ok}=\mathrm{false}$.
The same first pivot at dps $60$ (to $n=3793$,
$428\,\mathrm{s}$) is bit-identical.
The sign is stable under a dps raise.
Hence the r445 break is REAL relative to the
float64-atom construction: it is not a
float64 recurrence artefact.
\end{prop}

$k=11$: the first flip is the same near-null
class at tighter $\mathrm{rel}$; the $B_w$
explosion is signed $\rho=f_b^2/\eta$ after
many flips, not a second phenomenon.
A full $k=11$ mpmath chain to $n=10581$ is
outside the $45\,\mathrm{min}$ budget
(larger atoms, $\sim 3\times$ steps).
$k=12$ dies by \texttt{ETA\_UNDERFLOW}:
float hits exact $\eta=0$ on the same
near-null (not overflow, not a division
exception).

\section{Mesh}\label{sec:mesh}

r397 leaves room in the $m_k$ choice at fixed
anchors $a_k=2^k$.
\texttt{pp\_kz} rounding does not move
$k=10,11,12$ ($2^k$ sits exactly on the $U$-grid).

\begin{prop}[Mesh does not repair]\label{prop:mesh}
Every tested legal neighbour of the $k=10$
anchor ($k_z193..201$) and of the $k=11$
anchor ($k_z337..341$) is ABD-dead in float.
A denser sample $k_z137..210$ is likewise dead.
The last observed living window is $k_z136$
($N=1641$); $k_z137$ ($N=8300$) already flips.
No small $m$-variation at the $2^k$ anchors
restores ABD-living.
\end{prop}

This is a finding about the construction,
not a rounding bug.
The r397 cofinality theorems ($a_k\to\infty$,
$\Delta_k\to 0$) do not imply ABD-cofinality.
A revised sequence that stays in the living
band would need a different window family
(not a local $m$-tweak of $a_k=2^k$).
Whether infinitely many living windows exist
at all is open; the empirical living band
ends at $k_z136$ in every sample we ran.

The r445 ABD-ok slice $k=5..9$ (live $k=8$)
and its floor fit are unchanged
($\delta_\infty=+0.02741$, $\Delta\mathrm{AIC}=5.11$).
There is no new deep $\delta$-point.

Dead $\chi_3$-$15$: $\delta=-0.023181$ (control).
$k=5$ determinism: run1 $=$ run2.
