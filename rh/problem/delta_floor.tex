\section{The lemma, and the chart}\label{sec:lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Selected $\delta$-floor]\label{lem:floor}
On the selected sequence $a_k=2^k$,
$\liminf_k\delta_k>0$.
\end{lemma}

\noindent
Not proved this round.  The working identity is:

\begin{lemma}[Chart]\label{lem:chart}
$\delta=-\mathrm{sch}$.
On the vacuous branch
$\delta=R+\tau^{2}$ with $R=-\phibb$.
On the P1 branch
$\delta=R+(b_{\mathrm{un}}^{2}-a_{\mathrm{un}}^{2})$.
In both cases $R=2-\mathrm{den}-\Sigma$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
r433 and r417, over $\mathbb{Q}$:
P1 $\mathrm{sch}=-2/3$, VAC $\mathrm{sch}=-7/6$.
On the six live selected windows the chart residual
is $0$.
\end{proof}

Selected $k=6,7$ are vacuous; the rest are P1.
$\tau^{2}$ is already $O(10^{-3})$ on VAC and falls
$0.019\to 0.0006$ on P1, so at $k=9$ one has
$|\delta-R|=3.5\cdot 10^{-4}$.
$\mathrm{den}$ is flat in $[1.48,1.70]$
($O(0.5)$ room).
$\Sigma=2-\mathrm{den}-R$ wanders $0.211\to 0.480$.
The decay of $\delta$ \emph{is} the decay of $R$.

\section{Conditional close, and its honesty}\label{sec:cond}

If a floor $\delta_{\infty}>0$ exists then, eventually
on Selected,
$\qd\le 1-\delta_{\infty}\approx 0.973$,
hence $\kapd=0$ by r442, hence the frequently-mincut
is filled.
That implication is tautological.
The missing input is existence of the liminf.
It is now a scalar sequence, not a matrix cone ---
but it is still a census ($k=5..9$ plus EXT), not a
theorem.
A provable lower bound would need
$\Sigma_{\infty}<|c_{J,\infty}|$ with
$c_J=\mathrm{den}-2$.
The $\mathrm{den}$-room is $O(0.5)$ and near a
theorem via $\gamma<1$ (r424:
$\lVert b\rVert^{2}\le S$ is SATZ;
$S<B_w\Leftrightarrow q_N<1$).
$q_N$ is \emph{not} $\qd$ (r433); that bridge is
the circularity gate.
