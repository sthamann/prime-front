\begin{abstract}\noindent
Round 483 sealed Filon on $[0,150]$ and a four-term
IBP tail, and Higham-certified the finite even Schur
($\mu\ge1.20\cdot10^{-3}$).
The SATZ $\lambda_*(0.3)\ge c$ was blocked by leftover
$\|P_{\mathrm{block}}A P_{\ge12}\|_F=2.76\cdot10^{-2}$
against budget $2.4\cdot10^{-4}$.
The hoped close was a $\sim20\times$ coupling drop per
even double-step, so that a block through $\psi_{18}$
would push leftover below budget, together with a
Nystr\"om-$n=128$ (or QR-polynomial) certificate of the
high-mode jets.
Both routes fail.
A mode is usable iff $|u(L)|$ at $n$ and $2n$ agree to
$10^{-3}$: $\psi_0,\psi_2,\psi_4,\psi_6$ pass;
$\psi_8$ is marginal ($10^{-4}$);
$\psi_{10}$ and above are $O(1)$-unstable at every
$n\le128$ ($\lambda_{10}$ sits at the binary64 floor).
On the resolved $n=96$ surface the NO-TAIL leftover
after $\psi_{10}$ is $1.38\cdot10^{-1}$ (the $n=48$
figure $2.76\cdot10^{-2}$ was an undercount);
after $\psi_{18}$ it is $9.60\cdot10^{-2}$;
after $\psi_{20}$, $4.40\cdot10^{-2}$
(factor $\sim180$ versus budget).
The drop $10\to18$ is $1.43\times$, not $20\times$.
Even Legendres QR-orthogonalised against certified
$\psi_0..\psi_8$ leave leftover $O(0.08)$ at $12$ extra
columns; four-term sinc-IBP on those vectors makes
$\lambda_{\min}(S)\approx-0.065$.
Enlarging $B$ to $\{\psi_0..\psi_8\}$ recovers
$\|C_{\mathrm{rest}}\|\approx0.29$, the r480
$\kappa_{\mathrm{high}}$, still alive in the discrete
nullspace.
Verdict
\texttt{STUCK(20x-drop-falsified@n96; jets>=10-garbage@n128; QR-IBP-kills-S)}.
Fourier--HS is not reused.
Yoshida--Bombieri zone.  No RH claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{QR escape}

$\{\psi_0,\psi_2,\psi_4,\psi_6,\psi_8\}$ is legal for
the Schur; $\varepsilon=\|(1-P)K(1-P)\|$ need only
be controlled at the end.

Not used as a pad.

the complement by garbage eigenmodes.
