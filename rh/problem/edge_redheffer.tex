\section{The Redheffer step}\label{sec:star}

Write $A_{0}=R_{N-3}-\tfrac12 I$ and $U_{\mathrm{CD}}$
for the two last dual OP columns on $Y$ (r367).
The mixed form of r369 is
\[
A=\operatorname{blkdiag}(A_{0},-\tfrac12),\qquad
U=\begin{pmatrix}U_{\mathrm{CD}} & s\\ 0 & -1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
E=J=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1/\mathrm{den}),
\]
with $s=R\,v$ the Sherman--Morrison border vector and
$\mathrm{den}=(1+\gamma)-v^{T}Rv$.
The bulk colligation is the $2\times 2$ block
$\Theta=\bigl[\begin{smallmatrix}A & U\\ U^{T} & 0_{3}\end{smallmatrix}\bigr]$.
The terminating Redheffer star with load $E$ is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:load}
\mathcal{R}_{E}(\Theta)
\;=\;
A+UEU^{T}.
\end{equation}
Equivalently, first close the two-dimensional CD port
($A_{0}+U_{\mathrm{CD}}U_{\mathrm{CD}}^{T}$), pad the
$-\tfrac12$ corner, then close the one-dimensional
border port with $1/\mathrm{den}$.

\begin{lemma}[Redheffer identification]\label{lem:star}
On the rational $4$-node mixed toy,
\eqref{eq:load} equals the r362 inverse
$\Rd-\tfrac12 I$ with residual $0$
($\mathrm{den}=\tfrac{23}{30}$).
The sequential CD-then-border product equals the packed
$3\times 3$ load, residual $0$.
At the flagship window $k_{z}=9$ all three residuals
are $1.9\cdot 10^{-15}$.
\end{lemma}

The last $1\times 1$ Schur complement of the
$3\times 3$ characteristic matrix
$\Phi=J^{-1}+U^{T}A^{-1}U$ after eliminating the
$K_{2}$ block is the r401 scalar $\sch$
(Haynsworth).  That equality is an identification,
not a definition of the pivot: the pivot is read
from $\Md$ independently.

\section{The last pivot}\label{sec:piv}

Let $\schb$ be the Schur complement of $\Md$ onto the
border coordinate (r362~(A6)) and
\[
\qdag
\;=\;
\gamma+v^{T}(I-E_{n})^{-1}v
\]
the dual-frame load (r362~$q^{\dagger}$).
Then $\schb=(1-\qdag)/(2(1+\qdag))$ identically, so
\begin{equation}\label{eq:delta}
\delta
\;:=\;
1-\qdag
\;=\;
\frac{4\,\schb}{1+2\,\schb}.
\end{equation}
This is the energy form $\delta=1-\lvert\alpha_{\mathrm{edge}}\rvert^{2}$
with $\lvert\alpha_{\mathrm{edge}}\rvert^{2}=\qdag$:
the cancellation that a triangle inequality would
spend sits in the complex load, not in an absolute
majorant.

\begin{lemma}[pivot dictionary]\label{lem:piv}
On the rational toy $\sch=-\tfrac{187}{450}$,
$\schb=\tfrac{187}{1426}$, and \eqref{eq:delta}
gives $\delta=\tfrac{187}{450}=-\sch$
($c'=1$).
On every named window of the census,
$1-\qdag=-\sch$ to f64 residual
(flagship $9.5\cdot 10^{-13}$; selected $k=9$
$5.5\cdot 10^{-8}$, graded).
\end{lemma}

The constant-vector Woodbury discriminant of r415
is a different number (flagship $\Delta_{\mathbf{1}}=2.11\cdot 10^{-3}$
against $\delta=0.06696$).  Dropping the border column
leaves no $3\times 3$ last pivot
($\operatorname{ind}_{-}K_{2}=\operatorname{ind}_{-}A_{0}$).

\section{The quarter is not a universal multiple}\label{sec:qtr}

The r263 dictionary is $q_{N}=\tfrac75 Z^{2}$, so
$q_{N}<1$ if and only if $\lvert Z\rvert<\sqrt{5/7}$.
Since $\tfrac14<\tfrac57$, the bound
$\lvert\Zloc\rvert\le\tfrac12$ (r428/r429, selected
census) is a sufficient $M$-bound on $\Zloc$, not
an identity with $\sch$.

\begin{prop}[quarter not universal]\label{prop:quarter}
There is no world-blind $c>0$ with
$\delta=c\bigl(\tfrac14-\lvert\Zloc\rvert^{2}\bigr)$
on the named census.
On selected $k=3,4,5,6,7,9$ both sides are positive
and the conversion
$c_{k}=\delta_{k}/(\tfrac14-\lvert\Zloc,k\rvert^{2})$
lies in $[0.155,5.51]$
($k=3$: $5.51$; $k=4$: $0.297$).
On vacuous MAIN ($k_{z}=12,16$) and living $\chi_{3}$-$9$,
$\delta>0$ while $\tfrac14-\lvert\Zloc\rvert^{2}<0$.
The r263 form $\tfrac57-\lvert\Zloc\rvert^{2}$ is likewise
not a multiple of $\delta$
($\qdag\neq q_{N}$; flagship $0.933$ against $0.214$).
\end{prop}

\end{prop}

\section{What this does not close}\label{sec:open}

The Redheffer writing makes the last transformation
identifiable as a Schur step, which was the stop
condition of DCCXCVII~(D).
It does not prove $\delta>0$ cofinally, nor
$\lvert\Zloc\rvert\le\tfrac12$ cofinally, nor
$R^{\dagger}\succeq\tfrac12 I$ on the selected
sequence.  The quarter remains a selected census
(r429), not a pivot.  Mincut unchanged
(base $4$ / refined $5$).
No $L^{*}$ claim.  No $R^{\dagger}$ claim.  No RH claim.
