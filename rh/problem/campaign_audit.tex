\section{Intertwiner}\label{sec:T1}

\begin{prop}[independent M\"obius]\label{prop:int}
On $k_z=9,17,12$, the r367 block
$R_{N-3}=A_0+\tfrac12 I$ agrees with
$R=C(I+C)^{-1}$ built from a dense
Chebyshev--Hankel Gram on $Y$
(not the campaign Cholesky), and
\[
  \mathrm{ind}_-(R-\tfrac12 I)
  \;=\;
  \#\{\lambda(C)<1\}.
\]
\end{prop}

Over $\mathbb{Q}$: $C=\mathrm{diag}(2,1/2)$
gives $R=\mathrm{diag}(2/3,1/3)$ and both
counts equal $1$.
On w9: $\Cmin=0.857119$ (pins $0.85712$ /
$0.857119$), formula $3.38\cdot10^{-12}$.
On kz17: $\Cmin=0.279842$, one negative
half-mode.
On kz12: $\Cmin=1.000074$, dictionary
$0=0$ (PD bulk).
mpmath dps-$40$ vs float64 Hankel at
$n=12$: relative $\le 1.5\cdot10^{-14}$.

kz42 ($N_w=2472$) was \emph{not}
Hankel-rebuilt (native depth is the r395
class).  The PD razor of r411 is audited
via $\kappa(C)$ and an independent kz12
window, not by native Hankel at $N=2472$.

\section{QD mass}\label{sec:T2}

\begin{prop}[one dual]\label{prop:qd}
The r413 ratio $1.357$ is the restriction
of a \emph{single} full-carrier dual
$u^\vee=1/(u\cdot P_\Xi'^2)$ with $P_\Xi$
on the whole grid, then split to $X$ and $Y$.
\end{prop}

Route A (log-shifted product) and route B
(r356 $\kappa$-formula) are proportional at
$3.9\cdot10^{-12}$.
A subset-$P'$ computed separately on $X$
and on $Y$ yields $1.525$ --- that is the
X/Y-norm bug; the campaign does not use it.
Signed $u$ produces $|Y|$ negative duals;
the campaign uses $|u|$.

Impact if the subset bug had been live:
both the QD-refutation and the mass
asymmetry would move.  It is not live.

\section{$\|\Tnull\|$ and float64 razors}\label{sec:T3}

Independent KKT Chebyshev interpolant:
$\|\Tnull\|_{\mathrm{w9}}=1.080138437324$,
matching r411/r413/r416 pins at
$1.080138437$ and $1/\sqrt{\Cmin}$ at
$5\cdot10^{-12}$.
The printed r409 band $[1.05,1.12]$
contains the tight pin.

The r409 PD print $\|\Tnull\|=1.00000$ is
rounding of
$1/\sqrt{1+4.565\cdot10^{-8}}=0.999999977$.
The gap $4.57\cdot10^{-8}$ sits
$56\times$ above
$\varepsilon_{\mathrm{mach}}\cdot\kappa(C)\cdot C_{\max}$
with $\kappa(C)=2.086\cdot10^3$ on w9
(Hankel $\mathrm{cond}(G)\sim10^7$ is
\emph{not} the eig-scale of $C$).
Independent kz12: $\Cmin-1=7.36\cdot10^{-5}$,
$\kappa(C)=29$, noise $\sim10^{-13}$.

Campaign razors run in float64
\texttt{eigvalsh}.  At the measured
$\kappa(C)$ this is adequate; a
$\kappa(C)\gtrsim10^{8}$ window would
make a $10^{-8}$ gap indistinguishable
from noise.  That is T7's precision ward,
not a flipped r411 verdict.

\section{Kernel Loewner}\label{sec:T4}

If both Gram forms are positive definite
on $P_{<N}$ and $\nu\ge0$, then
$\langle p,p\rangle_\mu\ge\langle p,p\rangle_{\mu-\nu}$
and inversion monotonicity gives
$K^{\mu-\nu}\succeq K^\mu$, hence
$\|b\|^2\le S$.
Over $\mathbb{Q}$: $\mu=(2,1)$ at $(0,1)$,
$\nu=(1)$ at $1$, constants,
$K^\mu=1/3\le K^{\mu-\nu}=1/2$.

If the tilde form is indefinite the
hypothesis is false: $\mu=(3,1)$ at
$(0,1)$, $\nu=(2)$ at $1$, depth $2$,
$G(\mu-\nu)$ has $\lambda_{\min}=-1.30$;
$K_t-K_\mu$ has $\lambda_{\min}=-2$.
The campaign states the tilde chain is
definite; the implication then holds.
Truncated PD Grams $n=16$ on
$k_z=9,17,12$: $K_{\tilde{}}\succeq K_\mu$
with $\lambda_{\min}(K_{\tilde{}}-K_\mu)
\sim -10^{-9}$ (float64 residual).

\section{Dead $\chi$ coordinates}\label{sec:T6}

\[
  q_N>1
  \;\Longleftrightarrow\;
  |\Zloc|_{\mathrm{term}}\ge\sqrt{5/7}
  \;\Longleftrightarrow\;

($|\Zloc|_{\mathrm{term}}^2=q_N\cdot5/7$).

$\mathrm{sch}=-0.065$, overlap
$6.9\cdot10^{-7}$.

coordinate: $\chi3$-$23$, $\chi3$-$33$,
$\chi4$-$20$ have $\Cmin>1$ and still
die at the edge (r401).
