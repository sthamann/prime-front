\begin{abstract}\noindent
Round 423 left $\gamma<1$ as the missing chain
step for $\mathrm{den}<2$.
The border vector $b$ is the coefficient vector
of the signed border measure $\sigma$ in the
$\mu$-orthonormal frame:
$b_k=\langle\sigma,\hat\pi_k^\mu\rangle$,
and $\|b\|^2$ equals the $\mu$-side Parseval
budget.
$\|b\|^2\le B_w$ compares this energy to a
\emph{different} chain (the $\mu$-tilde
$\rho$-budget plus $\tfrac57$).
Termwise $w_k=b_k^2/\rho_k$ is not bounded by
a uniform $\bar w<1$.
On the named census $\|b\|^2\le 0.80\,S$
(so the $\tfrac57$ buffer is not needed)
and $\gamma\le 0.724$.
No RH claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{The lemma}\label{sec:lem}

\begin{lemma}[$\gamma$ chain]\label{lem:gamma}
On the border chain,
$\|b\|^2\le B_w=S_{N-2}+\tfrac57$
(or $\|b\|^2\le q\,B_w$ with an explicit $q<1$),
so $\gamma<1$ is a theorem and
$\mathrm{den}=1+\gamma-v_t\cdot s<2$
follows even at $v_t\cdot s=0$.
\end{lemma}

\noindent
\textbf{Verdict: REDUZIERT.}
$b$ is identified (Proposition~\ref{prop:bessel}).
A uniform termwise bound $w_k\le\bar w<1$ is
refuted.
The sharpest holding form on the census is
$\|b\|^2\le 0.80\,S$, which is stronger than
$\|b\|^2\le B_w$ but is not proved.
$\gamma<1$ as a chain theorem remains open.

\section{What $b$ is}\label{sec:b}

\begin{prop}[$\mu$-Parseval of the border]\label{prop:bessel}
Let $\{\hat\pi_k^\mu\}$ be the $\mu$-orthonormal
OP frame (three-term recurrence of the positive
zone, start $1/\sqrt{h_0^\mu}$).
The Sherman--Morrison border column is
\[
  b_k
  \;=\;
  \sum_a w_a\,\hat\pi_k^\mu(t_a)
  \;=\;
  \langle\sigma,\hat\pi_k^\mu\rangle,
\]
where $\sigma$ is the signed border measure
(border atoms of $\mu$ with weights $w$,
border atoms of $\nu$ with weights $-v$).
Parseval on the truncated frame is exact:
$\|b\|^2=\sum_{k<N}(\int\hat\pi_k^\mu\,d\sigma)^2$,
equal to the $\mu$-side budget.
Over $\mathbb{Q}$,
$b=(\tfrac35,\tfrac45)$ with leftover
$\tfrac79$ gives
$\|b\|^2=1<\tfrac{16}{9}=\|\varphi\|^2$.
At $k_z=9$,
$\|b\|^2=5.6813$ matches the $\mu$-side
budget to $4\cdot10^{-14}$.
\end{prop}

Thus a same-frame Bessel
$\|b\|^2\le\|\varphi_B\|^2_{\mathrm{chain}}$
is true with $\varphi_B=\sigma$
\emph{and} with the $\mu$-norm on the left.
The budget $B_w=S_{N-2}+\tfrac57$ is the
partial energy of the \emph{$\mu$-tilde} chain
($\rho_k=F_k^2/h_k$).
$\gamma<1$ is therefore a cross-chain
comparison, not an identity of one inner
product.

\section{The ratio $w_k=b_k^2/\rho_k$}\label{sec:w}

If $w_k\le\bar w<1$ uniformly then
$\|b\|^2\le\bar w\,S<B_w$ at once.
This fails: on every core window about $40\%$
of the terms have $w_k>1$, and the maxima
are $10^6$--$10^9$ (they sit on near-null
$\rho$ steps).
At $k_z=9$,
$w_0=0.939$, median $0.623$,
$\mathrm{frac}(w>1)=0.43$,
$w_{\max}=1.6\cdot10^5$.
The sum still closes because large $w_k$
carry tiny $\rho_k$.
A weighted Cauchy--Schwarz of the two chains
is the remaining termwise language;
a uniform $\bar w$ is not.

\section{The $\tfrac57$ buffer and the sharp census}\label{sec:buf}

Dropping $\tfrac57$ still leaves
$\|b\|^2<S$ on every measured world
(selected max $\|b\|^2/S=0.799$ at $k=5$;
core-$42$ max $0.766$).
The buffer is not what saves the inequality
on the census.
The sharpest holding form is
\[
  \|b\|^2
  \;\le\;
  0.80\,S
  \;<\;
  S
  \;<\;
  S_{N-2}+\tfrac57
  \;=\;
  B_w.
\]
Core-$42$: $\gamma\in[0.548,0.724]$.
EXT $6/6$ the same pattern.
$k=8$ is pinned ($\gamma=0.6386$), not re-gated.

\section{What is a theorem, what is a census}\label{sec:honest}

Proposition~\ref{prop:bessel} is a theorem.
Lemma~\ref{lem:gamma} is not:
$\gamma<1$ is a cross-chain census,
$w_k$ has no uniform bound,
and $q=0.80$ is measured, not derived.
If $\gamma<1$ is proved,
$\mathrm{den}<2$ is immediate
($1+\gamma<2$).
No RH claim.
