\begin{prop}[A new $\zeta$-death type]\label{prop:zeta}
$k_z197$ is not a dead-$\chi$ world.
Dead $\chi$ keep a positive chain and die by
pole overshoot ($q^{\dagger}>1$ at the edge).
$k_z197$ breaks \emph{inside} the signed mass
chain at $n/N\approx 0.931$;
$\lvert Z_{\mathrm{loc}}\rvert=0.033$ is
\emph{living}-small, while $\lvert Z\rvert=1.132$
overflows.
This is a chain-positivity death on a
$\zeta$-window, the first measured.
\end{prop}

The $2^{10}$ anchor $a=1024$ is log-$2$
commensurable with the dyadic fold, but
Proposition~\ref{prop:generic} shows the same
in-chain death at a generic prime.
The resonance is not the cause.
