\section{Input completeness}\label{sec:inv}

\begin{lemma}[comb inventory]\label{lem:inv}
An atom count $k_a$ is accepted only if
$k_a=\#\{p^{\ell}\le a^2\}$ and the sieve
reaches $a^2$.
This is the \texttt{ExactPrimeSource}$(a)$
cutoff (prime powers, not $\pi(a^2)$).
Reflection tents are already in the
three-lag identity.
Powers $p^{\ell}>a^2$ are outside the
object; their absence is not an artefact
inside the object.
\end{lemma}
