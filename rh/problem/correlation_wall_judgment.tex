\begin{abstract}\noindent
This is an adjudication note.  It builds no probe, measures nothing
new, and proves no new theorem; it restates sealed identities, rules
on five proposed languages plus three of its own, and issues a
resource directive.  The three verdicts are
\textbf{U1 = \texttt{INTERNALLY\_EXHAUSTED}} (no admissible new
language; the reason is not fatigue but that the residual object is,
after the named bridges, equivalent to the full Weil criterion, and
that every language surviving the falsification test of round 467
demands input of Riemann-hypothesis strength),
\textbf{U2 = \texttt{EXTEND\_AND\_REFRAME}} (the standalone package
is the right external object, but must carry the r467/r468 wall
anatomy, a warning label about its own strength, and a separate
fixed-window prize), and
\textbf{U3 = \texttt{REDIRECT\_TO\_FIXED\_L}} (the core is closed for
rounds; the resources move to an unconditional classical theorem at
\emph{fixed} support length, which sits behind three already-named
and individually standard tasks).  No RH claim.  No anti-RH claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{The object, and the wall in one page}\label{sec:object}

Fix a selected window $W_k$ of the named sequence
$a_k=2^k$, $r_k=\lfloor\sqrt k\rfloor$, $m_k=k2^{r_k}-1$
(r461, r463).  Let $\mu_k,\nu_k$ be the positive and negative parts
of the folded signed measure built from the von~Mangoldt comb and the
archimedean kernel, let $p_{k,0},\dots,p_{k,N_k-1}$ be the
$\mu_k$-orthonormal polynomials, let $\sigma_k$ be the fixed smooth
comparison comb, and let $B_k$ be the standing budget with reserve
$5/7$.  With
$C_k=\mathcal B_k^{T}\mathcal B_k$,
$(\mathcal B_k)_{yi}=\sqrt{\nu_k(\{y\})}\,p_{k,i}(y)$ and
$u_k=b_k/\sqrt{B_k}$, $b_{k,i}=\int p_{k,i}\,d\sigma_k$, round 460
proved
\begin{equation}\label{eq:spectral}
 \qdag_k=u_k^{T}(I-C_k)^{-1}u_k
 =\sum_j\frac{|\langle u_k,e_{k,j}\rangle|^2}{1-\lambda_{k,j}}
 \qquad\Egrade,
\end{equation}
and $\Rd(W_k)\succeq\half I\iff\qdag_k\le1$.  The target is
$\qdag_k\le1$ for infinitely many $k$.

\paragraph{The measured wall.}
$\lambda_{\max}(C_k)\uparrow1$ fast
($1-\lambda_{\max}=8.7\cdot10^{-5}$ at $k=12$), while the squared
overlap of $u_k$ with the top mode is
$1.5\cdot10^{-6}$ to $9.5\cdot10^{-3}$; the race itself stays in
$[0.606566,0.757783]$ for $k=5,\dots,12$ \Cgrade{} (r459, r460), and
interval certificates now cover $k=5,\dots,16$ \Cgrade{} (r465,
extended r468).  Every bound that treats the data and the modes
separately fails by two to five orders of magnitude: the raw
resolvent norm grows $4.825\to4991.649$, the optimal two-band bound
exceeds $1$ from $k=6$, Gershgorin is negative on every row (r460),
and the exact-coordinate Abel chain overshoots by
$91.703$ to $119482.632$ with the prescribed $10^{3}$ stop gate
firing on $k=7,\dots,12$ (r467).

\paragraph{The protective cancellation is real.}
Three independent controls (r467, r468):
the top modes are low-frequency (share $0.926$--$0.989$) and the
smooth ARCH border aligns with its top mode at
$0.995813$--$0.998970$; a deterministic source-position scramble
\emph{raises} the dangerous normalized top overlap by a factor
$28.5$ to $5.3\cdot10^{5}$; and at $k=10\to11$ the isolated new
prime octave moves $\qdag$ by $+0.2199$ at fixed geometry while the
total step is $-0.01180$.  The cancellation therefore lives in the
joint dependence of $u_k$ and of the eigenbasis of $C_k$ on the same
arithmetic data.

\paragraph{Why every pairing bound must fail (diagnosis, not a bound).}
Since $\int p_{k,i}p_{k,j}\,d\mu_k=\delta_{ij}$, the matrix $C_k$ is
the Gram matrix of $\{p_{k,i}\}$ in $L^2(\nu_k)$ and $I-C_k$ is the
Gram matrix of the \emph{signed} folded measure
$\omega_k=\mu_k-\nu_k$ on $\Pdeg_{<N_k}$.  Restating (A5) of the
dossier and \eqref{eq:spectral} in the dual variational coordinate,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:extremal}
 \qdag_k
 =\frac1{B_k}\,
 \sup_{0\neq p\in\Pdeg_{<N_k}}
 \frac{\bigl(\int p\,d\sigma_k\bigr)^2}{\int p^2\,d\omega_k},
 \qquad
 1-\qdag_k=\frac{\det\mathcal A_k}{\det(I-C_k)}
 \qquad\Egrade,
\end{equation}
with $\mathcal A_k=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}I-C_k&u_k\\
u_k^{T}&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$.  Both forms are one object.  The
supremum in \eqref{eq:extremal} is attained at the
$\omega_k$-Riesz representer of the smooth functional
$p\mapsto\int p\,d\sigma_k$ --- a single vector in which the
arithmetic and the smooth comparison are already fused.  Every route
in the campaign's kill list replaces that one supremum by a
\emph{product of two} suprema (a data majorant times a mode
majorant).  The loss is exactly the alignment that the controls
measure, which is why the observed slack ($10^{2}$--$10^{5}$) is the
same order as the scramble factor ($28.5$--$5.3\cdot10^{5}$).  This
paragraph is a diagnosis of the failure class; it is not an estimate
and must not be counted as progress.

\section{U1 --- the language question}\label{sec:u1}

\subsection{A falsification test for candidate languages}

The r467 controls turn into a methodological gate.

\begin{criterion}[Scramble sensitivity; necessary, methodological]
\label{crit:scramble}
Let $\mathcal M$ be a method producing an upper bound
$\mathcal B_k\ge\qdag_k$ from window data.  If the hypotheses of
$\mathcal M$ are invariant under the r467 source-position scramble
(which preserves channel weights, border atoms, and dimensions, and
changes only the arithmetic positions), then $\mathcal B_k$ is also a
valid bound in the scrambled world, whose dangerous edge alignment is
larger by $28.5$ to $5.3\cdot10^{5}$.  Such an $\mathcal M$ must
therefore absorb that factor, and cannot deliver $\mathcal B_k\le1$
along the sequence while the measured value sits near $0.7$.
\end{criterion}

This is a necessary condition of methodological type, not a theorem:
the scrambled $\qdag$ was not measured, only the alignment it feeds.
Its force is nevertheless decisive as a filter, because
\emph{all} thirty-one closed routes of the dossier
(DCCXLVI--DCCCXXX) plus the r467 Abel chain and the r468 octave
perturbation are scramble-invariant.  That single sentence explains
a decade of the kill table in one stroke, and it is hereby made a
build gate (Section~\ref{sec:u3}).

\subsection{What the target actually is}

\begin{observation}[The residual is not a lemma]\label{obs:rh}
Round 461 proved, from nestedness of the admissible support spaces,
that $\lambda_*(L)=\inf\{Q_{\mathrm W}(h)/\|h\|_2^2:
\operatorname{supp}h\subset[-L,L]\}$ is nonincreasing, so
nonnegativity along \emph{any} unbounded sequence $L_k$ forces
nonnegativity at every finite $L$.  Conditional on the three named
external bridges (density/continuity, explicit-formula
identification, zeta interface) and the two internal identifications,
the requested cofinal statement is therefore equivalent to the full
Weil criterion, hence to RH.  Unconditionally it is at least as
strong as cofinal compact-window positivity of the program's own
three-channel functional.
\end{observation}

Observation~\ref{obs:rh} is not new --- it is r461's own conclusion
and it is printed in the standalone package.  What is new here is the
\emph{judicial} use of it.  Question U1 asks for a language that
proves the residual.  By Observation~\ref{obs:rh} that request is,
up to bridges the program itself classifies as ``known mathematics'',
a request for a proof of RH.  A judge may not answer such a request
by naming a language; a judge may only answer whether any candidate
survives Criterion~\ref{crit:scramble} at bounded cost.  None does.

\subsection{Verdict U1}

\noindent\fbox{\parbox{0.95\linewidth}{%
\textbf{U1 = \texttt{INTERNALLY\_EXHAUSTED}.}\quad
No admissible new language is named.  The reason is structural, not
a lack of imagination: exactly one candidate (c) survives the
scramble filter of Criterion~\ref{crit:scramble}, and executing it
requires the asymptotics of the $\mu_k$-orthonormal polynomials
(the dossier's retained Riemann--Hilbert problem) together with
power-saving cancellation for
$\sum_n\Lambda(n)n^{-1/2+it}$; by Observation~\ref{obs:rh} the
target it would serve is, modulo bridges the program calls known
mathematics, RH itself.  The internal core is therefore closed
\emph{for rounds}.  It is not abandoned: it stays as a documented
external problem (Section~\ref{sec:u2}), and \eqref{eq:extremal}
plus Criterion~\ref{crit:scramble} are what this round adds to it.
No RH claim, no anti-RH claim, and no claim that the residual is
unprovable.}}

\begin{remark}[What would reopen the core]
Three named events, and only these: a proved edge-symbol or
equilibrium-measure asymptotic for $\mu_k$ from outside the
programme; a scramble-sensitive bound on
$\max_r|\sum_{i\le r}u_{k,i}|$ that is derived from the source rather
than measured after the basis change (the verbatim r467 gap); or an
external theorem improving $\lambda_*(L)\ge0$ past the certified
window range, which would make the fixed-$L$ lane
(Section~\ref{sec:u3}) collide with the cofinal one.
\end{remark}

\section{U2 --- the externalization question}\label{sec:u2}

\paragraph{Ruling.}
\texttt{EXTEND\_AND\_REFRAME}.  The standalone package
\path{cofinal_weil_positivity.tex} is the right external object: it
is self-contained, repo-free, graded, and it already carries the
faithful fold, the three equivalent coordinates, the certified
census, and a failed-approach section.  In its present form it is
nevertheless mis-framed in one respect and under-powered in another.
It \emph{asks for a lemma} whose own r461 nestedness argument makes
it RH-equivalent, and it omits the only genuinely new content the
programme produced this week: a measured, reproducible mechanism plus
a filter that explains the entire kill list.

\paragraph{What to add (five items, in this order).}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A strength warning, on page one.}  State
Observation~\ref{obs:rh} explicitly: after the three bridges the
requested statement is equivalent to the full Weil criterion.  Offer
it as a \emph{precisely located finite-matrix form of} RH, never as a
lemma below it.  This protects the document from both readings that
would discard it --- naive, or an RH claim in disguise.
\item \textbf{The wall anatomy} (r467, r468) as one section: the
three controls with their numbers (low-frequency share
$0.926$--$0.989$; ARCH alignment $0.995813$--$0.998970$; scramble
factor $28.5$--$5.3\cdot10^{5}$; isolated octave $+0.2199$ against
total $-0.01180$), the extremal identity \eqref{eq:extremal}, and the
diagnosis of Section~\ref{sec:object}.
\item \textbf{Criterion~\ref{crit:scramble} as the attacker's
filter}, with the honest label that it is methodological.  This is
the highest-value paragraph for an external specialist: it says which
methods are dead before any work is done.
\item \textbf{The compressed kill list}: the thirty-one closed
routes plus r467 and r468, one line each with the killing number.
Attacker gold, and it is what makes the document a contribution
rather than a request.
\item \textbf{The fixed-window prize, as a clearly separated
section}: the interval-certified base $k=5,\dots,16$ with its
widths, and the \emph{conditional} classical reading
$\lambda_*(L_k)\ge0$ up to $L_{16}=k\log2/4=2.77$, printed together
with the exact list of what must be closed to make it unconditional
(Section~\ref{sec:u3}, F1).  Presented as a second, strictly weaker,
finite target --- because it is the part an outsider can actually
help with.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Where to send it.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{arXiv}, math.NT primary with math.CA cross-list, as a
problem-and-anatomy note (not as a result).  The kill list and the
filter are the citable content.
\item \emph{Direct correspondence}, three groups: the author of the
compact-window certificate preprint (same technology, complementary
coordinate --- they hold the classical side, this programme holds the
deeper certified windows); workers on Weil positivity and the
archimedean place; and specialists in discrete orthogonal-polynomial
duality and Riemann--Hilbert asymptotics, for candidate (c)/(d),
which is precisely the retained RHP problem.
\item \emph{A specialist mathematics forum}, with the question
\textbf{stripped of all RH framing}: a Markov-type inequality between
an explicit signed folded measure and an explicit smooth comb on a
polynomial window, in the form \eqref{eq:extremal}.  Judge's estimate:
this is the highest-yield channel, because it removes the single
feature that makes competent readers stop.
\item \emph{The Lean community channel}, for the two named
formalization tasks only (Section~\ref{sec:u3}, F1(i)--(ii)).
\end{itemize}
The repository package stays the reproducible appendix; no external
document may depend on it.

\paragraph{What must not be claimed.}
No RH claim and no anti-RH claim.  The mechanism is not evidence for
RH and must never be phrased as such.  The finite census and the
interval certificates are not progress toward RH; they are finite
statements about finite matrices.  The residual must not be called a
lemma.  The compact-window preprint must not be cited as a theorem.
The classical improvement of $\lambda_*(L)$ must not be claimed
before F1(i)--(iii) are closed --- until then it is conditional and
must be printed with its conditions in the same sentence.  Nothing in
the anatomy may be described as explaining ``why RH is true''.

\section{U3 --- the resource directive}\label{sec:u3}

\subsection{Core: empty}

Because U1 is \texttt{INTERNALLY\_EXHAUSTED}, no round may target
$\qdag_k\le1$ for infinitely many $k$, in any coordinate.  The core
survives as an external problem only.  There is no core contract.

\subsection{Foundation, in priority order}

\paragraph{F1 (first).  The unconditional classical theorem at fixed
$L$.}
This is the directive's centre.  The programme has spent its recent
rounds on the cofinal statement, which
Observation~\ref{obs:rh} shows to be RH, while a genuinely new
\emph{unconditional} classical result sits behind three tasks that
are individually finite or standard:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] close \code{SelectedReadQuadraticRepresentation} --- the
single named remainder of r464, a precise finite structure problem
about the channel map under unbounded \code{GridElement.steps};
\item[(ii)] close \code{arch_gauss_mellin_digamma_identity} ---
classical digamma/Mellin analysis, r464 already collapsed the
archimedean gap to this one identity; formalization work, preceded by
a Mathlib survey;
\item[(iii)] prove the density and continuity step \emph{at fixed
support length only}: dyadic step autocorrelations are dense in
$\{h\in L^2:\operatorname{supp}h\subset[-L,L]\}$ and $Q_{\mathrm W}$
is continuous there.  At fixed $L$ this is standard analysis; it is
the cofinal version, and only that, which is hard.
\end{enumerate}
Payoff: for a \emph{single} $k$ the r465/r468 interval certificate
becomes an unconditional statement $\lambda_*(L_k)\ge0$.  At the
currently certified top, $k=16$, that is $L_{16}=2.77$ against the
$0.8$ of the current external certificate --- an improvement of the
certified compact-window range by roughly a factor $3.5$, with no
cofinal quantifier anywhere.  Why first: it is the only place in the
programme where existing rigorous certificates convert into a
classical, citable, non-RH result, and it is entirely independent of
the wall.

\emph{First-round contract (Foundation, not Core).}  One round, one
worker at implementation tier, scope limited to F1(i): produce either
a proof of \code{SelectedReadQuadraticRepresentation} or a sealed
statement of the exact finite obstruction, with no new
\code{sorry} and no change to any other file; deliverable is the Lean
change plus one \texttt{rh/problem} note and an INVENTORY row; exit
gate is the RH suite green and the sorry census honestly reported.
Explicitly out of scope: any statement about infinitely many $k$.

\paragraph{F2 (parallel, cheap).  Certificates in the classical
coordinate.}
Reproduce the external $L=0.8$ certificate independently, then
certify $\lambda_*(L)\ge0$ directly at $L_5=0.866434$ and upward
using the r465 interval machinery \emph{in the classical
coordinate}, i.e.\ without the fold dictionary.  This makes F1's
payoff dictionary-independent, and it is the one lane whose value
does not depend on any open bridge at all.

\paragraph{F3.  Spectral side, fixed-$L$ form only.}
The Guinand--Weil identification needed to complete F1(iii).
Formalize it at fixed $L$.  Do not formalize the cofinal quantifier;
by Observation~\ref{obs:rh} that is RH and does not belong in a
formalization backlog.

\paragraph{F4.  Lean hygiene, continuous.}
The \code{sorry} census stays honest at eight and decreases only by
real closures.  No new \code{sorry} without a round reference.  Every
closure gets an INVENTORY row and a \code{run_rh.py} gate in the same
change.

\paragraph{Deliberately unfunded.}
The Riemann--Hilbert/steepest-descent route and the retained
minimal-inertia problem stay documented and unfunded.  They are the
correct external asks (Section~\ref{sec:u2}), not rounds.

\subsection{Anti-list (binding; r303 discipline)}

\begin{enumerate}
\item No further majorant, operator-norm, Gershgorin, triangle,
Abel/summation-by-parts, or two-band variant.  The class is
exhausted, and its failure is now explained by
Criterion~\ref{crit:scramble} rather than merely recorded.
\item No restatement of the $\infty$-quantifier: block limits,
octave Cauchy rates, density of good $k$, plateau arguments,
subsequence extraction.  Alias by construction (r460, r468, r303).
\item \textbf{Mandatory scramble gate.}  No probe may be built until
its proposed bound is declared scramble-sensitive, with the reason
written down.  A scramble-invariant proposal is refused before any
code is written.
\item No round on candidates (a), (b), (e), (f), (g), (h) of
Section~\ref{sec:u1} without first exhibiting the missing hypothesis
(small parameter, Gibbs potential, independence, uniform minorant)
and showing that it survives item 3.
\item No round that requires the RH-asymptotics of the
$\mu_k$-orthonormal polynomials.  That is the retained RHP problem:
an external ask, not a round.
\item No further anatomy of the same wall without a named bound
target in the round contract.  Anatomy is cheap and it is now
sufficient; bounds are what is missing.
\item No RH claim, no anti-RH claim, no ``evidence'' language, and no
promotion of any census or interval certificate to a statement about
$\zeta$.
\end{enumerate}
