\large P2 as alternation, P1 as a single prefix defect,\\

\begin{abstract}\noindent
On an odd cosine grid of $S=2N-1$ nodes the two Haynsworth
premises of the dual two-rank cut translate, by a finite Hankel
dictionary, into statements about the monic Jacobi norms
$h_j=H_{j+1}(w)/H_j(w)$ of the original signed weight $w$:
\[
\det K_2<0\iff h_N(w)\,h_{N+1}(w)<0,
\qquad
\indm H_{N+2}(w)\le 1.
\]
The first equivalence is a ratio of Hankel determinants, checked
in $\mathbb{Q}$ on a five-atom toy ($\det K_2=-196/35719$ on both
routes).  It does \emph{not} hold as a law of every canonical
window: $35/85$ MAIN rows have $h_N h_{N+1}>0$, with the first
negative pivot at offset $2$, $3$, or later (explicit
counterexample $kz=12$, first defect at $N+2$, $\det K_2=+12.46$,
$A_0$ already positive definite).  On the complementary
nneg-$1$ branch the product is negative on $50/50$ MAIN rows, and
the unique prefix defect sits in $\{h_N,h_{N+1}\}$.  The node
polynomial $P_X(x)=2^{1-S}(x+1)U_{S-1}(x)$ and the Chebyshev
aliasing $T_{S+k}(x_j)=(-1)^j T_k(x_j)$ are theorems; they do not
yield the sign of $h_N h_{N+1}$ without a free-window Gram inverse.
The weak inertia bound $\indm H_{N+2}\le 1$ holds on $85/85$ MAIN
and $84/84$ character-twisted windows, and fails on a matched
scramble (first defect at degree $21$, $37$ negatives in the
prefix).  Nothing in this document is evidence for or against
the Riemann hypothesis.
\end{abstract}

Lemmas~\ref{lem:dpp}--\ref{lem:alias-T} are proved (finite

Lemma~\ref{lem:adv} is proved (a $2\times 2$ adversary).
Proposition~\ref{prop:A-refute} is a named counterexample, not a
cofinal law.
Proposition~\ref{prop:census} is a finite machine census of $85$
MAIN and $84$ character-twisted windows.
Goal~A as stated --- $h_N h_{N+1}<0$ on every canonical window ---
is \emph{refuted}.
Goal~B --- $\indm H_{N+2}(w)\le 1$ --- is reduced to a
construction census: it holds on every tested cosine-tent window
and fails on scramble; a source Sturm uniqueness theorem was not
obtained.
No RH claim.

\section{The object}\label{sec:obj}

Let $X=\{x_j=\cos(\pi j/S):j=1,\ldots,S\}$ with $S=2N-1$ odd, and
let $w$ be a real atomic weight on $X$ (the folded signed
von-Mangoldt tent density times the mesh factor $1-x$).  Write
$H_r(\rho)$ for the $r\times r$ Hankel determinant of a weight
$\rho$ on $X$, $H_0:=1$, and
\[
h_j(\rho)=\frac{H_{j+1}(\rho)}{H_j(\rho)}
\]
for the monic Jacobi norms whenever the minors are nonzero.
The dual positive weight is $a(x)=1/(|w(x)|P_X'(x)^2)$ and
$\sigma a$ is its sign-dressed twin, $\sigma=\operatorname{sign} w$.

On the dual CD restriction $R_r$ to the negative atoms $Y$, the
Haynsworth two-rank cut at $r=N-3$ has premises
\begin{itemize}
\item[(P1)] $\indm\bigl(R_{N-3}-\tfrac12 I\bigr)=1$
  (exactly one negative direction of $A_0$),
\item[(P2)] $\det K_2<0$ with $K_2=I_2+U^T A_0^{-1}U$.
\end{itemize}
A weaker form of the first premise, used below as Goal~B, is
$\indm\bigl(R_{N-3}-\tfrac12 I\bigr)\le 1$.  When the index is
zero, $A_0$ is already positive definite and the Loewner update
gives $R_{N-1}\succ\tfrac12 I$ without (P2).

\section{The dictionary}\label{sec:dict}

\begin{lemma}[DPP and complement]\label{lem:dpp}
In the $a$-orthonormal polynomial basis,
$\det(I-2R_r)=H_r(\sigma a)/H_r(a)$.
The signed Borodin identity
$H_r(\sigma a)=H_{S-r}(w)/(\Delta(X)^2\prod w)$
holds for every rank $r$.  Consequently, at $r=N-3$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:detk}
\det K_2
=\frac{H_N(w)\,H_{N-3}(a)}{H_{N+2}(w)\,H_{N-1}(a)}
=\frac1{h_N(w)\,h_{N+1}(w)\,h_{N-3}(a)\,h_{N-2}(a)}.
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The first identity is Sylvester plus the change to the
orthonormal basis of $a$ (the discrete OPE parity expectation).
The second is Cauchy--Binet on the Vandermonde embedding of a
signed atomic measure, the finite-set dual of~\cite{borodin}.
Substitute $S=2N-1$ and $r=N-3$, and rewrite consecutive Hankel
ratios as $h_j$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Sign dictionary]\label{lem:sign}
If $a>0$ (all dual Hankels positive) and the named minors are
nonzero, then $\det K_2<0$ if and only if $h_N(w)\,h_{N+1}(w)<0$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Immediate from~\eqref{eq:detk}.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Complementary inertia]\label{lem:iner}
$\In H_k(w)+\In H_{S-k}(\sigma a)=\In\operatorname{diag}(w)$
for $1\le k\le S-1$.  For $r\ge |Y|$,
$\indm(R_r-\tfrac12 I)=\indm H_{S-r}(w)$.  In particular
(P1) at rank $N-3$ is equivalent to $\indm H_{N+2}(w)\le 1$
in the weak form, and to equality with $1$ on the nneg-$1$
branch.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Vandermonde congruence plus a Schur complement whose inverse
block is, after an invertible triangular change of basis, the
complementary dual Hankel.  The identification with
$R_r-\tfrac12 I$ is the SVD of the rank-$r$ projector on $Y$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Nonzero is not enough]\label{lem:adv}
There exist $A=\operatorname{diag}(-2,1)$ and a two-column $U$
with $\indm A=1$ and $\det K_2=\tfrac12\neq 0$ for which
$A+UU^T$ remains indefinite.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Take $U=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$.
Then $K_2=\operatorname{diag}(\tfrac12,1)$ and
$M=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1)$.
\end{proof}

On the nneg-$1$ branch, $\det K_2<0$ is equivalent to
$M\succ 0$ by the determinant lemma $\det M=\det A\,\det K_2$
together with Loewner ($\indm M\le 1$) and the parity of a
nonzero determinant.  That circularity is real; it is not a
collapse of (P2) onto (P1) plus nonvanishing
(Lemma~\ref{lem:adv}).

\section{Aliasing on the cosine grid}\label{sec:alias}

\begin{lemma}[Node polynomial]\label{lem:PX}
The monic node polynomial of $X$ is
$P_X(x)=2^{1-S}(x+1)U_{S-1}(x)$.  It vanishes on every node,
including $x=-1=\cos\pi$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$U_{S-1}(\cos\theta)=\sin(S\theta)/\sin\theta$ has simple zeros
at $\theta=\pi j/S$ for $j=1,\ldots,S-1$.  The remaining node
$\theta=\pi$ is the factor $x+1$.  The leading coefficient of
$U_{S-1}$ is $2^{S-1}$, so $2^{1-S}(x+1)U_{S-1}$ is monic of
degree $S$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Chebyshev aliasing]\label{lem:alias-T}
On $X$, $T_{S+k}(x_j)=(-1)^j T_k(x_j)$ for every $k\ge 0$.
In particular $T_S(x_j)=(-1)^j$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$T_n(\cos\theta)=\cos(n\theta)$ and
$\theta_j=\pi j/S$, so
$\cos((S+k)\theta_j)=\cos(\pi j+k\theta_j)=(-1)^j\cos(k\theta_j)$.
\end{proof}

The power-moment sequence of any atomic measure on $X$ is
linearly recurrent of order $S$ with characteristic polynomial
$P_X$.  The first two post-cap norms $h_N,h_{N+1}$ are exactly
the first two Jacobi steps that consume recurrent (aliased)
moments: $H_N$ uses the complete independent window
$m_0,\ldots,m_{S-1}$, while $H_{N+1}$ and $H_{N+2}$ use
$m_S,\ldots,m_{S+3}$.  The classical bordered identity
\[
h_N=m_{2N}-v^T H_N^{-1}v
\]
is this step in the monomial basis.  It is a free-window Gram
inverse --- the kill criterion of the aliasing route.  Live
power-moment recurrences at $S=367$ cancel in IEEE-754;
the trigonometric form of Lemma~\ref{lem:PX} is exact
($\max|P_X|\le 10^{-123}$ on the construction grid).

A low-mode density $f=1+0.3\cos\theta+0.1\cos 2\theta$ on $S=5$
produces a strictly positive weight times $(1-x)$ and therefore
$h_j>0$ for all $j$: post-cap alternation is not a theorem of
the cosine mesh alone.  The sign, when it occurs, is carried by
the high Fourier content of the tent comb.  No identity was
found that reads that sign from the two Nyquist DCT blocks
without inverting the free-window Gram.

\section{Goal A, refuted as stated}\label{sec:A}

\begin{prop}[Universal post-cap alternation is false]\label{prop:A-refute}
There exist canonical windows with $h_N(w)\,h_{N+1}(w)>0$.
On $kz=12$ one has $N=151$, $h_0,\ldots,h_{152}>0$,
$h_{153}<0$, $\det K_2=+12.4632$, and $A_0\succ 0$.
\end{prop}

The same pattern --- first defect at offset $2$, $3$, $4$, $5$,
or $8$ --- occurs on $35/85$ MAIN rows (classes LATE and NONE of
the census).  The twelve NONE rows, hunted to $N+16$, have
offsets $\{4,5,8\}$:
$kz\in\{50,73,96,72,113\}$ at $4$;
$kz\in\{43,42,115,89,133\}$ at $5$;
$kz\in\{107,117\}$ at $8$.
It is compatible with half-filling positivity
$h_0,\ldots,h_{N-1}>0$, which holds on $85/85$ MAIN and $84/84$
character-twisted rows.  The eighteen $\delta=0$ core windows
with $h_N<0$ (r241) are exactly the PINNED-$N$ subclass of the
core $42$.

On the nneg-$1$ branch ($\indm H_{N+2}=1$) the unique prefix
defect lies in $\{h_N,h_{N+1}\}$ and the product is negative, on
$50/50$ MAIN rows ($19$ at $N$, $31$ at $N+1$).  That is the
content of (P2) after Lemma~\ref{lem:sign}.  It is a finite
census, not a source sign theorem: the aliasing identities of
\S\ref{sec:alias} do not evaluate the product.

\section{Goal B, reduced to a construction census}\label{sec:B}

A second negative pivot in $h_0,\ldots,h_{N+1}$ would be a second
sign change of the Sturm sequence of monic norms (LDL of the
signed Hankel).  The three-term recurrence
$\pi_{n+1}=(x-\alpha_n)\pi_n-\gamma_n\pi_{n-1}$ with
$\gamma_n=h_n/h_{n-1}$ makes every sign of $h$ a recurrence
coefficient, but does not by itself forbid a second change: a
generic signed atomic measure (the five-atom toy, or a matched
scramble) has many early negatives.

\begin{prop}[Prefix census]\label{prop:census}
On the $85$ MAIN windows, $\indm H_{N+2}(w)\le 1$ holds $85/85$,
with $50$ rows equal to $1$ and $35$ equal to $0$
(PINNED-$N$ $19$, PINNED-$N{+}1$ $31$, LATE $23$, NONE $12$;
DOUBLE/EARLY/MULTI $0$).  On the $84$ character-twisted windows
the same bound holds $84/84$ ($\chi_3$: PINNED-$N$ $8$,
PINNED-$N{+}1$ $13$, LATE $18$, NONE $3$; $\chi_4$: $4$, $15$,
$23$, $0$).  Defects on $\chi$ wander through offsets $0..3$
(and three $\chi_3$ NONE).  The six terminal-dead character
rungs ($q_N>1$) still satisfy the bound
($\chi_3$: $15$/$19$ PINNED-$N{+}1$, $23$/$33$ LATE, $39$ PINNED-$N$;
$\chi_4$-$20$ LATE); terminal death is not a prefix-pivot failure.
A matched scramble at $w=9$ breaks the bound (first defect at
degree $21$, $37$ negatives among $h_0,\ldots,h_{185}$).
No row of the cosine-tent family realises two negatives in
$\{h_N,h_{N+1}\}$.
\end{prop}

Rank-one Loewner monotonicity
$\indm(A_{n+1})\in\{\indm(A_n),\indm(A_n)-1\}$
(Fable, Weyl plus interlacing) then spreads the bound from rank
$N-3$ through the tail.  The missing source lemma is a reason
that the cosine-tent density cannot produce a second prefix
sign change.  Saturation bulk coercivity
($x\perp\psi_{\mathrm{edge}}\Rightarrow x^T A_{n_0}x>0$) was
not needed: the census never entered a two-defect prefix, so
there was no second mode to coerce.

\section{Outcomes}\label{sec:out}

\paragraph{Goal A (P2-POSTCAP).}
\emph{Refuted} as the universal statement $h_N h_{N+1}<0$ on
every canonical window (Proposition~\ref{prop:A-refute},
$35$ MAIN counterexamples).
\emph{Reduced}, on the nneg-$1$ branch, to a $50/50$ census of
alternation at $\{h_N,h_{N+1}\}$, with the exact dictionary
Lemma~\ref{lem:sign} and with aliasing stopped at the
$H_N^{-1}$ kill.

\paragraph{Goal B (P1-SINGLE-DEFECT).}
\emph{Reduced} to Proposition~\ref{prop:census}:
$\indm H_{N+2}\le 1$ on every tested cosine-tent window,
named-broken by scramble, not proved from the Sturm recurrence.
The bound is not zeta-specific (character-twisted and
terminal-dead rungs obey it).

\paragraph{What remains.}
A source reason that the first aliased Jacobi step of a
cosine-tent weight is a \emph{simple} Nyquist crossing on the
nneg-$1$ branch, without inverting $H_N$.  The dictionary, the
node polynomial, and the Chebyshev wrap are no longer the
obstruction.
