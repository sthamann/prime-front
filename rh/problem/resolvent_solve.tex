\begin{abstract}\noindent
Route (a) of r486: invert $R=A^++\Pi$ on the
certified enemy modes of $K_{t_c}$.
\textbf{Coercivity first.}
$\lambda_{\min}(R)$ on the Nystr\"om space collapses
with $n$: $0.337$ ($n=48$, $m=8$),
$0.106$ ($n=96$, $m=20$),
$0.076$ ($n=128$, $m=16$).
$A^+$ vanishes on $|t|<t_c$; $\Pi$ lifts one
direction.  $R$ is positive semi-definite but not
coercive.  Shift $R_s=R+s$, $A^-_s=A^-+s$ with
$s=0.05$ is mandatory; $R_s\succeq s\,I$ as forms.
The Birman--Schwinger inequality is
shift-invariant ($\QW\succeq0$ iff $\mathrm{BS}\le1$
for every $s$); $s$ only converts residual to
inverse error, $\|u-R_s^{-1}\varphi\|\le r/s$.
$s\to0$ recovers r486 ($0.993651$).
At $s=0.05$ the correct $G$-certificate
$\lambda_{\max}(G^{1/2}BG^{1/2})$
with $G=P_ER_s^{-1}P_E$ is
$0.99415$ ($N=3$) and $0.99439$ ($N=5$).
Discrete residual in $V_n$ is $2\cdot10^{-16}$.
$N=3$ plus $|\sigma_A(0)|\cdot\mathrm{rest}/s$
overshoots ($1.078$) and is not a SATZ pad.
$N=5$ tail $5.5\cdot10^{-7}$ is negligible.
The discrete $G$ is the inverse of the $T=150$
Galerkin $R$, not of the operator $R$ on $L^2$.
Verdict
\texttt{REDUCED(R-not-coercive; s=0.05-G-cert-N5-BS=0.994; operator-R-gap-open)}.
Fourier--HS is not reused.
Yoshida--Bombieri zone.  No RH claim.
