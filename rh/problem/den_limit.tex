\section{The lemma}\label{sec:lem}

\begin{lemma}[$\mathrm{den}$ limit]\label{lem:den}
On the selected sequence $a_k=2^k$,
$\mathrm{den}_k\to\mathrm{den}_\infty<2$ with
$\mathrm{den}_\infty$ source-pure
(or at least $\limsup\mathrm{den}_k\le\bar d<2$
with $\bar d$ explicit).
\end{lemma}

\noindent
\textbf{Verdict: REDUZIERT.}
Hierarchy (i) --- a constructed limit --- is not
reached.
Hierarchy (ii) --- $\gamma\le\bar\gamma$ as a chain
theorem --- is a universal census $\gamma<1$, not a
SATZ; $\|b\|^2$ is not a subsum of the $\rho$-series.
Hierarchy (iii): on core-$42$ plus EXT plus selected,
$\mathrm{den}\le 1.652<1.66$ and the gap to $2$ is
at least $0.348$.

\section{The formula}\label{sec:form}

\begin{prop}[border normalisation]\label{prop:den}
$\mathrm{den}=1+\gamma-v_t\cdot s$ with
$\gamma=\|b\|^2/B_w$ and $B_w=S_{N-2}+\tfrac57$.
Over $\mathbb{Q}$, $\gamma=\tfrac23<1$,
$v_t\cdot s=\tfrac16>0$ gives $\mathrm{den}=\tfrac32<2$.
Even $v_t\cdot s=0$ would give $1+\gamma=\tfrac53<2$.
At $k_z=9$,
$\mathrm{den}=1.60111$, $\gamma=0.6778$,
$v_t\cdot s=0.0767$, $B_w=8.382$.
\end{prop}

Thus $\mathrm{den}<2$ follows from the formula as
soon as $\gamma<1$.
The correction $v_t\cdot s$ only widens the gap.

\section{The bound that is actually in hand}\label{sec:bound}

On the named census,
$\gamma\le 0.724$ and $v_t\cdot s\ge 0.067$, so
\[
\mathrm{den}\;\le\;1+0.724-0.067\;=\;1.657\;<\;2.
\]
The measured maximum is $1.652$; the gap to $2$ is
at least $0.348$.
An honest explicit $\bar d$ is the census number
$1.66$, not a source-pure constant.

\section{What is a theorem, what is a census}\label{sec:honest}

Proposition~\ref{prop:den} is a theorem.
Lemma~\ref{lem:den} is not:
$\mathrm{den}_\infty$ is not built,
$\gamma<1$ is not a chain identity,
and $\|b\|^2\le B_w$ is observed rather than
proved.
The cofinal edge still needs a constructed
$\mathrm{den}_\infty$ or a proof of $\gamma<1$.
No RH claim.
