\section{Topology}

The r487 carrier is corrected to use an explicit compactly supported
\(L^2(\mathbb R)\) witness \(h\), rather than merely \(L^1\), with
\[
 g(u)=\int_{\mathbb R}h(t)h(t+u)\,dt .
\]
For a target supported in \([-R,R]\), a grid sequence \(g_j\) now
satisfies:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(\operatorname{supp}g_j\subseteq[-R,R]\);
\item \(g_j\to g\) uniformly on \([-R,R]\);
\item every \(g_j\) is interval-integrable there;
\item \(\int_{-R}^{R}|g_j-g|\to0\).
\end{enumerate}
Uniform convergence is the right input for the finite set of prime
point evaluations.  The explicit \(L^1\) component is retained because
it gives bounded-weight integral continuity without deriving it again
from uniform convergence and interval length.

\section{Prime channel: closed}

Define the common support anchor
\[
 A_R=\max\{1,\lceil e^R\rceil\}.
\]
Lean proves \(\operatorname{elementAnchor}(g_j)\le A_R\), hence every
native prime side is the same finite sum
\[
 \sum_{n\in\operatorname{windowAtoms}(A_R)}
       \frac{2\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\,g_j(\log n).
\]
Global pointwise convergence follows from uniform convergence on the
support and simultaneous vanishing outside it.  The theorem
\code{fullWeilCombSide_tendsto} then follows from
\code{tendsto_finset_sum}.  This channel is sorry-free.

\section{Polar channel: continuity closed, dictionary exposed}

The standard compact-support polar functional is now concrete:
\[
 P(g)=\int_{-R}^{R}-2\cosh(u/2)\,g(u)\,du .
\]
On \([-R,R]\), its weight is bounded by
\(2\cosh(R/2)\).  Lean proves
\[
 |P(g_j)-P(g)|\le
 2\cosh(R/2)\int_{-R}^{R}|g_j(u)-g(u)|\,du
 \longrightarrow0
\]
as \code{fullWeilPoleIntegral_tendsto}.

The internal r376 object \code{weilPoleSide} is, however, still the
native-mesh second-difference pairing of \code{polePotential}.  Its
finite-element equality with \(P(g_j)\) is not definitional and was
already represented by the unproved mesh dictionary in r376.  R489
names its exact landing site
\code{GridPoleIntegralIdentification}; no equality is silently assumed.

\section{Single remaining completion}

The dense bridge now has one direct sorry,
\code{fullWeil_fixedSupport_completion}, whose proposition is the
transparent conjunction:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \code{FullWeilFixedSupportGridDensity}: dyadic step approximation
of compactly supported \(L^2\) witnesses and uniform convergence of
their autocorrelations;
\item \code{FullWeilArchContinuity}: continuity of r475's concrete
\(u\)-space archimedean pairing;
\item \code{GridPoleIntegralIdentification}: the r376
second-difference/integral finite-element dictionary.
\end{enumerate}
The public density and channel-continuity theorems are projections and
compositions of this single package; the prime and standard polar
continuity subproofs are already closed.

For the arch component, Gauss--digamma is not required merely to prove
continuity of the concrete r475 \(u\)-space pairing.  It remains needed
when that pairing is identified with the standard spectral
Guinand--Weil normalization in the separate explicit-formula bridge.

\section{Mathlib status and census}

Mathlib supplies \(L^p\) spaces, Hölder inequalities, Bochner
integration, interval-integral bounds, simple functions, and group
convolution.  No directly reusable theorem was found that simultaneously
produces compactly supported dyadic steps and proves uniform convergence
of translated autocorrelations.  The intended next proof is
\[
\|h_j\star\widetilde h_j-h\star\widetilde h\|_\infty
\le(\|h_j\|_2+\|h\|_2)\|h_j-h\|_2,
\]
after a support-preserving dyadic \(L^2\)-density lemma.

The external sorry census decreases from four to three and the
repository census from \(9\) to \(8\): the two r487 density/channel
sorries are replaced by one completion sorry.  The other external
sorries remain explicit-formula normalization and off-critical
separation.
