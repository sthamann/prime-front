\title{\bfseries Isolation of the fold mask, refuted\\[2mm]
\large Pair counts are small but not a SATZ, isolated masks
do not close $\Delta^2\log\tau$, and Gershgorin stays open}
\author{Stefan Hamann}
\date{August 28, 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}\noindent
Round 395 refuted three-gap of the $\nu$-mask and left a
$2$--$3$ dominated histogram with two $F_1$ pairs on FRAME-A
and a sparse large-gap tail.  The candidate closing object was
\emph{isolation}: gap-$1$ pairs source-countably rare, so the
$2\times2$ Uvarov blocks are finitely many explicit terms and
the isolated singleton chain plus a thin tail sits in the
$\Delta^2\log\tau$ box and has $\lambda_{\max}<1$.
What is proved: folded consecutive small integers are not
adjacent on the fold circle (PNT-free, log monotone);
wrapping $\{2,3,4\}$ is isolated; pair counts on named windows
are machine identities; the Dirichlet envelope at angular
separation $2$ is still $\gg 1$.
What is \emph{refuted}: $P(N)/n_\nu\to 0$ or $P\le$ a fixed
constant (core-$42$ has up to $16$ pairs, EXT $kz=97$ has $22$,
density stays $O(0.03)$); that pairs are small-$n$ log
collisions (an atom $n=e^{u}\approx 211$ near the cap lands on
a pair bin); that isolated masks with a thin tail imply the
box ($h=40$: $3/12$ IN; the r395 $8/8$ at $h=80$ is an
$n_{\mathrm{ref}}=60$ truncation, and dies at natural depth
$2/8$); that isolation implies $\lambda_{\max}<1$ ($1010$);
that isolation plus the Dirichlet hull closes Gershgorin
($\mathrm{gersh}=1.77$).  No RH claim.
\end{abstract}

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Status.}\enspace
Lemmas~\ref{lem:foldsmall}--\ref{lem:hull} are proved (finite
identities).
Propositions~\ref{prop:count}--\ref{prop:assist} name the
refutation.  No RH claim.  No $L^*$ claim.

\section{Recollection}\label{sec:rec}

Occupied Fej\'er deletes the $\nu$-support $\Xi$ from the
discrete Bernstein--Szeg\H{o} cosine grid~\cite{r393,r395}.
Under $F_1$ the clusters are singletons and adjacent pairs;
$\tau$ splits into $1\times1$ Sherman--Morrison factors,
$2\times2$ pair blocks, and a coupling determinant
$\kappa$~\cite{r393}.
r395 showed the mask is not three-gap and that wrapping
$\{2,3,4\}$ (isolated singles) sat in the box on $8/8$ seeds
at $h=80$, $n_{\mathrm{ref}}=60$, with
$\lambda_{\max}(E_{n_0})=0.997$, while wrapping $\{1,2,3\}$
(pair-rich) and random $F_1$ did not.
The candidate: the source has only two pairs on FRAME-A, so
isolation is the class and the two $2\times2$ terms are a
finite remainder.

\section{Pair counts, packing, not a smallness SATZ}\label{sec:count}

\begin{lemma}[folded small integers are not adjacent]\label{lem:foldsmall}
Let $L=734$ and $x_n=\bigl\lfloor L\{\log_2 n\}\bigr\rfloor$,
folded $f_n=\min(x_n,L-x_n)$.
For $n=2,\ldots,63$ no consecutive pair $(n,n+1)$ is adjacent
on the fold line ($0$ adjacencies).
PNT-free: only monotonicity of $\log$.
\end{lemma}

So gap-$1$ on the cosine grid is \emph{not} the small-$n$
log-collision $n<e^{1/\delta}$.

\begin{lemma}[PNT-free packing is $O(n_{\mathrm{atom}})$]\label{lem:pack}
On FRAME-A the $70$ source log-atoms have $24$ consecutive
pairs with $\Delta u<2D$ ($D$ the mesh).
This is a packing identity, not a smallness SATZ:
$P/n_\nu\not\to 0$ from log-density alone.
\end{lemma}

\begin{prop}[pair census]\label{prop:count}
FRAME-A $w=9$: $2$ pairs on $104$ atoms (density $0.019$),
locations $(13,14)$ and $(67,68)$.
An atom $n=e^{u}\approx 211$ (near the cap $X=256$) lands on a
pair bin; $n\approx 97$ sits at fold-distance $2$.
Atom-fold adjacencies are $18$ (same-bin $9$) against $2$
cosine pairs --- aggregation, not a Steinhaus remainder.
Core-$42$: pair counts in $[0,16]$ (median $4$), density
$\le 0.0405$, $3/42$ zero-pair windows.
EXT $kz=97$: $22$ pairs on $795$ atoms, density $0.0277$.
Dead $\chi$ sample: densities $0.040,0.039,0,0.041$.
Thus $P$ is not a fixed constant and $P/n_\nu$ stays
$O(0.03)$, not $\to 0$ on the named range.
\end{prop}

\section{Isolation does not imply the box}\label{sec:box}

\begin{prop}[discriminator]\label{prop:disc}
Wrapping $\{2,3,4\}$ is isolated (pairs $=0$, max run $=1$,
$F_1$).
At $h=40$, $n_{\mathrm{ref}}=40$: $3/12$ seeds IN --- not a
class SATZ.
At $h=80$, $n_{\mathrm{ref}}=60$: $8/8$ IN (r395 pin).
At $h=80$, $n_{\mathrm{ref}}=80$: $2/8$ IN.
The $8/8$ is a truncation census, not isolation.
Denser isolated wrapping $\{2,2,3\}$: $0/8$ IN.
Sparser $\{3,4,5\}$: $5/8$ IN.
Fill, not the pair counter, moves the box.
Injecting $k$ pairs into wrapping $\{2,3,4\}$ at $h=40$:
$k=1$ already $0/8$ IN; $k=10$ jump up to $3.86$.
The source's two pairs is not a closing threshold on this
class.
Punching a fat gap ($d_{\max}\sim 60$): $6/8$ IN against
$1/8$ for the thin wrap --- a fat tail \emph{helps}.
Source $d_{\max}/d_{\min}=23$ is not the kill.
Wrapping $\{1,2,3\}$ (density $0.22$) and random $F_1$
(density $0.42$) stay OUT.
On core-$42$ the source is $42/42$ IN at JUMP and
$\mathrm{corr}(\#\mathrm{pairs},\mathrm{jump})=-0.38$:
pair count does not drive the source jump.
\end{prop}

The singleton $1\times1$ chain cannot be a SATZ of the box:
the isolated class itself does not sit in the box at natural
depth.  The finitely many $2\times2$ terms on the source are
real, and do not explain $F_\varepsilon$.

\section{Assist: isolation does not close Gershgorin}\label{sec:assist}

\begin{lemma}[hull still huge]\label{lem:hull}
On the $h=40$ cosine grid ($S=79$),
$1/\lvert\sin\Delta\theta\rvert=12.59$ at angular separation
$2$.
The r394 envelope $\lvert D_n(a)\rvert\le 1/\lvert\sin(a/2)\rvert$
does not become useful under isolation.
\end{lemma}

\begin{prop}[Assist]\label{prop:assist}
At $h=40$, $n_0=16$: wrapping $\{2,3,4\}$ has
$\lambda_{\max}=0.9968<1$ but Gershgorin $1.768>1$ and
row-amplification $g_A=6.10$.
$1010$ is isolated and has $\lambda_{\max}=1.405>1$.
Two injected pairs already give $\lambda_{\max}=1.020>1$.
Random $F_1$: $2.010$; wrapping $\{1,2,3\}$: $1.858$.
Two-period $c=2/3$: $\lambda_{22}=1.0288>1$~\cite{r387,r394}.
Isolation is not $\lambda_{\max}<1$, and isolation plus the
Dirichlet hull does not close Gershgorin.
\end{prop}

Named kills: wrapping $\{1,2,3\}$ on pair density; random
$F_1$ on density plus fill; two-period on $\rho_{\mathrm{AP}}$;
scramble seed $3$ pair density $0.431$; pair-counter mutant
(gap-$2$ count $44$ versus gap-$1$ count $2$ on FRAME-A);
tail-$\times 2$ mutant (fattail helps, does not kill).

\section{What remains}\label{sec:rest}

Pair density $\sim 2\%$ on the source is a \emph{shadow} of
the r395 $2$--$3$ histogram with a sparse tail: most atoms are
isolated singletons, two $F_1$ pairs, a thin large-gap tail.
That shape is not isolation as a class, not a Steinhaus set,
and not a random $F_1$ word.
Both $F_\varepsilon$ and Assist still hang on the histogram,
now with the pair counter and the isolated synthetic class
ruled out as the closing lemma.

\section*{Claim boundary}

Finite identities for folded small integers and named-window
pair counts; a named refutation of isolation as a closing
implication for $\Delta^2\log\tau$ and for Assist; named
periodic / random-$F_1$ / scramble / two-period breaks.
No statement about zeros of $\zeta(s)$, in either direction.
No RH claim.
