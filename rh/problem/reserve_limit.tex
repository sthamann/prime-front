\section{The lemma}\label{sec:lem}

\begin{lemma}[reserve limit]\label{lem:lim}
On the selected sequence $a_k=2^k$,
$R=-\phibb=|c_J|-\Sigma$ converges to a strictly
positive floor $R_\infty>0$
(equivalently: the edge does not saturate $R=0$).
\end{lemma}

\noindent
\textbf{Verdict: REDUZIERT.}
A five-point AIC census on $k=5,\ldots,9$ prefers
$R_\infty\approx 0.030$ versus $k$
($0.034$ versus $a_k=2^k$).
$M3$ ($R\sim c/\log k$) is killed
($\Delta\mathrm{AIC}>15$).
$M2$ ($R\to 0$ as a power) loses by
$\Delta\mathrm{AIC}\approx 7$.
EXT vacuous windows sit in $[0.029,0.037]$.
$k=10$ ($k_z=197$, $N=4071$) border-fails;
$k=11$ ($N=12508$) is skipped.
The $0$--versus--$0.03$ split is therefore not
seen at the hoped depth.
Not a theorem.

\section{Identities}\label{sec:id}

\begin{prop}[reserve]\label{prop:R}
$R=-c_J-\Sigma=-\phibb$.
Over $\mathbb{Q}$ (vacuous toy) $c_J=-\tfrac32$,
$\Sigma=\tfrac12$ gives $R=1=-\phibb$.
At $k_z=9$, $R=0.08562$.
\end{prop}

\begin{prop}[$T_0$ dictionary]\label{prop:T0}
By the r411 identity $\Tfrak_0^*\Tfrak_0=C^{-1}$,
$\|\Tfrak_0\|=C_{\min}^{-1/2}$.
If $C_{\min}=1+\varepsilon$ then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:T0}
\|\Tfrak_0\|-1
\;=\;
-\tfrac12\varepsilon+O(\varepsilon^2).
\end{equation}
On vacuous $k_z=26,43$ the ratio
$(\|\Tfrak_0\|-1)/(-(C_{\min}-1)/2)$ equals $1.0000$.
This is the razor scale, of size $10^{-6}$--$10^{-7}$
on those windows.
\end{prop}

\section{What drives $R$}\label{sec:drv}

$\mathrm{den}$ is flat in $[1.48,1.65]$
(gap-to-$2$ is $O(0.5)$, not shrinking).
$\Sigma$ wanders in $[0.24,0.48]$ and is the
balance partner.
Three $\Sigma$-channels, measured separately:

\begin{itemize}
\item $\|s\|^2\sim 0.06$ is flat --- not the driver.
\item mass-weighted $\lambda$ falls mildly
$5.5\to 3.8$, then bounces ($k=8$: $4.52$)
--- not a collapse onto the razor.
\item $n_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/\sum p_i^2$ grows
$18\to 146$ ($k=5..9$) and $559$ at $k=8$:
occupation \emph{broadens}.
\end{itemize}

The razor share of $\Sigma$ stays $\le 3\cdot 10^{-7}$.
$B_w=S_{N-2}+5/7$ grows $6.4\to 18.9$ on the
selected range: the border-mass series $S=\sum\rho_k$
is not convergent here.
$\gamma=\|b\|^2/B_w$ falls only slowly ($0.71\to 0.58$).

So $\Sigma$ moves because the $s$-occupation gets
wider, not because $\|s\|^2$ grows and not because
the occupied $\lambda$ crash into $1$.

razor rate.

\section{What is a theorem, what is a census}\label{sec:honest}

Propositions~\ref{prop:R} and~\ref{prop:T0} are
theorems (finite identities).
Proposition~\ref{prop:aic} is a named refutation of
$M3$ plus an AIC census for $M1$.
Lemma~\ref{lem:lim} is not a theorem:
$R_\infty>0$ is preferred, not proved;
$k=10/11$ are missing;
$R\to 0$ remains a losing but living alternative
on a wrong abscissa.
If the floor holds, the cofinal edge reduces to
showing $\Sigma\to\Sigma_\infty<|c_{J,\infty}|$
with $\mathrm{den}_\infty<2$ already $O(1)$.
If $R\to 0$, the edge saturates and the
cofinal argument has the same depth as P1.
The measurement leans toward the first reading
and does not close either.
No RH claim.
