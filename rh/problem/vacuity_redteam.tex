\begin{abstract}\noindent
Rounds 450--455 named the frequently-mincut as the
$n_{\mathrm{stab}}$-compression of $R^{\dagger}$
and proved that this prefix is exactly the
archimedean fold through $J_P=\lceil\log 2/\Delta-1\rceil$.
A worlds test with identical fold geometry
(MAIN $=$ von~Mangoldt comb, ARCH $=$ comb deleted)
shows that every datum the prefix consumes is
the same in both worlds to machine precision.
The certified prefix positivity cannot tell a
world with primes from one without, and therefore
cannot imply RH.
Lean never truncated the cone: \texttt{Iff.rfl}
identified two Python charts by naming.
The honest object is $R^{\dagger}$ at depth
$\sim U_f/\Delta$, or the full cap for
$\forall f$.  No RH claim.  No anti-RH claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Q1 --- what Lean actually consumes}\label{sec:q1}

\begin{prop}[consumption map]\label{prop:map}
The theorem
\texttt{weil\_nonneg\_of\_frequently\_selected}
(\texttt{FrequentlySelected.lean}:414)
consumes, for each \texttt{GridElement} $f$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item one selected index $k$ with
$a_k\ge a_0(f)$ and $m_k\ge\texttt{meshExp}\,f$
(\texttt{selected\_covers}, \texttt{Selected.lean}:412);
\item \texttt{selectedWindowConeSemidef} $k$
(\texttt{FrequentlySelected.lean}:261--268,280):
$R^{\dagger}(W^{\mathbb{R}}_k)\succeq\tfrac12 I$ at
$n=\mathtt{cap}=(S+1)/2$
(\texttt{Selected.lean}:246), in a
$\mu$-ONB with $Q^{T}(\mathrm{combHankel})\,Q=I$
(\texttt{Selected.lean}:287) --- the
\emph{full} window, including the comb;
\item the named bridge to plain
\texttt{fullRead}$\ge 0$ for every $f$ with
$\texttt{meshExp}\,f\le m_k$
(\texttt{FrequentlySelected.lean}:322--325,358--362);
\item $\texttt{fullRead}=\texttt{archRead}-\texttt{combRead}+\texttt{poleRead}$
(\texttt{Elementwise.lean}:718--719), with
\[
\texttt{combRead}(a,f)
=\sum_{n\in\mathrm{atoms}(a)}\mu_n\,f(\log n)
\]
(\texttt{Elementwise.lean}:386--387).
\end{enumerate}
The guard $\texttt{meshExp}\,f\le m_k$ is a
\emph{mesh-resolution} filter on the test function.
It is not a grade cut of the window chain at
$\nstab$.  That is a category error.
\end{prop}

The r450 declaration
\texttt{frequently\_selected\_prefix\_augDualResolvent\_ge\_half}
(\texttt{FrequentlySelected.lean}:309--315) is
definitionally the existing cone;
\texttt{frequently\_prefix\_mincut\_ident} is
\texttt{Iff.rfl}.  No truncation is stated,
and none is proved.
$M_{\partial}/\rho_0$ never enters the Lean cone.
It enters the Python plateau identity
$q_*=M_{\partial}/(M_{\partial}+5/7)$ (r452).

\section{Q2 --- the worlds test}\label{sec:q2}

Fix fold geometry $(\alpha,M,L,\Delta)$.
MAIN uses $\mathtt{arch\_lags}+\mathtt{prime\_lags}$.
ARCH uses $\mathtt{arch\_lags}$ only.
Both keep the production $\mathtt{smooth\_comb}$
border.

\begin{lemma}[prefix is world-blind]\label{lem:vac}
On kz$69$ ($\nstab=119<J_P=711$),
\[
\bigl|q^{\dagger}_{\mathrm{MAIN}}(119)
-q^{\dagger}_{\mathrm{ARCH}}(119)\bigr|
=3.33\cdot 10^{-16}.
\]
On kz$17$ at depth $8$ (strictly below $J_P=19$),
the same difference is $2.22\cdot 10^{-16}$.
Signed folded $m_0$ agrees to $10^{-14}$
(kz$17$: $m_0=2.5994580787932398$ in both worlds).
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[arithmetic onset is $J_P$]\label{lem:arith}
MAIN leaves the plateau at the first prime tent.
kz$17$: at $n=J_P=19$,
$\Delta q=0.05166$ (pin);
ARCH $q^{\dagger}$ stays at $q_*\approx 0.78566$.
kz$69$: at $n=J_P=711$, $\Delta q=0.00401$;
at the honest depth $\nf=\lceil U_f/\Delta\rceil=2053$
for the tent $U_f=2$, $\Delta q=0.005698$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{combRead} sees primes]\label{lem:comb}
For the even tent $F(u)=\max(0,1-|u|/2)$
(support past $\log 2$),
\[
\texttt{combRead}
=\sum_{n\in\{2,3,4,5,7\}}\frac{2\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}F(\log n)
=1.7458508391553806
\]
in MAIN, and $0$ in ARCH.
The same tent truncated at $U=0.5<\log 2$
gives $0$ in both worlds.
mpmath $\mathrm{dps}=50$ agrees with float64
to $2\cdot 10^{-16}$.
\end{lemma}

The r455 ``eventually per fixed $n$'' argument
is the opposite of coverage: as $\Delta\to 0$,
every \emph{fixed} grade falls into the blind
zone $n<J_P(\Delta)$.  It does not cover
$\nf\sim U_f/\Delta$, which grows.

\section{Q3 --- the honest object}\label{sec:q3}

\begin{prop}[honest depth]\label{prop:honest}
A test function of support $U_f$ requires chain
depth $\nf\sim U_f/\Delta$.
The RH quantifier is $\forall f$, so $U_f$ is
unbounded and the honest object is $R^{\dagger}$
of the full cap (or a cofinal selected window
large enough to cover $f$).
The r450 two-chart split stands with the
opposite moral: the prefix chart is vacuous;
the full-window chart is load-bearing
(kz$17$: $\delta_{\mathrm{pre}}=0.21432$,
$\delta_{\mathrm{full}}=0.10864$).
\end{prop}

Against the finite tent $U_f=2$,
$\nf/N_w\in[0.247,0.311]$ on the sealed
r447/r448 windows, and every recorded flip
sits past $\nf$ (kz$197$: flip $3788>\nf=1175$).
\texttt{TAIL\_ONLY} (r449) survives for this $f$.
It does not survive the $\forall f$ quantifier:
that demand is the full window, on which
kz$197$ is \texttt{EXACT\_DEAD}.

$M_{\partial}$ grows like $0.79\log N$ because
the \emph{archimedean} window mass grows.
It is equal in MAIN and ARCH.  It is not
von~Mangoldt.
