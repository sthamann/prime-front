\title{\bfseries Three-gap of the fold mask, refuted\\[2mm]
\large Steinhaus and log-local SATZ, the $\nu$-mask is not
three-gap, and $n_{\mathrm{uniq}}\le 3$ does not close $F_\varepsilon$}
\author{Stefan Hamann}
\date{August 28, 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}\noindent
Rounds 393--394 reduced both remaining analytic rests
($\Delta^2\log\tau$ under $F_1$, and Assist) to
\emph{occupation regularity} of the $\nu$-mask on the cosine
grid: not run-lengths, not checkerboard signs, not
equidistribution.  The candidate was the Steinhaus three-gap
theorem for $\{n\alpha\}$, locally for folded $\log(p^k)$ via
the $f''$ of $\log$.
What is proved: Steinhaus on $\Z/q$ (exact, $309/309$ Farey and
Fibonacci windows) with the additive law $a+b=c$ when three
gaps occur; PNT-free integer-log local three-gap for
$n\ge 512$, $M\le 64$.  What is \emph{refuted}: that the
$\nu$-mask of a canonical window is globally or blockwise
three-gap (FRAME-A $n_{\mathrm{uniq}}=12$; core-$42$ in
$[8,18]$, none $\le 5$; EXT $n_{\mathrm{uniq}}$ grows with
$S$), and that $n_{\mathrm{uniq}}\le 3$ plus $F_1$ plus
$\rho_{\mathrm{AP}}<1/5$ implies the $\Delta^2\log\tau$ box
(wrapping random $(1,2,3)$-gap $F_1$-masks leave every legal
JUMP at $h=40$; mechanical $\varphi$ misses the box at
$h=80$).  The $3/8$-floor and MED-CAP $8/3$ are not shadows of
the cosine-grid gap spectrum.  No RH claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Recollection}\label{sec:rec}

Occupied Fej\'er deletes the $\nu$-support $\Xi$ from the
discrete Bernstein--Szeg\H{o} cosine grid~\cite{r393,r390}.
$F_1$ (max $\nu$-run $\le 2$) is necessary and not
sufficient~\cite{r393}: periodic tiles $1010/1100/110$ stay
inside $\lvert\gamma-1/4\rvert\le 1/16$, a random $F_1$
half-filling jumps $1.18$.  Sign-Schur is a
coin~\cite{r394}.  The remaining object is the occupation of
$\Xi$ itself.

The fold construction places $\log(p^k)$ on a circle of length
$L=4h-2$ at angle $2\pi k/L$~\cite{r379} and folds
$j\mapsto\min(j,L-j)$ onto the cosine grid.  Sequences
$\{n\alpha\}$ have, by Steinhaus, at most three distinct
circular gaps.  The folded log sequence is not a pure
$\{n\alpha\}$, but is locally log-linear
($\log(n+1)-\log(n)\approx 1/n$).

\section{Steinhaus, and log-local three-gap}\label{sec:satz}

\begin{lemma}[Steinhaus on $\Z/q$]\label{lem:steinhaus}
Let $p,q$ be coprime positive integers.  For every
$N=1,\ldots,q$ the circular gaps of
$\{0,p,2p,\ldots,(N-1)p\}\bmod q$ take at most three values.
Machine identity on every coprime pair with $q\le 30$ and on
the Fibonacci convergents with $q<500$ ($309/309$).
When three values $a<b<c$ occur, $a+b=c$
(witnessed on $\{\,k\varphi\,\}$ at $N=4$).
\end{lemma}

A random $20$-point subset of a $40$-point Steinhaus set on
$\Z/89$ has five gap lengths: thinning does \emph{not}
preserve three-gap.  Prime-power selection from a log
quasi-AP is a thinning.

\begin{lemma}[integer-log local three-gap]\label{lem:loglocal}
Let $L=800$ and $x_n=\bigl\lfloor L\,\{\log_2 n\}\bigr\rfloor$.
For every $n_0\ge 512$ and every block length $M\le 64$, the
circular gaps of $\{x_n:n_0\le n<n_0+M\}$ take at most three
values ($12/12$).  The onset is $n_0=128$, $M=8$
($n_{\mathrm{uniq}}=3$).
For $n_0\le 64$, $M=32$ one has $n_{\mathrm{uniq}}\in\{8,17,18,19\}$:
fast drift, a finite census, not a SATZ.
The argument is PNT-free: it uses only that $(\log)''=-1/n^2$
is small at large $n$.
\end{lemma}

Small-$n$ blocks of the log skeleton therefore remain a
named census.  They are the honest exception list of
Lemma~\ref{lem:loglocal}.

\section{The mask is not three-gap}\label{sec:mask}

Write $n_{\mathrm{uniq}}$ for the number of distinct
\emph{linear} gaps of the occupied cosine-grid indices.

\begin{prop}[mask spectrum]\label{prop:mask}
On FRAME-A $w=9$ ($S=367$, $n_\nu=104$):
$n_{\mathrm{uniq}}=12$, gap-$1$ count $=2$ (the two $F_1$
pairs), $d_{\min}/d_{\mathrm{med}}=1/3<3/8$,
$d_{\max}/d_{\min}=23>8/3$.
Sliding windows of $12$ occupied nodes are three-gap in
fraction $0.45$ and five-gap in fraction $0.74$ (partial, not
a block SATZ).  Spatial dyadic $L=4$: nine blocks, all
$\le 5$, seven $\le 3$; shifting the cut by $S/32$ moves the
counts.  The pre-fold circle has the same
$n_{\mathrm{uniq}}=12$.
Core-$42$: $n_{\mathrm{uniq}}\in[8,18]$ (median $13$),
$0/42$ with $n_{\mathrm{uniq}}\le 5$, sliding-$12$
three-gap fraction in $[0.31,0.60]$.
EXT $kz=97$: $n_{\mathrm{uniq}}=13$; larger EXT windows
reach $20$--$27$ (grows with $S$).
Dead $\chi_3/\chi_4$ sample: $n_{\mathrm{uniq}}\in[10,15]$.
\end{prop}

The $3/8$-floor of the mean sieve~\cite{r361} and MED-CAP
$8/3$ live on the \emph{log-sieve block packing}, not on the
cosine-grid gap spectrum of $\Xi$.  They are not shadows of
three-gap.

\section{The implication fails}\label{sec:disc}

\begin{prop}[discriminator]\label{prop:disc}
On the $h=40$ cosine grid, wrapping concatenations of
$\{1,2,3\}$ (an $F_1$-legal three-gap mask with
$\rho_{\mathrm{AP}}=0.194<1/5$) leave the box
(jump $2.33$).  Random $F_1$ has $n_{\mathrm{uniq}}=5$ and
jump $7.03$.  Periodic $1010$ ($n_{\mathrm{uniq}}=1$,
$\rho_{\mathrm{AP}}=1$) and $1100$ ($n_{\mathrm{uniq}}=2$)
stay in.
At $h=80$: random $F_1$ jump $1.179$ OUT (r393 reproduced);
$\{n\alpha\}$ with $F_1$ and $\rho_{\mathrm{AP}}<1/5$ can sit
in (e.g.\ $\sqrt{2}-1$, jump $0.30$);
wrapping $\{2,3,4\}$ (isolated singles) is IN on $8/8$ seeds;
wrapping $\{1,2,3\}$ (random $F_1$ pairs) is IN on $1/8$.  Mechanical $\varphi$ at fill $0.45$ misses the box
($d=0.1016>1/16$) even with three-gap and $F_1$.
\end{prop}

Thus $n_{\mathrm{uniq}}\le 3$ is neither necessary (the source
mask has $8$--$18$ global gaps and stays in by the r393 census)
nor sufficient (random three-gap with $F_1$ pairs leaves the
box).  Isolated-single three-gap $\{2,3,4\}$ is a different
class from the source (which has a median of four gap-$1$
pairs on core-$42$, and a sparse tail out to gap $23$).

\section{Assist and named kills}\label{sec:kills}

On $h=40$ at $n_0=\lfloor 2N/5\rfloor=16$,
$\lambda_{\max}(E_{n_0})$ of the Fej\'er-deleted Gram is
$2.01$ on random $F_1$, $0.997$ on wrapping $\{2,3,4\}$, and
$1.405$ on $1010$.  Two-period $c=2/3$ has
$\lambda_{\max}(E_{22})=1.0288>1$~\cite{r387,r394}.
The Assist spectrum separates sparse isolated-single
three-gap from random $F_1$ and from global AP; it does not
identify the source class (which has pairs and a tail).

Named kills:
\begin{itemize}
\item two-period / $1010$: one gap, $\rho_{\mathrm{AP}}=1$
(non-periodicity);
\item random $F_1$: many gaps, jump $1.18$;
\item scramble seed $3$: $n_{\mathrm{uniq}}=15$, sliding-$12$
three-gap fraction $0.00$ (occupation, matching r393's $F_1$
break);
\item dyadic-cut shift: blockwise $n_{\mathrm{uniq}}$ moves
with the partition (not a SATZ of a canonical cut).
\end{itemize}

\section{What remains}\label{sec:rest}

The occupation object is now strictly smaller than
``three-gap of the mask''.  The source histogram on FRAME-A is
\emph{$2$--$3$ dominated} (counts $44$ and $27$) with two
$F_1$ pairs and a sparse large-gap tail.  That is the shape of
a prime-power thinning of the log skeleton of
Lemma~\ref{lem:loglocal}, not of a Steinhaus set and not of a
random $F_1$ word.  Both $F_\varepsilon$ ($\Delta^2\log\tau$)
and Assist still hang on this histogram, not on
$n_{\mathrm{uniq}}\le B$.

\section*{Machine check}

Standalone: Steinhaus on $\Z/q$; additive $a+b=c$; thinning;
log-local large $n$; small-$n$ census; $3/8$ and $8/3$ shadows.
Construction: $w=9$ spectrum, sliding partial, discriminator,
scramble, Assist split.
Discovery census: \texttt{three\_gap\_mask\_probe.py}

Exit line: \texttt{THREE GAP MASK VERIFIED}.

\section*{Claim boundary}

Finite identities for Steinhaus on cyclic groups and a named
range for integer-log local three-gap; a named refutation of
three-gap as a property of the $\nu$-mask and as a closing
implication for $F_\varepsilon$; named periodic / random-$F_1$
/ scramble / two-period breaks.
No statement about zeros of $\zeta(s)$, in either direction.
No RH claim.
