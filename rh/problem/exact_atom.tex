\section{Exact builder}\label{sec:build}

Integer mesh $(M,L,N_w)$ is locked to
\texttt{V.window\_shape} (the r397 family).
Values are mp from the start:
$u_i=\log n_i$ on exact prime powers
$n\le 400000$ (the table cap),
tent lags, archimedean GL-$48$ (nodes refined
from float seeds to working dps), circulant DFT
by Bluestein, cosine fold.
Border: sealed smooth comb $\Delta u=0.01$ with
the same arch+DFT and the PIK fold.
No float64 cast into the chain.

\begin{prop}[Atoms are ulp-close]\label{prop:atoms}

\section{The decision}\label{sec:dec}

\begin{prop}[EXACT\_DEAD]\label{prop:dead}
The $k=10$ chain on exact atoms at dps $50$
(full, $530\,\mathrm{s}$) flips first at
$n=3788$,
$\eta=-8.853149582220489\cdot 10^{-14}$,
$\mathrm{rel}=1.255988618504423\cdot 10^{-6}$,
$n_{\mathrm{flip}}=115$.
The same first pivot at dps $70$ (to $n=3793$)
is bit-identical.
Hence the window is EXACT\_DEAD:
the sign is a property of the exact-source
construction, not of float64 atoms or of
recurrence rounding.
\end{prop}

$n/N=3788/4071\approx 0.931$: the signed
$\tilde\mu$-chain dies at the same relative
depth as in r445/r446.
Divergence versus the float-atom chain first
crosses rel $10^{-8}$ at $n=218$
(absolute $\sim 10^{-15}$, same sign);
there is no sign-split --- both constructions
flip at $n=3788$.

$k=9$ exact-atom prefix $80$: $0$ flips, $\eta$
matches float (the living side of the band
is consistent).
$k_z137$ exact atoms match to one ulp; the
window stays the first dead neighbour of the
r446 scan.

\section{Consequence}\label{sec:cons}

No $\delta_{10}$ (the window is not ABD-living).
The r445 slice $k=5..9$ with live $k=8$ and its
floor fit are unchanged
($\delta_\infty=+0.02741$).

On \emph{this} Selected family ($a_k=2^k$,
the r397 mesh), infinitely many ABD-living
windows do not exist: the living band ends
at $k=9$ / $k_z136$, and exact atoms do not
revive $k=10$ or $k_z137$.
The frequently-quantifier
$\exists^{\mathrm{f}}k:\,R^{\dagger}(W_k)\succeq\tfrac12 I$
is therefore not fed by this family.
Whether a \emph{different} cofinal family
(other $a_k$, other legal mesh) stays ABD-living
is a reviewer fork, not a repair of $a_k=2^k$.
