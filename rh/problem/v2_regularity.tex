\title{\bfseries Run-length regularity $V_2$ at the $x$-mask\\[2mm]
\large Anatomy of $v_2$, what pairing and products prove,
and why the MED-CAP chain freezes here}
\author{Stefan Hamann}

\begin{abstract}\noindent
The length invariant $X_n$ of a folded sign field reduces MED-CAP
to a single edge-return.  The named remainder $V_2$ forbids a
$6$-plateau of $x$-order run lengths after three consecutive
singletons at the $20\%$ $x$-mask.  This note records the anatomy
of that colouring, proves the combinatorial half that can be
proved, and applies the hydra ward.  The factor $v_2$ is the
degree-$(N-2)$ arithmetic-comb chain polynomial, evaluated on the
smooth-border nodes; it carries source arithmetic through the
Jacobi coefficients of the bordered three-term recurrence.  The
factor $w$ is the arm sign of the explicit smooth fold, independent
of primes.  Product combinatorics: a product flips exactly where one factor
flips.  A $2$-regular factor does \emph{not} imply $V_2$ (the
tempting reduction is false).  A Chebyshev colouring
after the $x$-mask never ends $(1,1,1)$, and Chebyshev times a
flip-first Nyquist burst of $w$ never violates $V_2$.  Random
independent products \emph{do} violate, so $V_2$ is not a theorem
of abstract paired colourings.  The pattern $(1,1,1)$ produces the
dangerous $\theta$-prefix $(1,2)$ if and only if the run count is
odd.  On the $134$-window surface $V_2$ holds ($0$ violators);
exactly two colouring triples occur, both regular, only one odd
(the known saturator $\chi_4$ at index $53$, $w$-driven).  The
candidate reductions --- Jacobi-near-Chebyshev / Freud spacing of
$v_2$, or a further named condition $V_3$ --- are not strictly
more elementary than $V_2$ (they are statements about the same
chain polynomial, or about its coefficients, which are the north
star of the program).  Verdict $\mathrm{REGRESS\_DETECTED}$: the
escape $T_2'\Leftarrow\cdots\Leftarrow V_2$ freezes at $V_2$.
The pointwise floor $g_i\ge 3/8$ is a theorem of the construction
\emph{modulo} $V_2$.  T1 is unchanged.  No RH claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Recollection}\label{sec:rec}

We use the sign-field notation of~\cite{xn} without change.
Blocks of the source are paired consecutive same-sign runs of
$c_t=w\,x\,v_2\,\phi$ on the bulk $\theta$-grid after a $20\%$
$x$-hull mask, pairing from $x$-min, odd tail its own block.
Condition $V_2$ of~\cite{xn}~\S5: if the last three $x$-order run
lengths $R_{\mathrm{mask}}$ of $\mathrm{sign}(v_2\,w\,x)$ are
$(1,1,1)$, then among the four preceding run lengths at least two
are $\le 2$.  Equivalently, no $6$-plateau at the mask after a
triple singleton.  The minimal grid-consistent violator is the
$x$-order
\[
(\underbrace{3,\ldots,3}_{8},\;1,\;1,\;1),
\]
which pairs to $\theta$-order $n=(1,2,6^4)$ and violates MED-CAP
at the edge (short $C_2$).  A spike $(1,2,6,4,\ldots)$ saturates
and does not violate.

The chain after~\cite{medcap,xn} is
\[
T_2'
 \;\Longleftarrow\;
 g_i\ge\tfrac38
 \;\Longleftarrow\;
 \mathrm{MED\text{-}CAP}
 \;\Longleftarrow\;
 X_n
 \;\Longleftarrow\;
 V_2,
\]
with $X_{\mathrm{tile}}$, the SEP-Satz, and the pairing lemmas of
\cite{xn} already proved.  This note attacks $V_2$.

\section{Anatomy}\label{sec:anatomy}

\subsection{What $v_2$ is}

A window of depth $N$ carries an arithmetic comb (prime-power,
Dirichlet, or second-family) and a smooth border comb.  The
bordered chain of~\cite{xn}~\S2 is the three-term recurrence of
the \emph{arithmetic} signed measure $\mu-\nu$, with Jacobi
coefficients $(\hat\alpha_k,\gamma_{k+1})$ computed from that
comb; the smooth border enters the pairing scalars $\rho_k$ but
not those Jacobi coefficients.  The polynomial $v_2$ is the
scaled monic chain value of degree $N-2$, evaluated on the
smooth-border nodes.  It is therefore an orthogonal polynomial of
a discrete signed source measure, sampled on a different
(border) subset of the same fold-grid.  It is \emph{not} a
deterministic function of the mesh/fold geometry alone: the
primes enter through $(\hat\alpha_k,\gamma_{k+1})$.  Terminal
coefficients on the $134$-window surface sit near Chebyshev
($\max_k|\gamma_k-\tfrac14|\le 0.038$, median $0.012$ among the
last three), but that proximity is measured, not a theorem, and
the coefficients \emph{are} the chain --- the north-star object
of the program.

\subsection{What $w$ is}

The weight $w$ on a border node is the folded smooth-comb mass of
that fold-lag.  Its sign is the arm: $\mathrm{sign}(w)=+1$ on
$\mu$ cells of $d_{\mathrm{arm}}$ and $-1$ on $\nu$ cells.  The
smooth comb is an explicit positive log-grid times the
archimedean tent; $d_{\mathrm{arm}}$ can change sign by
arch-versus-atom cancellation.  No primes enter $w$.  On the four
$w=9$ worlds, $w$ has a single sign-run on the whole bulk, so
the colouring equals $\mathrm{sign}(v_2)$ times $\mathrm{sign}(x)$
(one flip at $x=0$, interior to the bulk).  At depth, $w$ may
oscillate: $\chi_4$ index $53$ has fourteen $w$-runs, with a
Nyquist tail at the $+x$ mask.

\subsection{What the $x$-mask is}

The bulk keeps atoms with $x$ inside the central $60\%$ of the
hull (edge fraction $0.20$).  $x$-order is increasing $x$; the
last runs of $R_{\mathrm{mask}}$ sit at the $+x$ mask edge
($x\approx +0.60$, $\theta=\arccos x$).  Because $x=\cos\theta$ is
decreasing in $\theta$ on $[0,\pi]$, that edge is $\theta$-min of
the bulk, and an odd tail there becomes block $0$ in
$\theta$-order.

\section{What pairing and products prove}\label{sec:comb}

\begin{lemma}[flip XOR]\label{lem:contain}
Let $A,B\in\{\pm 1\}^n$.  The product $AB$ changes sign at index
$i$ if and only if exactly one of $A,B$ changes sign at $i$.
In particular, a simultaneous flip of both factors does
\emph{not} end a product-run (in-phase locking).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$(AB)_i=A_i B_i$, so the sign ratio
$(AB)_i/(AB)_{i-1}$ equals $-1$ precisely when exactly one factor
ratio is $-1$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[$2$-regular does not imply $V_2$]\label{lem:2reg}
There exist colourings in which one factor is $2$-regular (all
runs of length $1$ or $2$) and the product nevertheless violates
$V_2$.  Among $8000$ random such products, a positive fraction
violate (companion script, gate G2).
\end{lemma}

The tempting reduction ``$v_2$ Chebyshev-class $\Rightarrow V_2$''
is therefore not a theorem of product combinatorics.  It is also
false as a hypothesis on the source: $v_2$ has bulk-mode $2$ but
admits runs of length $6$ at the mask (already on the $w=9$
FRAME-A window).

\begin{lemma}[Chebyshev mask]\label{lem:cheb}
A colouring $\mathrm{sign}(\sin m\theta)$ on a uniform
$\theta$-grid, after the same $20\%$ $x$-mask, never ends with
three consecutive singleton runs.  (Re-gated from~\cite{xn}
Lemma~5.)
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[parity of $(1,1,1)$]\label{lem:parity}
Let $R=(r_0,\ldots,r_{\nu-1})$ end with $(1,1,1)$.  Pairing from
$x$-min, the $\theta$-order prefix is $(n_0,n_1)=(1,2)$ if and
only if $\nu$ is odd.  If $\nu$ is even, then $n_0=2$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The odd tail exists only for odd $\nu$, and equals $r_{\nu-1}=1$;
the last complete pair is then $(r_{\nu-3},r_{\nu-2})=(1,1)$,
summing to $2$.  Reverse to $\theta$-order.  If $\nu$ is even there
is no tail: the last pair is $(1,1)$, which becomes $n_0=2$.
\end{proof}

Thus a colouring triple is load-bearing for $X_n$ only when the
run count is odd.  An even triple cannot create the unique
small-separation locus of~\cite{xn} Lemma~3.

\begin{lemma}[Chebyshev times a Nyquist burst]\label{lem:cheb-burst}
On $425$ trigonometric windows ($T\in\{80,\ldots,400\}$, five
degrees, burst lengths $3$ through $7$), the product of a
Chebyshev colouring after the $x$-mask with a flip-first Nyquist
burst of $w$ at the $+x$ edge produces $0$ tails $(1,1,1)$ and
$0$ $V_2$ violations.  A constant $w$ times a $v_2$ that itself
ends $(2^8,r,r,1,1,1)$ with $r\ge 3$ satisfies $V_2$ (the four
preceding include two bulk $2$s) and pairs to a spike, not a
plateau.
\end{lemma}

The burst is the natural adversary for a $w$-driven triple:
monochrome bulk, then three alternating cells.  Against a
$2$-regular factor it does not produce the dangerous pattern.
Random independent run-products, by contrast, violate $V_2$ in a
few per thousand draws: the obstruction is not abstract product
combinatorics.

\section{The named violator, precisely}\label{sec:viol}

The $x$-order $(3^8,1,1,1)$ has odd length, pairs to
$n=(1,2,6^4)$, fails $V_2$ (prev4 $=(3,3,3,3)$), and fails
MED-CAP.  The shorter ending $(2^8,3,3,1,1,1)$ satisfies $V_2$
(prev4 $=(2,2,3,3)$) and pairs to $n=(1,2,6,4,\ldots)$, whose
third-of-five is $4$ (saturating spike).  Degree counting does
not forbid the plateau: a degree-$(N-2)$ polynomial may cluster
zeros.  Fold multiplicity $\le 2$ and the mesh identity constrain
occupation, not $v_2$-run lengths.

\section{The source surface}\label{sec:census}

\begin{prop}[one-shot census]\label{prop:census}
On the $134$ windows of~\cite{medcap} Proposition~5
($42$ prime-power core $+\,8$ second-family $+\,42+42$ Dirichlet;
the same surface as~\cite{xn} Proposition~8):
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V_2$ holds on every window ($0$ colouring violators).
  Chebyshev colourings on the same hulls: $0$ violators.
  Chebyshev times the actual $w$: $0$ violators.
\item Colouring triples $(1,1,1)$ occur twice, both regular:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item $\chi_3$ index $16$: $v_2$ itself ends $(1,1,1)$, $w$ is
    constant on the mask neighbourhood (three $w$-runs globally,
    a length-$1$ stub far from the $+x$ edge), prev4 $=(3,2,1,3)$,
    even run count, $\theta$-prefix starting $n_0=2$ (Lemma
    \ref{lem:parity}: not small-sep).
  \item $\chi_4$ index $53$: the unique $(1,2)$ of~\cite{xn}.
    The triple is $w$-driven ($v_2$ last three $=(3,1,4)$, $w$
    last three $=(1,1,1)$), prev4 $=(4,1,2,4)$, odd run count,
    saturating prefix $(1,2,6,5,4,4)$.
  \end{itemize}
\item $v_2$-only triples: one (the $\chi_3$ index $16$ case).
  $w$-only triples: seven.  Six of those seven do not produce a
  colouring triple (the Chebyshev-times-burst cancellation of
  Lemma~\ref{lem:cheb-burst}, realised by the source).
\item On the four $w=9$ worlds, $w$ has one bulk sign-run and
  there is no colouring triple.  Weights on the mask tail satisfy
  $|w|\ge 1.2\cdot 10^{-5}$ (not a floating-sign artefact).
\item Prefix $(1,2)$ occurs once, as in~\cite{xn}.
\end{enumerate}
So $V_2$ holds on every window the construction currently
produces, including the unique load-bearing triple.  A deeper
window is not excluded by this census.
\end{prop}

The $\chi_3$ index-$16$ window is the existence proof that $v_2$
\emph{can} triple at the mask.  One cannot prove ``$v_2$ never
ends $(1,1,1)$''.  What remains is exactly $V_2$: when a triple
occurs, the four preceding runs are mixed.

\section{Hydra ward}\label{sec:hydra}

The charter: if a proof of $V_2$ only reduces it to a further
condition, that condition must be strictly more elementary
(closer to the explicit construction) than $V_2$.  Otherwise the
verdict is $\mathrm{REGRESS\_DETECTED}$ and the escape freezes at
$V_2$, with the complete conditional
$T_2'\Leftarrow\cdots\Leftarrow V_2$.  No further stage is opened.

Candidate reductions, and why they fail the elementary test:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{$v_2$ is a mesh/fold function.}  False: $v_2$ is the
  arithmetic OP (Section~\ref{sec:anatomy}).  Case (i) of the
  charter does not fire.
\item \emph{A named $p^k$-constellation is mesh-excluded.}  The
  violator of~\S\ref{sec:viol} is legal as a $\pm$ colouring, legal
  as a pairing, and legal as the restriction of a degree-$(N-2)$
  polynomial.  No finite construction identity plus named
  classical theorem forbids it.  It is not realised on the
  $134$-window surface; that is a census, not an exclusion.
\item \emph{Freud / arcsine spacing, or Jacobi $\gamma_k\to 1/4$.}
  $v_2$ is not the OP of a fixed positive continuous measure.
  The Jacobi coefficients of the discrete signed comb \emph{are}
  the bordered chain.  Reducing $V_2$ to a Chebyshev-asymptotic
  for those coefficients is a reduction toward the north star
  (L$^*$, the wall), not toward the fold/mesh.  Not more
  elementary.
\item \emph{Replace $V_2$ by ``$v_2$ is $2$-regular at the mask''.}
  Lemma~\ref{lem:2reg} refutes the combinatorial implication even
  as an abstract product statement, and the replacement is false
  on the surface (runs of length $6$).  Not more elementary, and
  not true.
\item \emph{Replace $V_2$ by a condition on $w$ alone.}  The
  load-bearing triple at $\chi_4$ index $53$ is $w$-driven, and
  $w$ is construction-explicit.  But the MAIN family (single-arm
  $w$) reduces $V_2$ back to $v_2$-only, and $\chi_3$ index $16$
  is a $v_2$-triple with $w$ constant at the mask.  A $w$-only
  lemma does not cover the family that carries the floor.
\end{enumerate}

None of these is a strictly more elementary lemma on which $V_2$
would rest.  Opening a $V_3$ would be the hydra.  The ward fires.

\section{The frozen chain}\label{sec:chain}

\begin{prop}[conditional floor, frozen]\label{prop:frozen}
On every tiled packing produced by the construction of
\cite{xn}~\S2,
\[
X_{\mathrm{tile}}
 \;+\;
\text{pairing lemmas of~\cite{xn}}
 \;+\;
 V_2
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
 X_n
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\mathrm{MED\text{-}CAP}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
 g_i\ge\tfrac38
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
 T_2'\ \text{trivial}.
\]
$V_2$ is the final named lemma of this chain.  It is verified on
the $134$-window surface and is not proved as a cofinal law of
the bordered chain.  The cubic-mass bound of~\cite{medcap}~\S T1
carries, still carrying $C_K$.
\end{prop}

Unconditional on the present surface: $X_n$, MED-CAP, the
pointwise floor, and $T_2'$.  Cofinal: the same statements
modulo $V_2$.

\section{T1}\label{sec:t1}

Unchanged.  $V_2$ is a constraint on run lengths of a sign field.
The masses $q_i$ see the same field as an amplitude.  Proving
$V_2$, even cofinally, does not produce a family-uniform $C_K$.
The r358 record (per-family freeze maximum fails on the test set;
ceiling $23.70$ against freeze $4.91$) stands.

builders (not a sealed probe), recorded here.  Exit line:
\texttt{V2 STEPS VERIFIED}.

\section*{Claim boundary}

Finite identities on explicit colourings, a pairing-parity lemma,
a product-containment lemma, and a named freeze of an analytic
remainder $V_2$ on a bordered-chain polynomial.  No statement
about zeros of $\zeta(s)$, in either direction.  No RH claim.
