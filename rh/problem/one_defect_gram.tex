\begin{abstract}\noindent
Round 403 found that on FRAME-A the compression
$M=P^{\mathrm{ref}}_- B P^{\mathrm{ref}}_-$ of the source
perturbation $B=R-R^{\mathrm{ref}}$ is positive definite
(residual~$0$, $49/49$, spectrum in $[0.023,0.710]$), while a
mask-preserving permutation of the von~Mangoldt weights
destroys both the Gram property and P1.
The reviewer hypothesis (DCCLXIX) is that this Gram is the
finite Euler-family Gram of $\Lambda(p^r)=\log p$ at
$\log(p^r)=r\log p$, completed by a Digamma--Loewner block,
an Uvarov off-diagonal, and exactly one null mode --- a
source-explicit identity
$Q^T A_0 Q=FF^T-\ell\ell^T$ from which $\indm A_0\le 1$ would
follow by congruence, with no asymptotic analysis.
Three classes are distinguished.
Only class~3 (closed-form $F$ from $\Lambda$, $\log$, Fold,
Tent, Digamma, and the explicit Bernstein--Szeg\H{o} modes,
breaking exactly under $\Lambda$-permutation and position
jitter) would count.
After one honest round, class~3 is not reached.
The only exact factor of $M$ is Cholesky of $M$ itself
(class~1).
No RH claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{The identity and the three classes}\label{sec:id}

Write $R=R_{N-3}$ for the dual Christoffel--Darboux Gram of
the first $N_w-3$ dual OP columns on the hole nodes $Y$,
$A_0=R-\half I$, $R^{\mathrm{ref}}$ for the Bernstein--Szeg\H{o}
Fej\'er dual on the same $Y$, and $B=R-R^{\mathrm{ref}}$.
Let $Q$ be an orthonormal basis of the negative space of
$A_{\mathrm{ref}}=R^{\mathrm{ref}}-\half I$, and
$M=Q^T B Q$.
Satz~G asks for a source-explicit factor
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G}
  Q^T A_0 Q \;=\; FF^T - \ell\ell^T
  \qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad
  Q^T A_0 Q + \ell\ell^T \;=\; FF^T,
\end{equation}
with $F$ a closed formula in $(\Lambda(p^r),\log(p^r),\mathrm{Fold},\mathrm{Tent},\psi,Q)$
\emph{before} evaluation, and $\ell$ the one null mode.
Congruence would then give $\indm A_0\le 1$ if~\eqref{eq:G}
held on a full flag, not merely on a subspace.

Three classes (binding):
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{CHOLESKY\_TAUTOLOGY.}
$F$ is a Cholesky factor, a square root, or an eigenfactor of
$M$ or of $A_0$, or a post-hoc solve of $XX^T=M$.
Always exact when $M\succ 0$; silent under every mutant of the
source that leaves $M$ positive definite.
\item \textbf{REFERENCE\_COORDINATE\_ONLY.}
$Q$ is the numerical projector of $A_{\mathrm{ref}}$, or a
full Chebyshev-$U$ basis of $Y$ that contains every subspace
tautologically.
The factor of $M$ remains class~1.
\item \textbf{SOURCE\_GRAM\_EXACT.}
$F$ is assembled from the Euler features
$f^{c,s}_{p,r}(j)=\sqrt{2\log p}\,p^{-r/4}\cos/\sin(r\,\theta_{p,j})$,
the Cauchy features $(m+z_j)^{-1}$, Fold/Tent as an outer
linear transfer, and an Uvarov stacking
$[F_-;F_+]$, minus one explicit null mode.
Residual~$0$ on MAIN; residual explodes under
$\Lambda$-permutation and position jitter.
\end{enumerate}
Only class~3 counts.
Cholesky of $M$ is logged as a named control, never as a
construction of $F$.

\section{Finite identities}\label{sec:satz}

\begin{lemma}[Chebyshev addition]\label{lem:cheb}
Over $\mathbb{Q}$, $T_2(x)\,T_1(x)=\bigl(T_3(x)+T_1(x)\bigr)/2$.
At $x=3/7$: $T_2=-31/49$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Cauchy kernel]\label{lem:cauchy}
Over $\mathbb{Q}$,
\[
  \frac{1}{(m+z)(m+w)}
  \;=\;
  \frac{1}{z-w}\Bigl(\frac{1}{m+w}-\frac{1}{m+z}\Bigr)
  \qquad(z\neq w).
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Digamma--Loewner]\label{lem:loewner}
For $z,w\in\mathbb{C}\setminus\{0,-1,-2,\ldots\}$,
\[
  \frac{\psi(z)-\overline{\psi(w)}}{z-\overline{w}}
  \;=\;
  \sum_{m=0}^\infty \frac{1}{(m+z)\,(m+\overline{w})}.
\]
At $z=\tfrac14+\tfrac7{10}i$, $w=\tfrac14-\tfrac3{10}i$,
mpmath $dps=40$ matches the series to $4.6\cdot 10^{-41}$.
\end{lemma}

The identity is classical
($\psi(z)=-\gamma+\sum_m\bigl[(m+1)^{-1}-(m+z)^{-1}\bigr]$)
and is \emph{not} the claim that the archimedean block of
$A_0$ equals this Gram.

\begin{lemma}[fold is linear in lags]\label{lem:fold}
The folded signed weights are the cosine transform of the
lag sequence~$c$.
On FRAME-A, $\|w-(w_P+w_A)\|_2/\|w\|_2=3.5\cdot 10^{-16}$.
\end{lemma}

Prime and archimedean contributions therefore add
\emph{before} the dual Christoffel--Darboux map.
The CD Gram itself is not linear in the weights
(dual weights $u^\vee\propto(1-x)^2/(Su\kappa^2)$ invert $u$).

\begin{lemma}[the ref-negative compression is already PD]\label{lem:qa0}
On FRAME-A, $Q^T A_0 Q$ has $\indm=0$ and
$\lambda_{\min}=5.227\cdot 10^{-3}$.
\end{lemma}

The remaining negative of $A_0$
($\indm A_0=1$, $\lambda_{\min}(A_0)=-3.85\cdot 10^{-2}$)
does \emph{not} live in the reference-negative space.
Any identity of the form~\eqref{eq:G} on this $Q$ would have
to have $\ell=0$ and $Q^T A_0 Q=FF^T$ with no defect.
The one-defect, if it exists, sits in the Uvarov complement
(cross terms $B_{-+}$ and the half-modes of $R^{\mathrm{ref}}$).

\section{The class audit}\label{sec:audit}

\begin{prop}[class 3 fails]\label{prop:class}
On FRAME-A the source-explicit candidates of legs 0, 2, 3, 4, 5
do not satisfy~\eqref{eq:G}.
The only exact factor of $M$ is Cholesky of $M$
(class~1).
\end{prop}

\paragraph{Leg 0.}
Chebyshev-$U$ (equivalently $\sin((k+1)\varphi)/\sin\varphi$)
restricted to $Y$ spans all of $\mathbb{R}^{104}$, hence spans
the $49$-space tautologically.
No consecutive $k$-block of size $49$ recovers $V_n$
(max principal angle $90^\circ$ for the first $49$ and the last
$49$ modes).
Greedy energy selection needs all $104$ modes.
The negative space of the explicit BS dual is not a Fourier
subset of $Y$.
The numerical projector of $A_{\mathrm{ref}}$ remains the
control --- class~2 coordinates, not an analytic $Y_k$.

\paragraph{Leg 2 (Euler).}
Features
$f^{c,s}_{p,r}(j)=\sqrt{2\log p}\,n^{-1/4}\cos/\sin\bigl((\log n/\Delta)\varphi_j\bigr)$
give a Gram whose compression to $V_n$ has residual $209$
against $M$, with Frobenius alignment $0.767$ after the fact.
A fitted global scale still leaves residual $0.642$.
The addition theorem is true as trigonometry
(Lemma~\ref{lem:cheb}) and the tent/cosine transform is true
as the definition of the lag map (Lemma~\ref{lem:fold});
neither survives the dual CD Gram.
Fold/Tent is an outer \emph{linear} transfer at the weight
layer, not at $A_0$.

\paragraph{Leg 3 (Digamma).}
Lemma~\ref{lem:loewner} holds on the function.
The Cauchy--Loewner Gram of
$z_j=\tfrac14+\tfrac12 i\,\varphi_j$ has residual $207$
against $A_0$ and alignment $0.098$ against $M$.
The archimedean block of $A_0$ is not this Loewner matrix.
A Digamma shift $\tfrac14\to\tfrac34$ stays at residual $38$
(still $O(1)$).

\paragraph{Leg 4 (Uvarov).}
Omitting the Gram part of $B$ on the negative space returns
$\indm=49$; keeping only that Gram leaves $\indm=13$
(r403).
The cross terms carry $\|B_{\mathrm{cross}}\|_F/\|B\|_F=0.617$
and numerical rank $104$.
Stacked tent or Euler features on $(Y,Y^c)$ do not reproduce
$B$ (YY-block residual $1$ and $266$).
``Only-Gram leaves $13$'' is the incomplete-PSD phenomenon of
r403, not the off-diagonal of a source factor.

\paragraph{Leg 5 (null mode).}
$|{\cos}|(v_-,1)=0.007$;
$|{\cos}|(v_-,V_n)=0.762$ and
$|{\cos}|(v_-,V_n^\perp)=0.648$ --- the defect is mixed.
The candidate
$\ell=\sqrt{\sum_{p\le e^{2\alpha}}\log p}\,Q^T 1$
has cosine $0.069$ with the lowest mode of $Q^T A_0 Q$
(which is already positive).
The ones-mode is not the null mode.

\section{Mutant table}\label{sec:mut}

A class-3 identity must break loudly under source mutants.
Because no class-3 identity is present, residuals stay $O(1)$.
Cholesky of $M$ stays exact on MAIN and ceases to exist as
soon as $M$ leaves the PD cone.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcc@{}}
\toprule
mutant & Euler/Cauchy residual vs $M$ & Cholesky of $M$ \\
\midrule
MAIN (von Mangoldt) & $209$ (align $0.767$) & $2.3\cdot 10^{-16}$ \\
$\Lambda$-permutation & $314$ & undefined ($M$ has $6$ negatives) \\
position jitter $10^{-3}$ & $209$ & --- \\
$\Lambda(p^r)\mapsto\log(p^r)$ & $249$ & --- \\
Digamma $\tfrac14\to\tfrac34$ & $38$ & --- \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The $\Lambda$-permutation ratio $314/209\approx 1.5$ is not an
explosion from residual~$0$.
It is a reshuffling of an already failed candidate.
Cholesky, by contrast, is bit-exact on every world in which
$M$ happens to be PD --- including none of the
mask-preserving permutations of FRAME-A.

\section{What is missing}\label{sec:miss}

The dual CD Gram is a nonlinear function of the folded
weights.
Legs 2--3 factor the \emph{linear} lag/weight layer
(Lemmas~\ref{lem:cheb}--\ref{lem:fold}), which is the right
algebra for the spectral density and the wrong algebra for
$A_0$.
An exact source factor of $A_0$ would have to intertwine
the Borodin dual inversion $u\mapsto u^\vee$ and the
depth-$(N_w-3)$ OP recurrence with the Euler/Digamma Gram
--- a dictionary that is not in the r342 Digamma/tent list
and is not produced by restricting Chebyshev-$U$ to $Y$.
The compression $Q^T A_0 Q$ being already PD
(Lemma~\ref{lem:qa0}) further shows that the one defect of
MAIN does not sit where the reviewer placed the null mode.
The stop rule forbids iterating a Cholesky factor, a
numerical square root of $M$, or a fitted scale of the Euler
Gram (alignment $0.767$ is a census, not a construction).
