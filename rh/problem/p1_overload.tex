\begin{abstract}\noindent
The reviewer reframes P1 as $\indm(A_0)\le 1$ on the
selected sequence (vacuous windows are the first case,
not an error).
The object $A_0=R_{N-3}-\tfrac12 I$ lives three ranks
before the end of the window.
The last exact dictionary is $R=C(I+C)^{-1}$ (r407), so
$\indm(A_0)=\nC(N_w-3)$ with $\nC(n)=\#\{\lambda(C(n))<1\}$
and $C(n)$ the $\mu$-only dual CD Gram on the holes $Y$.
The nested OP matrix gives the constructor identity
$C(n+1)-C(n)=vv^{\mathrm{T}}$ (rank-one Loewner); hence
$\nC$ is antitone and drops by at most one
(\texttt{rankOne\_inertia\_antitone}).
The unique interpolant at depth $d=|Y|$ still has
$\nC\gg 1$ (flagship $43$).
Extra OP ranks lift the excess down to $0$ or $1$ on
every selected window, and get stuck at $19$--$21$ on
permute / jitter / scramble.
A universal Gantmacher--Krein cap $\indm(W_Y-S_0)\le 1$
for the r431 Cauchy--$\pi$ residual is refuted already
on $2\times 2$ and $3\times 3$ toys over $\mathbb{Q}$.
No RH claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{The nested CD chain}\label{sec:cd}

Let $Y$ be the holes of a window, $u^{\vee}$ the r356
dual weights, and $B_n$ the $n$-column $\mu$-OP matrix
on $Y$ dressed by $\sqrt{u^{\vee}|_Y}$ (r407
\texttt{chain\_C}).
Write $C(n)=B_n B_n^{\mathrm{T}}$.

\begin{lemma}[nested rank-one Loewner]\label{lem:nested}
If $B_{n}=[B_{n-1}\mid v]$ then
$C(n)-C(n-1)=vv^{\mathrm{T}}\succeq 0$ of rank at most one.
Consequently $\nC(n)$ is nonincreasing and
$\nC(n-1)-1\le \nC(n)\le \nC(n-1)$
(Cauchy interlacing; Lean
\texttt{rankOne\_inertia\_antitone}).
At working depth, $\nC(N_w-3)=\indm(A_0)$ (r407/r412
graph-resolvent dictionary).
\end{lemma}

The identity is the constructor, not a fit.
On flagship MAIN ($k_z=9$, $|Y|=104$, $N_w-3=181$) every
sampled consecutive update has numerical rank $1$
(Loewner floor $-10^{-13}$).

\begin{lemma}[alternating $\Delta$]\label{lem:delta}
For ordered holes $y_1<\cdots<y_q$, the barycentric
weights $\pi_Y'(y_j)=\prod_{k\neq j}(y_j-y_k)$ alternate
in sign, $\mathrm{sign}(\pi_Y'(y_j))=(-1)^{q-1-j}$.
\end{lemma}

This is the sign bookkeeping of the r431 Cauchy--$\pi$
formula $S_0=\Delta^{-1}C\Pi W_X\Pi C^{\mathrm{T}}\Delta^{-1}$.
It does \emph{not} cap $\indm(W_Y-S_0)$.

\section{Gantmacher--Krein at interpolating depth}\label{sec:gk}

At $d_0=|Y|$ the interpolant is unique (kernel dimension
$0$) and $S_0$ is the Cauchy--$\pi$ kernel of the
$\pi_Y(X)^2$-dressed $X$-measure (r431, exact over
$\mathbb{Q}$).
Cauchy kernels of positive masses are totally positive;
$\Delta$ is alternating.
The candidate ``$W_Y$ minus a sign-regular dressing has
at most one negative square'' is \emph{false} as a
universal identity.

\begin{prop}[no universal $n_-$ cap at $k_{\mathrm{dim}}=0$]\label{prop:gk}
On the r409 five-atom toy at $d_0=|Y|=2$,
$D_0=W_Y-S_0$ has inertia $(1,1,0)$ and
$\det D_0=-3740/243<0$ (one negative square);
$S_0$ and the undressed Pick $P_X$ are positive definite.
Raising the depth by one lifts $D_0$ to inertia $(2,0,0)$,
and $D_0(3)-D_0(2)$ has inertia $(1,0,1)$ (rank-one PSD).
On an independent six-node toy, $d_0=3,4,5$ gives
$\indm(D_0)=2,1,0$.
A mass-imbalanced five-atom dual with the same two holes
has inertia $(0,2,0)$ and $\det D_0=113/2880000>0$
(negative definite already in $2\times 2$).
\end{prop}

Thus Gantmacher--Krein / variation-diminishing of the
Cauchy--$\pi$ residual does not forbid two negative
directions, even in the smallest cases.
The interpolating-depth residual is the \emph{wrong
depth} for the overload bound: on MAIN,
$\nC(|Y|)=43\gg 1$.

\section{The depth curve}\label{sec:curve}

\begin{prop}[source vs.\ adversaries]\label{prop:curve}
On flagship MAIN,
\[
\nC(1)=104,\quad
\nC(|Y|)=43,\quad
\nC(181)=1,\quad
\min\{n:\nC(n)\le 1\}=178.
\]
The last six values are $(2,2,1,1,1,1)$ for
$n=176,\ldots,181$: the $2\to 1$ drop is at $N_w-6$,
then three frozen ranks at $1$.
$\Lambda$-permute on the frozen mask: $\nC(|Y|)=54$,
$\nC(181)=20$, never hits $1$ (still dropping at the
last step).
Gaussian jitter ends at $19$; position-scramble at $21$
(different hole set $|Y|=68$).
A two-period world has $N_w-3=8<|Y|=11$ and ends at
$\nC=4$ (working depth before interpolating depth).
\end{prop}

The source and the adversaries obey the same rank-one
algebra; they differ by \emph{save rate} (how many of
the $N_w-4$ updates push an eigenvalue across $1$).
MAIN spends $103$ of $180$ updates on a crossing and
finishes at $1$; permute spends $83$ and finishes at $20$.

False depth: at $n=N_w-1$ MAIN has $\nC=0$ (the leftover
square \emph{is} liftable by extra OP).
The cutoff $N_w-3$ is the object, not a saturated
identity.

\section{Two-case census}\label{sec:census}

\begin{prop}[selected two-case]\label{prop:census}
On the $42$ admissible MAIN windows,
$\nC(N_w-3)\in\{0,1\}$ on $42/42$ ($28$ one-defect,
$14$ vacuous).
The $2\to 1$ locking rank (when it exists) sits at most
$6$ steps before $N_w-3$; the $1\to 0$ lift on vacuous
windows sits at most $3$ steps before $N_w-3$.
Living $\chi_3$ $37/37$ and $\chi_4$ $41/41$ keep
$\nC\in\{0,1\}$; dead $\chi_3$ $5/5$ and $\chi_4$-$20$
likewise (death remains the edge, r401).
\end{prop}

No margin correlations are used.

\section{What is a theorem, what is open}\label{sec:honest}

The nested CD chain, its rank-one Loewner updates, the
interlacing bound on $\nC$, and the r407 dictionary
$\nC(N_w-3)=\indm(A_0)$ are theorems.
That a totally positive Cauchy--$\pi$ residual has
$\indm\le 1$ is closed (false).
The selected save-rate $\nC(N_w-3)\le 1$ is a
$42/42$ census, not a source-pure identity: the
remaining question is why the von~Mangoldt dual OP
sequence on $Y$ spends enough updates on sub-threshold
modes, while a permutation of the same masses does not.
P1 stays $\lambda_2(C)\ge 1$ at degree $N_w-3$.
No RH claim.
