\begin{abstract}\noindent
The production-faithful selected windows of round 463 are certified by
outward interval arithmetic at every fixed index $k=5,\ldots,12$.
The proof encloses the complete von-Mangoldt source, folded signed
moments, two nested moment matrices, the smooth-border functional, and
the terminal quotient $\qdag$.  A naive interval Stieltjes--Lanczos
recurrence is complete only for $k=5,6$; dependency widening makes its
norm interval contain zero at degrees $11,11,10,10,9,8$ for
$k=7,\ldots,12$.  The stable route is therefore a direct Chebyshev
moment certificate with an exact basis change, validated Cholesky
lower bounds, and residual quadratic-form enclosures.

The resulting theorem is finite:
\[
 k\in\{5,\ldots,12\}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \Rd(W_k)\succeq\tfrac12 I .
\]
It does not prove the frequently-selected quantifier, close any
external Lean dictionary, or prove the Riemann hypothesis.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact sealed object}

For $k\ge5$ put
\[
 r_k=\lfloor\sqrt{k}\rfloor,\qquad
 a_k=2^k,\qquad m_k=k2^{r_k}-1,\qquad
 M_k=m_k+1,
\]
\[
 L_k=2M_k-2,\qquad N_k=M_k/2,\qquad
 \Delta_k=\frac{\log a_k}{M_k}=2^{-r_k}\log2 .
\]
This is the selected geometry used in
\texttt{RH/FaithfulFold.lean}.  The complete arithmetic lag is
\[
 c_i^P=-\sum_{\substack{n=p^\ell\le a_k^2\\p\ {\rm prime}}}
 \frac{\log p}{\sqrt n}
 \left[
 t_{\Delta_k}(i\Delta_k-\log n)
 +t_{\Delta_k}(i\Delta_k+\log n)
 \right],
\quad
 t_\Delta(x)=\max(0,1-|x|/\Delta).
\]
The prime-power list is generated by an integer Eratosthenes sieve;
$n$, $p$, $a_k$, $m_k$, $M_k$, $L_k$, and all tent coefficients are
exact integers or rationals.  The operations $\log p$, $\log n$,
$\sqrt n$, and $\log2$ are evaluated by outward
\texttt{mpmath.iv} intervals.  For $k\ge5$ one has
$\Delta_k\le\log2/4$, so the reflected prime tent is rigorously zero.
Moreover $n\ge a_k$ implies
$\log n\ge M_k\Delta_k$, hence all its tents at
$i=0,\ldots,M_k-1$ vanish.  The implementation still counts every
prime power through $a_k^2$, but evaluates only the proved-active
prefix $n<a_k$.

The archimedean lag $c_i^A$ is the r463
\texttt{productionArchLag}: the deployed 48-node Gauss--Legendre
realization of the two smooth segments.  Each Legendre root is enclosed
in radius $10^{-90}$ at 120 decimal digits; opposite endpoint signs of
$P_{48}$, disjoint root intervals, and
$2\in\sum_{\ell=1}^{48}\omega_\ell$ are checked.  Transcendental
evaluation uses \texttt{GL\_N=48} and \texttt{DPS=70}.  In the zero-lag branch the
constants are outward enclosures of Euler's constant and $\log\pi$.

The smooth completion is the production grid
\[
 u_\ell=\frac{\ell}{100},\qquad
 1\le\ell,\quad u_\ell<2\log a_k,\qquad
 \beta_\ell=\frac{2}{100}e^{u_\ell/2},
\]
sent through the same ordinary tents, archimedean lag, spectral
density, and fold.  This fixes the r463 completion data used here; its
budget is not an unrelated opaque scalar.

To include the literal deployed r463 arrays as well as the ideal
interval realization, each lag interval is finally hulled with the
corresponding binary64 production value.  A binary64 endpoint is an
exact dyadic rational.  It is not trusted as an approximation:
enlarging an interval can only make certification harder.  This
compatibility hull explains the visible widths at $k\ge9$ and ensures
that every r459/r463 point value lies inside the published certificate.

For $1\le j\le M_k-1$ set
\[
 \theta_j=\frac{2\pi j}{L_k},\qquad x_j=\cos\theta_j,
\]
\[
 d_j=c_0+2\sum_{i=1}^{M_k-2}c_i\cos(i\theta_j)
          +c_{M_k-1}\cos((M_k-1)\theta_j),
\]
\[
 w_j=\frac{2}{L_k}(1-\cos\theta_j)d_j ,
\]
with $w_{M_k-1}$ divided by two.  These are exactly the r463
lag $\to$ spectral density $\to$ $L=2M-2$ fold rules.
The positive and negative parts of $w_j$ are the two production
channels.  The interval signs are fixed on all eight windows.

\section{Stable certificate route}

Let $\sigma_k=\sum_jw_j\delta_{x_j}$ be the main signed fold and let
$\tau_k$ be the signed smooth-border fold.  In the Chebyshev basis
$T_i(\cos\theta)=\cos(i\theta)$ define, for $n\le N_k$,
\[
 (H_n)_{ab}=\int T_aT_b\,d\sigma_k,\qquad
 (g_n)_a=\int T_a\,d\tau_k,\qquad 0\le a,b<n.
\]
No sign-split subtraction is performed numerically: the signed
weights enter directly.  Product-to-sum gives
\[
 (H_n)_{ab}=\frac{h_{|a-b|}+h_{a+b}}2,\qquad
 h_s=\int T_s\,d\sigma_k ,
\]
which avoids both Vandermonde powers and the interval dependency of a
Stieltjes recurrence.

For each $n=N_k-1,N_k$, a frozen binary64 inverse-Cholesky factor is
used as a fixed basis matrix $C_n$.  Its entries are interpreted as
exact dyadic rationals and its diagonal is nonzero.  Thus
$C_n^TH_nC_n\succ0$ is equivalent to $H_n\succ0$; no computed
eigenvalue enters the theorem.  On the transformed interval matrix
the checker proves
\[
 \lambda_{\min}(C_n^TH_nC_n)\ge\mu_n>0
\]
by Cholesky of the midpoint shifted by half the floating hint,
Higham's backward-error bound
\[
 |\delta A|\le\gamma_{n+1}|L||L^T|,
\qquad
\gamma_s=\frac{(s+2)2^{-53}}{1-(s+2)2^{-53}},
\]
and subtraction of the interval-radius row sum.  Every radius is
inflated by $1+2^{-38}$ and $10^{-300}$.

\begin{lemma}[Residual enclosure]\label{lem:residual}
If $H\succeq\mu I$ with $\mu>0$, then for every seed $x$ and
$r=g-Hx$,
\[
 g^TH^{-1}g
 =2g^Tx-x^THx+r^TH^{-1}r
\in
\left[
 2g^Tx-x^THx,\,
 2g^Tx-x^THx+\frac{\|r\|_2^2}{\mu}
\right].
\]
The displayed endpoints are interpreted as outward intervals when
$H$ and $g$ are interval data.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Write $g=Hx+r$ and expand both sides.  Since
$0\preceq H^{-1}\preceq\mu^{-1}I$, the remainder lies in
$[0,\|r\|_2^2/\mu]$.
\end{proof}

The binary64 solve is only a seed for Lemma~\ref{lem:residual}; a poor
seed widens the enclosure and cannot create a pass.  Set
\[
 s_n=g_n^TH_n^{-1}g_n .
\]
The r453 partial-Parseval identity and the production completion give
\[
 B_w=s_{N_k-1}+\frac57,\qquad
 \qdag_k=\frac{s_{N_k}}{B_w}.
\]
Independent lower/upper residual enclosures of $s_{N_k-1}$ and
$s_{N_k}$ therefore yield
\[
 \frac{\underline s_{N_k}}
      {\overline s_{N_k-1}+5/7}
\le\qdag_k\le
 \frac{\overline s_{N_k}}
      {\underline s_{N_k-1}+5/7}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Finite selected-window certificate]\label{thm:cert}
For the exact production-faithful objects above,
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \text{for every }k\in\{5,\ldots,12\},\qquad
 \Rd(W_k)\succeq\tfrac12 I
 \quad}
\]
is rigorously certified.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The checker proves $H_{N_k-1}\succ0$ and $H_{N_k}\succ0$ by the
validated Cholesky bounds just described.  It then applies
Lemma~\ref{lem:residual}; Table~\ref{tab:cert} gives outward intervals
strictly contained in $(0,1)$ for every $\qdag_k$.
The r460 Schur-complement identity identifies $1-\qdag_k$ as the last
pivot of the augmented dual-resolvent form.  Its leading block is
positive by $H_{N_k}\succ0$, so the augmented form is positive
definite.  The production whitening congruence then gives
$\Rd(W_k)-\tfrac12I\succeq0$.
\end{proof}

\section{Why the chain was abandoned}

The same lag hull was propagated through a naive monic interval
Stieltjes recurrence.  It certifies all $10$ and $12$ required degrees
at $k=5,6$, but reaches only
\[
 11/14,\ 11/16,\ 10/36,\ 10/40,\ 9/44,\ 8/48
\]
for $k=7,\ldots,12$.  At the next degree the interval norm contains
zero, so division would be invalid.  Increasing transcendental
precision does not remove the deployed binary64 hull; recurrence
dependency repeatedly reuses the same uncertain coefficients and
widens algebraically.  The machine therefore reports refusal and
switches to the direct moment matrix.  It never treats the midpoint
chain as a certificate.

\section{Lean interoperability design}

There are two honest ports.

\paragraph{Exact dyadic data block.}
Export each sealed decimal endpoint, the binary64 basis matrix, the
shift, Cholesky factor, and row-sum allowance as integers over powers
of two or ten.  A small Lean checker can reconstruct the rational
matrix inequalities and decide positive pivots using
\texttt{native\_decide}.  Transcendental source enclosures remain
separate verified-data hypotheses, with hashes tying them to the
Python generator.

\paragraph{Direct rational proof terms.}
Round every interval endpoint outward to a rational and export
fraction-free $LDL^T$ witnesses for the shifted transformed moment
matrices.  Lean can verify reconstruction and positive pivots with
\texttt{norm\_num}; the residual norm and quotient inequalities are
then rational arithmetic.  This is slower to generate but minimizes
trusted executable code.

Either port should expose a proposition such as
\[
 \texttt{SelectedFiniteCert}\ k:
 \quad 5\le k\land k\le12\longrightarrow
 \Rd(W_k)\succeq\tfrac12I .
\]
It does not prove the frequently quantifier
$\exists^\infty k,\ \Rd(W_k)\succeq\tfrac12I$.
Finite certificates formalize base cases and test the object
dictionary; they cannot supply the missing cofinal mechanism.
Moreover, r463 still presents \texttt{productionArchLag} and
\texttt{productionCompletion} through interfaces.  A Lean data port
must identify the exported blocks with those interfaces; otherwise it
proves only a parallel rational object.

\section*{Claim boundary}

This is a rigorous theorem about eight fixed, explicitly sealed
production-faithful finite matrices.  It does not prove the frequently
selected statement, does not close the source-derived cofinal
spectral-overlap gap of r460, and does not validate the external
explicit-formula or density bridges.  No RH claim and no anti-RH
claim are made.
