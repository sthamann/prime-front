\section{The scalar}\label{sec:obj}

Write $A_{0}=R_{N-3}-\tfrac12 I$ on the hole nodes $Y$,
$U_{\mathrm{CD}}$ the last two dual Christoffel--Darboux
columns, and $s$ the Sherman--Morrison border vector of
r362/r401, with border scale $\mathrm{den}>0$.
The mixed $3\times 3$ is
\[
\Phi
=\begin{pmatrix} K_{2} & c_{0}\\ c_{0}^{T} & \varphi_{bb}\end{pmatrix},
\quad
K_{2}=I+U_{\mathrm{CD}}^{T}A_{0}^{-1}U_{\mathrm{CD}},
\]
$c_{0}=U_{\mathrm{CD}}^{T}A_{0}^{-1}s$ and
$\varphi_{bb}=\mathrm{den}-2+s^{T}A_{0}^{-1}s$.
The Schur complement of $K_{2}$ in $\Phi$ is the scalar
$\sch=\varphi_{bb}-c_{0}^{T}K_{2}^{-1}c_{0}$.
Haynsworth (r401) converts the border shift
$\indm\Phi=1+\indm A_{0}$ into $\sch<0$.

After the $2\times 2$ Sylvester map $D_{2}$ of $K_{2}$,
$(\tilde a,\tilde b)^{T}=D_{2}^{T}c_{0}$ are the
\emph{unnormalized} couplings; the r401 normalized
$\tau=(a,b)$ is $(\tilde a,\tilde b)/\sqrt{|\sch|}$.
The normalized family $\Phi_{\mathrm{edge}}$ has Schur
complement $\operatorname{sign}(\sch)$ identically, so the
sign is a chart choice, not a region of the $(a,b)$ disk.

\section{Finite identities}\label{sec:satz}

\begin{lemma}[Woodbury]\label{lem:wood}
\[
\sch
\;=\;
\mathrm{den}-2+s^{T}(A_{0}+U_{\mathrm{CD}}U_{\mathrm{CD}}^{T})^{-1}s.
\]
On the rational $P1$ toy $A_{0}=\operatorname{diag}(-\tfrac12,2,3)$,
$s=e_{1}$, $\mathrm{den}=1$ one has $\sch=-\tfrac23$ exactly.
On the vacuous toy $A_{0}=\operatorname{diag}(2,3,-1,4)$,
$s=e_{0}$, $\mathrm{den}=\tfrac12$ one has $\sch=-\tfrac76$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Sylvester chart]\label{lem:chart}
Let $J_{2}=\operatorname{diag}(\operatorname{sign}\mu(K_{2}))$.
On the P1 chart $J_{2}=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p1}
\sch
\;=\;
\varphi_{bb}+\tilde a^{2}-\tilde b^{2}.
\end{equation}
On the vacuous chart $J_{2}=I_{2}$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:vac}
\sch
\;=\;
\varphi_{bb}-(\tilde a^{2}+\tilde b^{2}).
\end{equation}
Both identities agree with Lemma~\ref{lem:wood} over
$\mathbb{Q}$ (P1: $\varphi_{bb}=-\tfrac12$, $\tilde b^{2}=\tfrac16$;
vacuous: $\varphi_{bb}=-1$).
\end{lemma}

\begin{remark}[who carries the sign]\label{rem:split}
Write $Q=s^{T}(A_{0}+U_{\mathrm{CD}}U_{\mathrm{CD}}^{T})^{-1}s$.
Then $\sch=Q-(2-\mathrm{den})$.
On the named census $Q\in[0.283,0.502]$ and
$\mathrm{den}\in[1.460,1.652]$ are both $O(1)$; $\sch$ is
their difference, of order $0.04$, not the $O(1)$ $K_{2}$-excess
of r375.  On the vacuous chart with $\varphi_{bb}<0$,
\eqref{eq:vac} forces $\sch<0$ for every coupling.
On the P1 chart the danger axis is large $|\tilde a|$
(the negative $K_{2}$ direction adds $+\tilde a^{2}$ to $\sch$).
\end{remark}

\section{What is a theorem, what is a census}\label{sec:honest}

Lemma~\ref{lem:wood} and Lemma~\ref{lem:chart} are theorems.
The reduction is: on the vacuous chart, $\varphi_{bb}<0$
implies $\sch<0$ outright; on the P1 chart,
$\tau_{\mathrm{un}}\to 0$ plus a uniform $\varphi_{bb}\le-\delta<0$
would give $\sch<0$ cofinally.
The census supports $\tau\to 0$, $|\sch|$ flat of order $0.04$,
and $\varphi_{bb}<0$ at large $N$, two-sided against dead $\chi$.
The observed rate $|\tau|\sim N^{-0.47}$ is \emph{not} a
corollary of the r392 Uvarov kernel update nor of fold
telescopy: the identities exist as exact finite-rank updates
of the CD kernel, but they do not force the slope
(gate \texttt{RATE\_OPEN}; PNT-free in form --- the sums are
window-finite --- and not RH-near).
No legitimate $k_{0}$, so $\sch_{k}<0$ cofinally on the
selected sequence is not proved.
Normalized $\tau_{\infty}=0$ does not evaluate $\sch(\tau_{\infty})$:
the scale was quotiented.
The $O(1)$ $K_{2}$-excess of r375 is a different coordinate.
No RH claim.
