\begin{abstract}\noindent
After the high-moment kill failed (r398), the reviewer route
to $R^\dagger_k\succ\tfrac12 I$ on the selected sequence runs
through a source-pure Weyl energy of the centered mass
difference $d=\mu-\nu-d^{\mathrm{ref}}$.
This note records the exact Fourier--Dirichlet representation
of $\dhat_k(r)$ (tent interpolant, even-circulant extension,
Fej\'er Laplacian, arch/Gamma/pole subtracted as
$d^{\mathrm{ref}}$), derives the transfer weights
$\omega_{k,r}$ from the r389 Chebyshev--Christoffel--Darboux
identity, splits the three source-defined edge modes, and
attacks the bulk sum by Montgomery--Vaughan mean value and
Gallagher's lemma on the lag lattice.
What is proved: the representation, the Laplacian identity,
$C_0=C_1=0$ identically after Fej\'er, and that the CD band
$m<N_w-3$ already excludes the cap.
What is refuted: $E_k^{\mathrm{bulk}}\le\eps_k\to 0$ in that
norm.
On core-$42$ the energy grows as $N_w^{0.54}$ and sits at
quadratic-mean ($\mathrm{mean}\,C_m^2/\mathrm{qm}\in[0.43,0.85]$);
on the selected sequence $a_k=2^k$ it grows through $k=9$.
The unconditional large-sieve majorant is $10^3$--$10^6$ too
large.
A bound that would force decay below quadratic-mean is
RH-near, hence circular as a strategy for $R^\dagger\succ\tfrac12 I$,
and is in any case contradicted by the census.
No RH claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{The object}\label{sec:obj}

On a window of log-cutoff $\alpha$ and lag-mesh $\Delta$,
write $M$ for the number of lags, $L=2M-2$ for the circulant
length, $\theta_j=2\pi j/L$.
The prime lag sequence is the tent interpolant of the von
Mangoldt comb~\cite{v563,r283}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cP}
c^P_i
\;=\;
-\sum_{n\le e^{2\alpha}}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\,
t_\Delta^{\mathrm{circ}}(i\Delta-\log n),
\end{equation}
$t_\Delta$ the unit tent of width $\Delta$, even-extended
across $i\mapsto L-i$, plus the reflected tent for
$\log n<\Delta$.
The arch lags $c^A$ are the explicit incomplete-Gamma /
digamma kernel of~\cite{v563} (Euler, $\log\pi$, Bose factor
$e^{-w/2}/(1-e^{-2w})$).
Spectral densities $d^P=\mathrm{FFT}(c^P_{\mathrm{ext}})$ and
$d^A=\mathrm{FFT}(c^A_{\mathrm{ext}})$ are real and even.
Folding $j\leftrightarrow L-j$ onto $x=\cos\theta_j$ with the
Chebyshev-$U$ factor $(2/L)(1-\cos\theta)$ produces the signed
union $\mu-\nu$.

\begin{definition}[centered mass]\label{def:d}
On the folded cosine grid $j=1,\ldots,L/2$,
\[
d_{k,j}
\;=\;
\mu_{k,j}-\nu_{k,j}-d^{\mathrm{ref}}_{k,j}
\;=\;
\frac2L(1-\cos\theta_j)\,d^P_j,
\]
where $d^{\mathrm{ref}}_j=(2/L)(1-\cos\theta_j)\,d^A_j$ is the
explicit Bernstein--Szeg\H{o} / arch background of~\cite{r390}
(Fej\'er on the full cosine grid is discrete Chebyshev-$U$).
The last node $j=L/2$ carries the fold-half.
\end{definition}

The Fourier coefficients on the unfolded circle are
$\dhat_k(r)=\sum_{j=0}^{L-1}d^{\mathrm{unf}}_{k,j}e^{-2\pi i rj/L}$,
and the folded cosine transform is
$C_m=\sum_{j=1}^{L/2}d_{k,j}\cos(m\theta_j)$.
Machine identity: $C_m=\tfrac12\mathrm{Re}\,\dhat_k(m)$.

\section{Exact representation}\label{sec:rep}

\begin{lemma}[tent interpolant]\label{lem:dcent}
$d_{k,j}=(2/L)(1-\cos\theta_j)\,d^P_j$ identically (folded
maxdev $0$ on FRAME-A $w=9$).
For every frequency bin $j$,
\[
d^P_j
\;=\;
-\sum_{n\le e^{2\alpha}}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\,K_{k,j}(\log n),
\]
where $K_{k,j}(u)$ is the discrete Fourier transform at bin
$j$ of the even-circulant tent centered at $u$.
Writing $t_{k,j}=2\pi j/(L\Delta)$ and
$f_n=\{\log n/\Delta\}$,
$K_{k,j}(\log n)=n^{-it_{k,j}}\sigma_k(f_n,j)$ with interpolant
symbol $\lvert\sigma_k\rvert\le 1$ (two-tap plus reflection:
a contractive linear operator).
The representation is the Dirichlet polynomial
$S_k(t)=\sum_{n\le e^{2\alpha}}\Lambda(n)\,n^{-1/2+it}$
twisted by $\sigma_k$, plus the explicit arch/Gamma/pole
terms that have been subtracted as $d^{\mathrm{ref}}$.
Checked at machine precision on $w=9$ (maxdev $2.5\cdot10^{-13}$),
$kz=18$ ($2.0\cdot10^{-13}$) and $kz=52$ ($3.9\cdot10^{-12}$).
\end{lemma}

The tent width equals the mesh, so $K_{k,j}$ has no extra
sinc-decay in $j$: high-frequency decay, if any, must come
from cancellation in $S_k(t_{k,j})$ itself.
Folding is the even extension, also linear and contractive.

\begin{lemma}[Fej\'er Laplacian]\label{lem:lap}
Let $a$ be the even-circulant extension of $c^P$ and
$\Delta a=2a-\mathrm{roll}(a,1)-\mathrm{roll}(a,-1)$.
On the unfolded grid,
\[
\mathrm{IFFT}\bigl(\Fej\odot d^P\bigr)
\;=\;
\frac{\Delta a}{L},
\qquad
\Fej_j=\frac2L\bigl(1-\cos\theta_j\bigr).
\]
The stencil is $h_0=2/L$, $h_{\pm1}=-1/L$, mass zero.
Exact over $\Q$ on a $6$-cycle; FRAME-A residual
$8.7\cdot10^{-19}$.
\end{lemma}

Thus $\dhat_k(m)$ is (up to the conventional $\tfrac12$) the
$m$-th Fourier coefficient of the discrete Laplacian of the
tent-sampled von Mangoldt comb: a Dirichlet polynomial in
the lags.

\section{The transfer norm}\label{sec:omega}

\begin{lemma}[CD weights]\label{lem:omega}
The r389 Chebyshev-$T$ Christoffel--Darboux energy of a signed
atomic mass $v$ on the cosine grid is
\[
\Phi_k
\;=\;
\frac1\pi C_0^2+\frac2\pi\sum_{m=1}^{k-1}C_m^2,
\qquad
C_m=\sum_j v_j\cos(m\theta_j).
\]
Taking $v=d$ and $k=N_w-3$ (the depth of $R_{N-3}$ and of the
Uvarov kernel that feeds $\Delta^2\log\tau$~\cite{r392,r393})
gives the transfer weights
$\omega_0=1/\pi$, $\omega_m=2/\pi$ for $1\le m<N_w-3$, and
$\omega_m=0$ outside the CD band.
This is the unique bandlimited CD bilinear; it is not a
fitted kernel.
\end{lemma}

The r392 Uvarov identity writes $\gamma_n(\mu)$ as a
$\tau$-ratio against the Bernstein--Szeg\H{o} chain, with
$\tau_n=\det(I-K_n[\Xi]W)$ and $K_n$ the CD kernel of the
background.
The Hilbert--Schmidt energy of that kernel on the deleted
nodes is exactly $\Phi$ at depth $n$.
Assist and $d\Delta$ are Uvarov readouts of the same Gram.
On core-$42$, $\mathrm{corr}(E/N_w,\mathrm{assist})=0.18$:
the named norm does \emph{not} dominate Assist as a closing
majorant (the $N$-confound $\mathrm{corr}(E,\mathrm{assist})=-0.78$
is the opposite slope).
The $5.4$ bridge is a named energy, not a proved inequality.

\section{Edge split}\label{sec:edge}

\begin{lemma}[three source modes]\label{lem:edge}
The source-defined edge is $\{r=0\}$ (mass),
$\{r=1,L-1\}$ (fold fundamental), and $\{r=L/2\}$ (cap /
Nyquist).
After Definition~\ref{def:d}, $C_0=C_1=0$ at machine zero
(Fej\'er vanishes at $\theta=0$ and the even extension kills
the first cosine of a mean-zero density).
The cap $m=L/2$ lies outside the CD band $m<N_w-3$
($N_w=L/4+O(1)$).
Consequently $E_k^{\mathrm{bulk}}=E_k$ identically: the three
modes are not majorized, they are absent from the transfer
norm.
\end{lemma}

This is the reviewer instruction (low frequencies into the
edge model, not a triangle bound) executed source-purely:
no eigenvalue peeking, no margin.

\section{Census: the energy does not fall}\label{sec:census}

Write $E_k=\Phi_{N_w-3}(d)$ for the CD energy of centered
$d$, and $\mathrm{qm}=\tfrac12\sum_j d_j^2$.

\begin{prop}[census]\label{prop:census}
On the sealed constructors:
\begin{enumerate}
\item FRAME-A $w=9$: $E=1.80686$,
$\mathrm{mean}\,C_m^2/\mathrm{qm}=0.619$, $C_{\max}=0.583$ at
$m=129$.
\item $kz=18$: $E=1.903$; $kz=52$: $E=5.698$.
\item Core-$42$: $E\in[1.178,5.983]$,
$\mathrm{d}\log E/\mathrm{d}\log N_w=+0.542$ (grows),
$E/N_w$ slope $-0.458$,
$\mathrm{mean}\,C_m^2/\mathrm{qm}\in[0.427,0.848]$.
\item Selected sequence $a_k=2^k$ of~\cite{r397}
($m_k=k\cdot 2^{\lfloor\sqrt k\rfloor}-1$, $n\le a_k^2$,
table cap $k\le 9$):
$E^{\mathrm{bulk}}=0.033,0.191,0.384,0.402,0.508,0.536,0.869$
for $k=3,\ldots,9$.  Not monotone to $0$.  ($k=2$ is all-edge.)
\end{enumerate}
The bulk sits at quadratic-mean, the same Parseval class as
the r389 $\nu$-angles.
It does not tend to $0$ in the CD-transfer norm.
\end{prop}

\section{Bound inventory and honesty gate}\label{sec:gate}

The frequencies $t_{k,r}=2\pi r/(L\Delta)$ are an arithmetic
progression of spacing $\delta=2\pi/(L\Delta)$: a large-sieve
configuration, not a single-$t$ problem.
Prime powers $n=p^\ell$, $\ell\ge 2$, contribute a lag-$L^2$
ratio $0.23$ on FRAME-A; the series
$\sum_{\ell\ge 2}p^{-\ell/2}\log p$ converges absolutely
(elementary).

On FRAME-A the crude majorant
$(1+X/T)\,(\sum\Lambda(n)^2/n)\cdot k$ exceeds $E$ by
$3\cdot10^3$; at $N_w=878$ the ratio is $4\cdot10^5$
($X/T=53$).
The unconditional large-sieve exponent lives at or above
$(1+X/T)$ times Parseval, which grows.
The data sit \emph{at} Parseval.
Decay of $E^{\mathrm{bulk}}$ would require a sub-quadratic-mean
bound on $S_k(t_{k,r})$ along the lag lattice --- quality of
$\zeta'/\zeta$ on the critical line, or equivalent --- which
as an input to $R^\dagger\succ\tfrac12 I$ is circular
(Weil positivity along the selected windows is the target).

\begin{prop}[honesty gate]\label{prop:gate}
The needed large-sieve exponent is not in the unconditional
range.
A bound that closed $E^{\mathrm{bulk}}\to0$ would be RH-near,
hence circular as a route to the dual-resolvent contraction.
Independently, the finite census of
Proposition~\ref{prop:census} refutes the decay in the
named norm.
\end{prop}

Vaughan's identity and a Heath--Brown decomposition of
$\Lambda$ were not needed: already the mean-value theorem at
classical quality fails to close, and the census is at the
random scale those theorems predict, not below it.

\section{Kills}\label{sec:kills}

\begin{prop}[controls]\label{prop:kills}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Scramble (seed $1$, log-positions uniform in
$(0,2\alpha)$, weights kept): $E^{\mathrm{bulk}}=3.39>1.2$
times MAIN $1.81$.
The centered difference \emph{does} separate scramble,
unlike the r389 $\nu$-angle quadratic-mean (which scramble
held).
The cancellation that MAIN spends lives in the $\mu$/lag
structure, not in the $\nu$-angles.
\item Two-period on the cosine grid, $S=21$, $c=2/3$:
$\mathrm{HHI}=0.629$, comb at $m=S$.
The concentrating adversary is a comb, as in r389;
$\rho_{\mathrm{AP}}=1$ remains spectral concentration on a
progression.
\item Mutant $\omega\equiv 1$ (drop $2/\pi$ and the CD cutoff):
$\sum C_m^2=2.85$ against $\Phi=1.81$ --- the weights are a
scale, not a decay mechanism.
Dropping the already-vanishing modes $m=0,1$ does not change
$\Phi$: the ``energy must not fall without the split'' mutant
is vacuous at this norm and is recorded as such.
\item Dead $\chi_3$-$15$: $E=1.69$; living $\chi_3$-$9$:
$E=1.47$; MAIN: $1.81$.
Two-sided: both $O(1)$.  The test is not a world separator
(the same honesty as r398).
\end{enumerate}
\end{prop}

\section{What remains}\label{sec:rest}

The representation and the transfer norm are available as
finite theorems for r400 (bulk one-defect / frame inequality).
They do not supply a vanishing bulk energy.
The macroscopic $1/2$-cluster of $R_{N-3}$ (r398: $36$
eigenvalues in $[\tfrac12,\tfrac35]$ on FRAME-A) is consistent
with a quadratic-mean $d$ whose CD energy grows: a
Hilbert--Schmidt smallness that would collapse the cluster is
exactly what $E^{\mathrm{bulk}}\to0$ would have been, and is
not observed.
The edge model must absorb more than three modes, or the
frame inequality of $5.4$ must treat the $1/2$-cluster
collectively, without asking the Weyl energy to vanish.
