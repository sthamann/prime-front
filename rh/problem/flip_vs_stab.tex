\section{Method}\label{sec:method}

\begin{prop}[$n_{\mathrm{stab}}$ by Fenster-Vergleich]\label{prop:method}
Let $m_n(W)$ be the $n$-th Chebyshev moment of the
folded $\tilde\mu$ of window $W$.
Skip $m_0,m_1$ (window scale / dipole).
Then
\[
n_{\mathrm{stab}}(W_k)
=\min\bigl\{n\ge 2:
\ \mathrm{rel}(m_n(W_k),m_n(W_{k'}))>\varepsilon
\text{ for a neighbour }k'\bigr\}.
\]
On the living pair $k_z17/18$ the first break is
$n=18$, identical for
$\varepsilon\in\{10^{-5},\ldots,10^{-2}\}$
--- flat grades agree (comb-stab anchor);
the cut is mesh-limited, not an $\varepsilon$-artefact.
Deep windows share the same $\varepsilon$-robustness
(the cut does not move from $10^{-6}$ to $10^{-2}$).
\end{prop}

This is the operational form of
``the $n$-th read already equals the limit'':
once neighbouring windows agree, the grade is
window-independent and is a Weil value
(comb channel: proved
\texttt{comb\_elementwise\_stabilization}).
