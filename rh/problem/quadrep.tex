\section{Exact finite obstruction}

The channel asked for is stronger than a degree bound: it asks that
every mesh-compatible native read lie in the quadratic image of the
finite matrix $A_{\mathrm{cap}}$.  Even after the honest onset guard
$\mathrm{elementAnchor}\,f\le a_k$, this fails at $k=5$.

Take $\mathrm{meshExp}=3$, $\mathrm{steps}=24$.  Then
$\mathrm{support}=3$, so
$\mathrm{elementAnchor}=\lceil e^{3}\rceil=21\le a_5=32$, and
$\mathrm{meshExp}\le m_5=19$.  The Hessian $T$ of
$\mathrm{fullRead}(a_5,m_5,\,\cdot\,)$ on $\mathbb R^{24}$ satisfies
\[
 \mathrm{rank}(T)=24>\mathrm{cap}+1=11,\qquad
 \lambda_{\min}(T)=-0.042885425277,\qquad
 \#\{\lambda<0\}=2.
\]
The selected bordered Hankel $A_{\mathrm{cap}}$ at the same window is
positive definite ($\lambda_{\min}(A_{\mathrm{cap}})=6.61842\cdot10^{-8}$,
$q^{\dagger}=0.877898027321<1$).  A negative native read cannot equal
$z^{\mathsf T}A_{\mathrm{cap}}z$ for any $z$.

The named obstruction (not a \code{sorry}) is
\begin{quote}
\code{SelectedOnsetCompatibleNegativeRead}:\\
$\exists\,k>0$, $\exists\,f$ with
$\mathrm{meshExp}\,f\le m_k$,
$\mathrm{elementAnchor}\,f\le a_k$, and
$\mathrm{fullRead}(a_k,m_k,f)<0$.
\end{quote}
The finite implication
\begin{quote}
\code{quadraticRepresentation\_refuted\_of\_negative\_read}
\end{quote}
is proved: a negative mesh-compatible read on a PSD cap refutes
\code{SelectedReadQuadraticRepresentation}.  The original proposition
is retained, unweakened, and unasserted.
