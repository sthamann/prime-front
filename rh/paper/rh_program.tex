\subsection{The window object}

Galerkin compression of the localized Weil operator onto a window of the
von-Mangoldt double comb (prime side $\mu$, smooth/archimedean side $\nu$),
together with three control worlds (\textsc{scramble}: randomized weights;
\textsc{epstein}: an Epstein zeta comb, off-line zeros; \textsc{smooth}: a
smooth density).  ``The wall'' of window $w$ is the statement that this
compression stays positive (quasi-definite) through the whole window ---
a finite, machine-decidable statement per window.  The measured ladder
spans $42$ rungs with terminal degrees $N=142$ to $878$.
